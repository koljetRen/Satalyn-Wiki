## Cultures

### Xi

- **Based on:** Sparta/Ancient Japan
- **Skills:** Martial training and strategy, discipline, order, and dueling
- **Physical looks:** Dark hair, usually cut short or in a tight bun for men. Women wear in braids with intricate pins. Tan, sun-kissed skin. Men are very lean and muscular
- **Clothing:** Men wartime - pauldron spanning both shoulders and the upper chest, with a spartan style helmet. Thick leather is used to protect arms and legs, but chest and stomach are exposed. formal - Japanese style hitatare with a flowing cape over the right shoulder, casual - light tunics without sleeves and the same cape as formal wear. Women formal - a thinner robe in the kimono style, matching colors with husband's hitatare. Casual - sleeveless tunics top flowing into a lose skirt
- **Gender norms:** Men train for war at the age of 8 and strive to bring honor to their family through military might. Women care for the home and are more religiously engaged as they are taught to be constantly praying for the God's to bless thier husbands with strength when they are gone
- **Architecture:** Very compact villages with ancient Japanese style homes with walls built out of stone bricks lining the villages. Each family home also has a shrine for their ancestors. Villages are usually built as harbors or on the side of a mountain
- **Government:** A council of 17 elders leads the people. When one of these 17 die, the elders elect a new elder from the men on the island
- **Religion:** Ancestors watch over the people. Men gain honor for their families through acts of battle (duels, war, etc) As men achieve honor and woman practice proper prayer etiqute and worship, families may stand united to bless their posterity. The more honor men gained and faith the woman showed is believed to bless their descendants and place them where they are now
- **Entertainment:** Children play a game called assassin. Children split into 2 teams, privatly chose a chief, and then need to eliminate the other teams chief by determining who the chief is and eliminating him. The game is played in a large area with many hiding places. Adults participate in watching men duel for sport when not at war
- **Other:** When children turn 3 years old, they start doing team building exercises. Men do trainings with men and women do trainings with other women, and there are other trainings that the genders mix. From this early age, the elders are watching, ready to pair 2 Xi youth up for marriage when they are 15
- **Naming system:** Typically 2 or 3 syllables in names. Common men names end in vowels. noble men names end in harsh consonants.

### Osisu

- **Based on:** Maori/Mayan
- **Skills:** Ocean navigation, tattooing and body art, fishing and canoe carving, music
- **Physical looks:** Deep brown skin with long black hair for both men and women. Women usually wear their hair in braids. Men can't grow facial hair
- **Clothing:** Men battle - barkcloth wraps for chest and legs. daily - shirtless with knee length skirts made out of barkcloth. Women daily - skirts made out of palm leaves or reeds, bandeau tops made out of barkcloth, jewelry (typically necklaces and ankle bracelets) made out of seashells
- **Gender norms:** Men are the providers and protectors. They go fishing, train for war, build up the island, and tend to the fields. The women are the religious leaders. Women are recognized as sacred and each is treated as royalty. At the home, they raise the children and make meals for their families
- **Architecture:** Large wooden docks hold majority of the marketplaces. Village centers are located along the beach while the buildings are built out of stone with decorative wood on the inside stretching into the jungled islands. Large, mayan-style pyramids are found on each island, which house the shaman and chief. Roofs are only found over bedchambers and places to preserve food as the people welcome the rain. Rock is volcanic rock
- **Government:** Each island is led by a chief. These chiefs are picked by the nations queen shaman, who represents Osisu's goddess. The chiefs who are selected are believed to be chosen by the goddess to lead the island. Each of the chiefs meet together to lead Osisu as a whole. The queen shaman is chosen from the collection of all the shamans of the islands, who meet together and with the help of their goddess determine the new queen shaman
- **Religion:** Worship an ocean goddess. Men get tattoos to show their calling in life and their achievements
- **Entertainment:** Children play games on the beaches. Islands participate in music festivals, including making instruments out of everyday objects and making music out of them. The people are also very fond of strong drinks and can usually out drink anyone on the mainland
- **Other:** Each island is lead by a chief and a shaman pair. Both are determined by the queen shaman. When a chief dies, a new one is appointed from the single men and he marries the shaman of the island. When a shaman dies, her apprentice is appointed to shaman and she marries the current chief. Each island shaman is allowed to appoint one of the single young woman of the island to train as her apprentice and the future shaman. Last names are chosen when the Osisuan's choose their choice of expertise. They are based off of something in that region, for example, a fisherman may be given the last name of Stormshark after one of the sharks in the region
- **Other notes:** Fishermen, called Seacasters, will dry fish guts on the deck while out fishing and eat it as their lunch. Islands are chain of volcanos, mix of active and dormant. Queen shaman lives on most active volcanic island

### Dyken

- **Based on:** Athens/Hindu
- **Skills:** Philosophy, architecture, trade
- **Physical looks:** Golden-brown skin with hair ranging from dark black to auburn
- **Clothing:** Men daily - draped chitons with sashes, with different colors of chitons representing different roles in society and different colors of sashes representing levels of knowledge. Women daily - long flowing chitons with silk scarves. Guards - Bronze breastplate, shin and forearm guards. Capes with various colors representing what region of the land they serve. Priests - full covering togas made of white, representing the color of ultimate knowledge
- **Gender norms:** Men are typically traders, scholars, philsophers, or the guards. They are expected to dedicate their life to learning and are expected to participate in public events. Women often manage small family shops and are viewed as the "guardians of culture and tradition" as they teach the culture to their children, but aren't viewed as public figures
- **Architecture:** Ancient Athens style marble buildings. Temples are the largest buildings topped with domes lined in gold. Homes are built in rectangles. Large amphitheatres are used as a meeting spot for the judges and lectures from the priests. Amphitheatres, temples, and other gathering places with many places to sit are at the center of the city, while homes spiral outward
- **Government:** Each city is ruled as a City-state, which is ruled by a council of judges. When a meeting is held, no man from the city is denied entrance and vocal input. The coucil of judges take the input from the townsmembers and the judges to make the decisions. Every 5 years, each city-state elects one representative to run for presidency. Behind closed doors, all judges from all city-states meet together, listen to the representatives, and elect one to be the president of Dyken
- **Religion:** Temples are located in each city, which also serve as schools for children and adults alike. The priests and acolytes looking over the temples serve as worship leaders and teachers, while also practicing philosophy during their time. The people believe the highest calling is that of a scholar and being granted the role of priest at the temple is the highest fulfilment one can achieve
- **Entertainment:** People in the city often engage in study activities, such as knowledge sharing, philosophical debates, or study groups. They can also be seen playing various board games
- **Other:** Colors of Chitons: Tan - farmer. Dark green - merchants. Purple - Judge. Light grey - scholar at a univeristy. White - Priest

### Aramore

- **Based on:** Medieval Europe/Scythian
- **Skills:** Horse breeding and riding, metalwork, storytelling, trade, dueling
- **Physical looks:** Light to fair skin, with hair ranging from blond to dark brown
- **Clothing:** Men daily - tunics worn with belts over simple trousers. In winter they wear fur-lined cloaks. Women daily - long dresses with long sleeves. Guards - armored kilt, breastplate, greaves, and vambraces. Nobility - show nobility and level of nobility through colors of tunics and fancy dress
- **Gender norms:** Men are expected to be the head of the household, managing the land, trade, and/or livestock. The only exception is when the men are at war then the women take care of those. Most men receive military training, but aren't expected to serve in the army. Women take care of the homes when their husbands are away, but also are instrumental in helping with trade negotiations
- **Architecture:** Tudor style homes and buildings. Each city has a keep made out of stone with noble homes located in the most inner wall with the keep. Depending on the size of the city, rings of large stone walls are located, with the further into the city being the richer residents, typically lower class nobles and merchants, while rich farmers live inside the outermost wall (shorter than the rest) and other farmers live outside the wall
- **Government:** Kingdom ruled by a king, successor is kings oldest son. Each city is ruled by a court of nobles, with various amounts in the court depending on the size of the town. If king dies and there is no successor, one of the nobles becomes king based on popular vote and points based on each city
- **Religion:** Ancient religion that no one seems to follow anymore. Spirits at found in the elements and each posess different powers to control the world. The leader of these spirits is called the Skyfather, who is the spirit dominating the sky. People used to have traditions and festivals when the auroras would be seen, which they thought was the Skyfather blessing them through dance, but the auroras haven't been seen since the desolation of the ground water from the evil wizard
- **Entertainment:** Sport dueling and horse races are the biggest sporting events in Aramore and are a depiction of a warriors strength and a horses speed. A good warrior captian (usually the ones dueling) depends on a strong and fast horse and they use the horse races to try and buy the best horse for them. Townspeople are found gathering in the taverns to listen to bards stories and songs

### Aegryamis

- **Based on:** Egypt/Byzantine
- **Skills:** Irrigation, architecture, luxury goods
- **Physical looks:** Deep bronze skin with dark eyes and black hair
- **Clothing:** Men daily - light kilts and tunics to protect skins and avoid the heat. Also wear closed-toed sandals. Women daily - light linen dresses with gold jewelry. Guards - scale armor with colorful capes representing the noble family they serve. Wear a bandana like clothing protecting face connected to helmets. Priests - silk robes with a gold edging
- **Gender norms:** Men are expected to chose a path between military, scholarly, or bureaucratic careers. Women do what they can to build up the men in their families, whether that be through helping them with in scholarly activities, building up their trade, or even being married into high ranking families through arranged marriages
- **Architecture:** Skyscraper like buildings made from stone and Akyethis tree to preserve as much fertile land as they can. The rich families can afford their own estate and don't live in skyscrapers while the lower and middle class resort to the skyscrapers, which have stairs leading up the outside of the building. The only buildings that are built on the river are the two royal families palaces. Domes are prominant in the cities, signifying places of importance such as palaces, temples, and education buildings. The cities are built on a grid pattern, with the marketplaces located closer to the palaces
- **Government:** 2 royal families, one for each town on opposite sides of the river. The ruling family switches between them with every king. When one king dies, the other family becomes the ruling family, with them electing a king out of the elegible men, which include every male in the direct line who is married. The men in these families are married through arranged marrages when they are 16, the women in the families are married at 14
- **Religion:** 2 brother gods were given the desert of Aegryamis as punishment from other gods. With only 1 fertile river, the 2 brothers went from bitter enemies to allies. To determine who leads the people, they decided on the dual monarchy. Each ruling family has a trusted advisor, who serves as the priest and mouthpiece of one of the brothers
- **Entertainment:** Concerts played by talented musicans. People are also found playing various board games

### Skarg

- **Based on:** Norse/Celtic
- **Skills:** Shipbuilding, woodworking, and storytelling
- **Physical looks:** Light to fair skin, with hair ranging from fiery red to dark brown
- **Clothing:** Men daily - tunics worn with belts over simple trousers. In winter they don fur mantles. Women daily - long dresses, with fur cloak in winter. Battle - chainmail with round shields, barbute helms. Ceremonial - animal pelts
- **Gender norms:** Genders have shared roles in the home and village, but men are expected to do the heavy lifting through fishing, lumber work, and warriors
- **Architecture:** Norse style longhouses built in a circular pattern around the city center, which stands a large totempole depicting one of the elemental spirits as interpreted by humans. Timbers line the cities in a wall of wood
- **Government:** Each town is ruled by a council of elders, made up of the 11 oldest and capable members of the town. When a nationwide decision is to be made, each of these town councils elects 2 representatives to go to the capital on the towns behalf
- **Religion:** Same as Aramore, but more people still believe it, especially those up north in Thrymfell or Northwatch
- **Entertainment:** Families gather together around firepits to tell stories. Villages gather together in the town square to listen to bards tell stories in the firelight
