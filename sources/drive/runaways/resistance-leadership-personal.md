# The Runaways Resistance Leadership - Personal

## Different levels of command

- Lead command (Leader, Co-leader)
- 1st level (Captains, commanders)
- 2nd level (lieutenants)
- 3rd level (sargents)

## Lead Command (general)

Notable people: Xander, Elliott, Aerlem

Main responsibilities:

- Lead the resistance
- Make decisions regarding resistance
- Plan out resistance's movements

## 1st Level (commander)

Notable people: Sam (dead), Brian (captured), Joseph Barker (captured), Kolt, Scott

Main responsibilities:

- Leads a division (4-5 battalions (320-1250 people))
- Take command in event of no leadership of level above present

## 2nd Level (lieutenant)

Notable people: Suew, Jaek, Eric

Main responsibilities:

- Leads a battalion (4-5 platoons (80-250 people))
- Take command in event of no leadership of level above present

## 3rd Level (sergeant)

Notable people: Nad, Lucas, Crewt

Main responsibilities:

- Leads a platoon (20-50 people)
- Take command in event of no leadership of level above present
