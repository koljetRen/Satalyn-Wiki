# The Runaways Book 2: Stomped in the Dust

After narrowly escaping the armies of King Carter at Misty Phlats, the resistance finds themselves at Keyesport Vale, home of King [Argo?]. But they aren't safe for long. King Carter's armies attack and capture the island and all of the men and women living there. Eric, now one of the leaders in the resistance, must find a way to escape the island and restart the force to dethrone King Carter from his evil ways. Along the way, Eric learns more about who he was before he woke up in the dungeon as well as his potential.
