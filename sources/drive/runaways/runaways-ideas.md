## Revealing the Xi Daggers

The warrior stood back in awe as Eric held his sword in an offensive form. The crimson red color flowing throughout the small black canyons on the blade. It almost seemed like it was pulsing at times.

Eric stepped forward, ready to attack their opposer. The mysterious figure takes off his hood, revealing his face. It had a look of awe on it as he studied the sword. After a couple of moments in silence, the figure spoke.

"You. Come with me,"

He spoke in broken english, Eric was barely able to understand him. The warrior started walking away, but turned after noticing Eric hadn't followed him.

"I will not leave my friends alone. Either we all come with you or none of us."

The warrior shook his head.

"Listen," he said, this time better able to understand him. "You have xiramite that has connected to you. You are Xi, so you must come with me. The rest of you are not Xi, so you cannot come."

Eric looked at Lucas, who slowly pulled his dagger out of its sheath and held it out to the warrior. He took a close look at it and gasped.

"You have xiramite as well, but no connection."

Lucas brought his dagger back closer to him.

"It was a gift from my mother. She said to reveal it only when it was necessary."

## The Gladiator Battle

A plume of dust rose from where Eric's body hit the ground of the arena. His head rested against the patchy grass floor, a line of blood trickling from the side of his mouth. With multiple grunts, Eric got his hands underneath him. Eric sat there on all fours for a moment. Sweat was beading on his forehead and falling to the ground. With another grunt, he got his right leg under his torso, then making sure that he was holding onto his sword and shield, he stood all the way up. Panting from being out of breath, Eric looked at his opponent. The black figure clearly was possessed with some evil power. He had an evil smile.

"You ready to give up?" his opponent said in a slippery voice.

Eric raised his sword in the direction of the black creature.

"You have no power over me, and I will destroy you."

With that sentence, Eric charged the figure. He raised his sword to slash down on his opponent, the xiramite pulsing rapidly as if it were filled with adrenaline. The now overpowered creature managed to defend against the mighty blows of Eric. Slash after slash, Eric kept pushing his opponent back, until in an instance, the blade shattered. Pieces of steel flew everywhere as the black and red sword kept going straight into the shadowy body. He let out a screech, then in an instance disappeared. All that was left of the intense battle was the hilt and the broken blade fragments and Eric's body dripping in sweat.

## Crazy plot twisting idea

No good guys in this war. Augo and Carter were brothers in line for the throne of Fillyolia because their father was the former king's younger brother. Dherlin attacked Fillyolia and killed the king and his entire family, making Augo become the rightful ruler. He was used to an easy life of nobility and didn't want the responsibility, so he declined, passing it to Carter. Carter showed humility and took the throne wanting to help rebuild after the disaster. Carter rebuilt the kingdom, using his own hands to help as well. Dherlin then learned of this and cast a spell on him to possess him whenever he wanted. Carter started to suffer with Bipolar because of this. When Dherlin's possession took over, he was power hungry and rude, but when it was just Carter, he was his normal, humble self. Augo, seeing the power that Carter was gaining, became greedy and wanted it, so he started rumors of a resistance against him. Xander, who was a nobody dreaming of power, came to Augo and offered to lead the resistance and make it a reality and in return, wanted to become his second hand man with intents to kill Augo when he obtained the throne for his own power. Xander and Augo then spread the story that Carter had stolen the throne from Augo and they were just wanting to put the rightful ruler on the throne. Now that Carter's life was on the line, he started to fight back in defense, but when Dherlin's possession kicked in, Carter attacked because he wanted to stay in his seat of power

## Jane's background

Her family joined the resistance because of her mother and brother's death. They lived in a village that was trying to build up its own kingdom after the fall of Fillyolia. King Carter had his forces going to try and reunite all the villages to make it strong against outside forces. Village declined the offer from King Carter's leader of the force that went to that village, and he went and attacked and ransacked it. So Jane and her family thought that King Carter had ordered the ransacking of their village when it was in reality, the leader who was in charge of that small force

## Eric/Jane Relationship

Starts out with being written like they are siblings. They are the best of friends and neither of them think of taking it a bit further. When Eric gets captured at the end of Book 2, Jane realizes how much Eric means to her and she understands that she has deeper feelings for him. After they reunite, Jane doesn't want to be separated again and unknowingly, helps Eric because of that. Eric, who is now suffering from depression and more ptsd from his flashbacks, realizes how much Jane means to him and how much she is helping him to beat those demons he's having. As his depression is leaving, he has a flashback of killing his mother and wants nothing to do with anyone. Jane finds him overlooking the ocean and comforts him. Eric tells her all that is happening and she gives him a hug and a kiss. Thus begins their romantic relationship.

After the battle of Carterville but before they leave for Averill, they wed and become husband and wife. Sometime during their adventure, Jane gets pregnant but doesn't know about it until one of their experiences with Dherlin, who mentions it and tries to curse the child. Scott then sacrifices himself, getting hit with the curse instead of Jane. The curse was to die quickly and despite their best efforts to cure him, he dies.

As Jane's belly gets bigger, she eventually can't join Eric on the final quest to defeat Dherlin, so at Eric's request and the council of others, she stays at Lucal while the rest go and attack Dherlin. Eric reunites with Jane after defeating the evil wizard and they start returning home but, as they start to leave Lucal, Jane goes into delivery. They name their newborn boy Scott after her brother who gave his life for the baby. After living in Lucal for a few years, they travel back to Carterville to live out the rest of their lives
