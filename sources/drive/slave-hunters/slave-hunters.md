# Slave Hunters (earlier opening draft)

Peter Snyder was enjoying life. He was at the sweet age of 15, the last year before adult responsibilities hit him. He had become a great swordsman and his father wasn't worried about him in any fights. Peter didn't have to worry about any fights either. Peter was the son of King William Snyder VII, king of the nation of Snyder. Peter was the eldest son of King William, putting him to be the heir to the throne. People wouldn't dare get into fights with him because he was in the royal family. Peter's father had agreed to counsel him on how to run the kingdom when Peter turned 16, allowing for Peter to be ready to take the throne at the age of 18.

However, 51 days before Peter's 16th birthday, tragedy struck. Both of his parents had died, although no one knew how, only that they died together. Suddenly, Peter was taken out of the last 50 days of his childhood and thrown into a room that he recognized as his father's council room. Seated around the u shaped table sat 22 people, ranging from looking around their early 20s to older than his grandfather, had he still been alive.

Peter was ushered to the largest seat in the room at the head of the table. There was an empty seat on each side of the chair at the end of the bottom of the u shape. On the outside of the sides of the u were 12 seats on each side. One chair on each side of the table was empty.
