# Slave Hunters Stuff

## Basic Idea

1. Kid becomes King at 15 due to parents death
2. Someone on council plots against him
3. King Kid gets kidnapped in the night
4. Council member takes control of kingdom
5. King kid gets sold to slavers going across the Great Desert
6. Halfway through the desert, king kid escapes slavers with 3 others
7. 1 escapee dies shortly outside of the camp
8. Another escapee dies of injuries a few hours later
9. On the second day, king kid and other escapee meet the slave hunter
10. Slave hunter is on a mission to find and free all slaves
11. King kid and other escapee join with slave hunter on mission
12. After 2 or 3 years of slave hunting, they meet the group from runaways
13. All slave hunters join with the runaways on their mission

## Plot

1 book

- 1st chapter - preview to escape from caravan
- 2nd chapter - jump back to months before parent's death
- Last chapter - find Eric and co
