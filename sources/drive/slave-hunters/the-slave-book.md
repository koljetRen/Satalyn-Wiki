# The Slave Book, From the Runaways Series

## Plot line

- Peter is in a slave caravan
- Him and three other slaves manage to escape
- One of the slaves dies from an arrow shot at him
- Another of the slaves dies a few hours later
- Peter and other guy spend time in the desert and Peter starts to think

### Flashback

- Peter was a prince, heir to the throne of Forin
- His father, mother, and older brother went on a trip to visit something and an accident happened and they die
- Peter ends up being placed in the ruling seat at 15 and has a council to help him

### End Flashback

- Slave hunters find Peter and other guy
- Dispite their efforts other slave dies
- Peter joins the slave hunters because he doesn't want others to suffer the same way he did
- Stuff with slave hunters

### Flashback pt2

- After a little bit of being leader, the head helper wants the power
- Head helper kidnaps Peter in his sleep and hides him away
- Head helper makes people think he ran away, and because there are no more heirs, he takes the king's spot
- Peter then wakes up and gets beaten, then given to some slavers, then back to start of the book

### End of flashback pt2

- Peter starts noticing some weird things happening around him
- Slave hunters decided it would be best for the next time they are on east side of the great desert to talk to a wizard at Aegyramis
- They continue on doing slave hunter things
- They met this dude named Eric (no idea who that could be) and he says (I'm a wizard Peter) and they join together on epic quest

## Draft opening

Peter Snyder was enjoying life. He was at the sweet age of 15, the last year before adult responsibilities hit him. He had become a great swordsman and his father wasn't worried about him in any fights. Peter didn't have to worry about any fights either. Peter was the son of King William Snyder VII, king of the nation of Snyder. Peter was the second son of King William, putting him to be the 2nd heir to the throne. People wouldn't dare get into fights with him because he was in the royal family. Peter's father had agreed to counsel him on how to run the kingdom when Peter turned 16, allowing for Peter to be ready to take the throne at the age of 18 if need be.

However, 51 days before Peter's 16th birthday, tragedy struck. Both of his parents and his elder brother William VIII had died, although no one knew how, only that they died together. Suddenly, Peter was taken out of the last 50 days of his childhood and thrown into a room that he recognized as his father's council room. Seated around the u shaped table sat 22 people, ranging from looking around their early 20s to older than his grandfather, had he still been alive.

Peter was ushered to the largest seat in the room at the head of the table. There was an empty seat on each side of the chair at the end of the bottom of the u shape. On the outside of the sides of the u were 12 seats on each side. One chair on each side of the table was empty.
