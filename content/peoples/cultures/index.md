The human cultures of Satalyn: [[Xi]], [[Osisu]], [[Dyken]], [[Aramore]], [[Aegryamis]] and [[Skarg]].

Each culture has its own page here; the region it lives in has a page of the same name under [[places/index|Places]].

| Culture | Homeland | Real-world inspiration | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[xi-people\|Xi people]] | The island of Xi | Sparta / Ancient Japan | Martial training, discipline, dueling |
| [[osisu-people\|Osisu people]] | A chain of volcanic islands | Maori / Mayan | Ocean navigation, tattooing, fishing, music |
| [[dyken-people\|Dyken people]] | Dry desert | Athens / Hindu | Philosophy, architecture, trade |
| [[aramore-people\|Aramore people]] | Dry prairie | Medieval Europe / Scythian | Horses, metalwork, storytelling, dueling |
| [[aegryamis-people\|Aegryamis people]] | Desert with one fertile river | Egypt / Byzantine | Irrigation, architecture, luxury goods |
| [[skarg-people\|Skarg people]] | Rainy coastal forest | Norse / Celtic | Shipbuilding, woodworking, storytelling |

Only the Osisu and Aramoreans are explicitly called [[Humans|human]] in the notes; the rest are presumed human.
