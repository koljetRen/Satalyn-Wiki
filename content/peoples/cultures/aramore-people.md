The **Aramoreans** are a [[Humans|human]] people of [[Aramore]], a dry prairie kingdom, known for horse breeding and riding, metalwork, storytelling, trade and dueling. Aramore is the homeland of [[Eric]], the protagonist of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Appearance

Aramoreans have light to fair skin, with hair ranging from blond to dark brown.

## Skills and livelihood

Aramoreans breed and ride horses, work metal, tell stories, trade and duel. Their crops include [[Colored Corn]].

## Clothing

| Who | Clothing |
| --- | --- |

| Nobility | Show their nobility, and its level, through the colors of their tunics and fancy dress. |

## Society and gender roles

Men are expected to head the household, managing the land, trade and livestock. The only exception is wartime, when the women take these over while the men are away. Most men receive military training but aren't expected to serve in the army. Women keep the home while their husbands are away and are also instrumental in trade negotiations.

## Architecture and settlements

Aramorean homes and buildings are Tudor-style. Every city has a stone keep, with the nobles' homes inside the innermost wall around it. Depending on a city's size, it has several rings of large stone walls. The further in a resident lives, the richer they are:

| Ring | Who lives there |
| --- | --- |
| Innermost wall, with the keep | Nobles |
| Inner rings | Richer residents, typically lower-class nobles and merchants |
| Inside the outermost wall (shorter than the others) | Rich farmers |
| Outside the walls | Other farmers |

## Government

Aramore is a kingdom ruled by a king, whose successor is his oldest son. Each city is ruled by a court of nobles, whose size depends on the size of the town. If a king dies with no successor, one of the nobles becomes king by popular vote, with points awarded by each city.

In *The Runaways* the king is [[King Carter]], whose seat is [[Carthollow]].

> [!info] Canon note
> King Carter's city is **Carthollow** in the 2026 sheet and **Carterville** in older notes (the Runaways ideas and the first draft).

## Religion

Aramore has an ancient religion that hardly anyone seems to follow any more. It holds that spirits are found in the elements and that each has different powers to control the world. The leader of the spirits is the **Skyfather**, the spirit who dominates the sky. See [[Skyfather and the elemental spirits]].

People used to hold traditions and festivals whenever the auroras were seen, which they took to be the Skyfather blessing them through dance. But the auroras haven't been seen since the [[Desolation of the Ground Water]] by the evil wizard.

## Entertainment and customs

- **Sport dueling and horse races** are Aramore's biggest sporting events, showing off a warrior's strength and a horse's speed. A good warrior captain (usually the ones dueling) depends on a strong, fast horse, and captains use the horse races to try to buy the best horse.
- **Taverns:** townspeople gather in taverns to listen to bards' stories and songs.
