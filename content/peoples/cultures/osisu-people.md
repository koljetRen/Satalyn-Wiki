The **Osisu** (Osisuans) are a [[Humans|human]] island people of [[Osisu]], a chain of volcanic islands, known for ocean navigation, tattooing and body art, fishing, canoe carving and music.

## Appearance

Both men and women have deep brown skin and long black hair. Women usually wear their hair in braids. Osisuan men can't grow facial hair.

## Skills and livelihood

The Osisu are ocean navigators, tattooists, fishermen, canoe carvers and musicians. Their fishermen are called **Seacasters**; out at sea they dry fish guts on the deck and eat them for lunch.

## Clothing

| Women | Daily | Skirts of palm leaves or reeds, barkcloth bandeau tops, and seashell jewelry (typically necklaces and ankle bracelets). |

## Society and gender roles

Men are the providers and protectors: they fish, train for war, build up the island and tend the fields. Women are the religious leaders. Every woman is recognized as sacred and treated as royalty. At home, women raise the children and cook for their families.

## Architecture and settlements

Large wooden docks hold most of the marketplaces. Village centers lie along the beach, and buildings of volcanic stone, decorated with wood inside, stretch back into the jungled islands. Each island has a large Mayan-style pyramid, which houses the shaman and the chief. Roofs are found only over bedchambers and food stores, because the people welcome the rain.

The islands are a chain of volcanoes, some active and some dormant. The queen shaman lives on the most active volcanic island.

## Government

Each island is led by a chief and shaman pair:

- **Chiefs** are picked by the nation's **queen shaman**, who represents Osisu's goddess, so the chosen chiefs are believed to be chosen by the goddess. When a chief dies, a new one is appointed from the island's single men, and he marries the island's shaman.
- **Shamans:** each island shaman may choose one of the island's single young women as her apprentice and future shaman. When a shaman dies, her apprentice becomes shaman and marries the current chief.
- **The queen shaman** is chosen by all the islands' shamans, who meet together and, with the help of their goddess, decide on the new queen shaman.

All the chiefs meet together to lead Osisu as a whole.

## Religion

The Osisu worship an ocean goddess (see [[Osisu ocean goddess]]). Men get tattoos to show their calling in life and their achievements.

## Entertainment and customs

- Children play games on the beaches.
- The islands hold music festivals, which include making instruments out of everyday objects and playing music on them.
- The Osisu are very fond of strong drink and can usually out-drink anyone on the mainland.

## Names

An Osisuan chooses a last name when they choose their expertise. It is based on something in their region: a fisherman, for example, may take the name **Stormshark**, after one of the sharks of the region (see [[Stormshark]]).
