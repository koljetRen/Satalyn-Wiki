The **Xi** are a warrior people of the island of [[Xi]], known for their martial training, discipline, order and dueling. Long ago they ruled an empire on the mainland, the [[Xi Empire]] (350–430).

## Appearance

The Xi have dark hair and tan, sun-kissed skin. Men usually wear their hair cut short or in a tight bun, and are very lean and muscular. Women wear their hair in braids with intricate pins.

## Skills and livelihood

The Xi are trained in martial skill and strategy, discipline, order and dueling. The Xi shield wall is famous: in a draft battle scene it is called "the infamous Xi shield wall, which was impenetrable".

## Society and gender roles

Men begin training for war at the age of 8 and strive to bring honor to their family through military might. Women care for the home and are the more religiously engaged sex: they are taught to pray constantly for the gods to bless their husbands with strength while the men are away.

Training starts even earlier. From the age of 3, children do team-building exercises: men train with men, women with women, and some trainings mix the sexes. From this early age the elders are watching, ready to pair two Xi youths for marriage when they turn 15.

### Xi blood

Xi blood doesn't mix with blood foreign to it. When it does, there is only a very low chance that a child survives past birth. During the [[Xi Empire]] the Xi had a rule against mating with anyone foreign to Xi. Only those with Xi blood can wield [[xiramite]].

## Architecture and settlements

Xi villages are very compact, with homes in an ancient Japanese style and walls of stone brick lining the village. Every family home has a shrine to its ancestors. Villages are usually built as harbors or on the side of a mountain.

## Government

A council of 17 elders leads the people (see [[Xi Council of Elders]]). When one of the 17 dies, the elders elect a new elder from among the men on the island.

## Religion

The Xi believe their ancestors watch over them. Men gain honor for their families through acts of battle (duels, war and so on), while women practice proper prayer etiquette and worship. As men win honor and women show faith, a family may stand united to bless its posterity: the more honor the men gained and the more faith the women showed, the more their descendants are blessed, and this is believed to be what placed each family where it is now. See [[Xi ancestor worship]].

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways ideas (a 2024 conversation about Xi culture) say Xi women are "constantly worshiping their God to bless them with strength", a single god. The 2026 Cultures sheet says the women pray for "the God's" (gods) to bless their husbands, and that ancestors watch over the people.

## Entertainment and customs

- **Assassin:** a children's game. The children split into two teams, each privately chooses a chief, and each team tries to work out who the other team's chief is and eliminate him. It is played over a large area with many hiding places.
- **Duels:** when not at war, adults watch men duel for sport.

## Names

Xi names typically have two or three syllables. Common men's names end in vowels; noble men's names end in harsh consonants.

## History

The Xi once lived only on their island. They heard rumors of a land much larger than their own, so their leaders, the council of 17 elderly men, decided to go and live there instead. They attacked the west coast, but after a few decades decided the large kingdom wasn't worth maintaining, and many of the men were growing sad at not seeing their families. The council of elders pulled them out. This venture is presumably the [[Xi Empire]], dated 350–430 in the Runaways timeline. The [[Xi City Ruins]] are among the sites the author pictured for the books.

The older note describes Xi as "a small tropical island that no one has discovered".
