The **people of Aegryamis** live in [[Aegryamis]], a desert land with a single fertile river, and are known for irrigation, architecture and luxury goods. They are ruled by two royal families who take turns on the throne.

> [!info] Canon note
> The 2026 sheet spells the name **Aegryamis**; the older Runaways ideas and the slave book spell it **Aegyramis**.

## Appearance

The people of Aegryamis have deep bronze skin, dark eyes and black hair.

## Skills and livelihood

They are skilled at irrigation, architecture and making luxury goods.

## Clothing

| Who | Clothing |
| --- | --- |
| Men (daily) | Light kilts and tunics, to protect the skin and avoid the heat, with closed-toed sandals. |
| Women (daily) | Light linen dresses with gold jewelry. |
| Guards | Scale armor with colorful capes showing which noble family they serve, and a bandana-like cloth protecting the face, attached to the helmet. |
| Priests | Silk robes with gold edging. |

## Society and gender roles

Men are expected to choose one of three paths: a military, scholarly or bureaucratic career. Women do what they can to build up the men of their families, whether by helping with their scholarly work, building up their trade, or marrying into high-ranking families through arranged marriages.

## Architecture and settlements

To preserve as much fertile land as they can, the people of Aegryamis build tall, skyscraper-like buildings of stone and [[Akyethis Tree|Akyethis wood]]. The lower and middle classes live in these towers, which have stairs running up the outside. Rich families can afford their own estates instead.

The only buildings built on the river are the palaces of the two royal families. Domes are prominent and mark places of importance: palaces, temples and places of education. Cities are laid out on a grid, with the marketplaces closer to the palaces.

## Government

Aegryamis has two royal families, one for each town on opposite sides of the river. Rule passes between them with every king: when one king dies, the other family becomes the ruling family and elects a king from its eligible men, meaning every married male in the direct line. The men of the royal families are married by arrangement at 16, and the women at 14.

## Religion

Two brother gods were given the desert of Aegryamis as a punishment by the other gods. With only one fertile river, the brothers went from bitter enemies to allies. To decide who leads the people, they settled on the dual monarchy. Each ruling family has a trusted advisor who serves as priest and mouthpiece of one of the brothers. See [[Aegryamis brother gods]].

An older note (Runaways ideas) lists the faith of Aegryamis as one of two major religions besides the worldwide pantheon.

## Entertainment and customs

People enjoy concerts by talented musicians and play various board games.
