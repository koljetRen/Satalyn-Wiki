The **Skargans** are a people of [[Skarg]], a rainy coastal forest, known for shipbuilding, woodworking and storytelling.

## Appearance

Skargans have light to fair skin, with hair ranging from fiery red to dark brown.

## Skills and livelihood

Skargans are shipbuilders, woodworkers and storytellers. Their land holds the [[Thrym Pine]] of [[Thrymfell Forest]].

## Clothing

| Who | Clothing |

## Society and gender roles

Men and women share roles in the home and village, but men are expected to do the heavy lifting: fishing, lumber work and fighting as warriors.

## Architecture and settlements

Skargans live in Norse-style longhouses built in a circle around the city center, where a large totem pole stands depicting one of the elemental spirits as humans interpret it. A wall of timbers surrounds each city.

## Government

Each town is ruled by a council of elders made up of the 11 oldest capable members of the town. When a nationwide decision has to be made, each town council elects two representatives to go to the capital on the town's behalf.

## Religion

Skargans share the old religion of [[aramore-people|Aramore]]: spirits found in the elements, led by the Skyfather, the spirit of the sky (see [[Skyfather and the elemental spirits]]). Unlike in Aramore, many people still believe in it, especially in the north, in [[Thrymfell]] and [[Northwatch]].

## Entertainment and customs

Families gather around firepits to tell stories, and whole villages gather in the town square to listen to bards telling stories in the firelight.
