The **Kurupal** are small shapeshifting mountain folk who live under the mountains, one of the five races [[Modingua]] placed on the world in [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase five]].

> [!info] Canon note
> The 2026 world-creation notes spell the name **Kurupal**. The older Runaways cast list (2024) spells it **Krerupal** ("Leader of the Krerupals in resistance"). This page uses the newer spelling.

## Appearance

The Kurupal are small, "a bit like dwarves crossed with tiki figures". They are shapeshifters. The two Kurupal named in *[[The Runaways]]* stand 3'8" and 3'3" tall.

## Homeland and migration

The Kurupal live under the mountains. Like every race they began in one homeland and spread from it. During the [[Ice Age]] they sheltered below ground.

## Society

The Kurupal build their settlements in big underground caverns, digging tunnels outwards from them. They see the mountains as sacred.

## Relations with other races

The Kurupal believe the [[Stoneskins]] should not touch the sacred mountains, which fuels an ancient rivalry between the two races. See [[Kurupal–Stoneskin Rivalry]].

## In the modern day

The Kurupal are an ancient race that is slowly dying out. Some fight for [[The Resistance]] in *The Runaways*:
