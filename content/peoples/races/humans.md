**Humans** are one of the five races [[Modingua]] chose and placed on the world in [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase five]], the phase of life.

## Homeland and migration

Like every race, humans started from one homeland and then migrated, splitting into different cultures as they spread. The [[Ice Age]] drove much of this migration. Where the human homeland lay is not yet recorded.

## Cultures

The world-creation notes give humans "four or five cultures", naming [[osisu-people|Osisu]] and Bardon's city (the [[Bardon]] of *[[The Runaways]]* is [[aramore-people|Aramorean]]). Some human cultures may echo ancient humans.

The six cultures worked out in detail in the author's notes are:

| Culture | Real-world inspiration | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[xi-people\|Xi]] | Sparta / Ancient Japan | martial training, discipline, dueling |
| [[osisu-people\|Osisu]] | Maori / Mayan | ocean navigation, tattooing, fishing, music |
| [[dyken-people\|Dyken]] | Athens / Hindu | philosophy, architecture, trade |
| [[aramore-people\|Aramore]] | Medieval Europe / Scythian | horses, metalwork, storytelling, dueling |
| [[aegryamis-people\|Aegryamis]] | Egypt / Byzantine | irrigation, architecture, luxury goods |
| [[skarg-people\|Skarg]] | Norse / Celtic | shipbuilding, woodworking, storytelling |

> [!note]
> Only Osisu and Aramore are explicitly called human in the notes. The other four are presumably human too, but the notes don't say.

## Relations with other races

Modingua loves every race equally. Tensions between the races come from life in the world, not from divine favouritism.

## Ideas and open threads

- Some human cultures "may echo ancient humans" (world-creation notes).
- The [[Ice Age People]] are the ancestors of later peoples, and northern families with tough skin are one of their traces in the modern day.

## Open questions

- Where was the first human homeland?
- Are the Xi, Dyken, Aegryamis and Skarg all human?
- The notes say "four or five" human cultures but six cultures are described. Is one of the six not human, or does "four or five" just predate the Cultures sheet?
