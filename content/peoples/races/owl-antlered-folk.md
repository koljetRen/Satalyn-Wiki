The **owl-antlered folk** are a rare, nocturnal and secluded race of excellent hunters, one of the five races [[Modingua]] placed on the world in [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase five]]. (The name is a placeholder; the race combines two earlier ideas, owl folk and antlered folk.)

## Appearance

- A humanoid body with an owl's head and eyes, and feathers.
- No wings.
- Deer-like antlers.
- Taloned, owl-like feet, so they don't wear shoes. They walk and run like raptor dinosaurs.

## Society

The owl-antlered folk live in small family clans that hunt by night and rarely mix with outsiders. Like most of the world, they have no single favourite god.

## Relations with other races

Most owl-antlered folk never leave their clans. A rare few travel the world as guides, hunters or omen-readers, and these travellers are how other races know of them.

Other races see them as very close to the gods, because their owl and antler features echo the [[Sacred Creatures]], which have elk antlers and fins that work like an owl's wings.
