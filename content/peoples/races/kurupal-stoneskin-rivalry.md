The **Kurupal–stoneskin rivalry** is an ancient feud between the [[Kurupal]] and the [[Stoneskins]], two mountain races, over who may live on the mountains.

## The two sides

| | Kurupal | Stoneskins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where they live | Under the mountains | On the highest peaks |
| Settlements | Big underground caverns, with tunnels dug outwards | Cities of stone and ice |
| View of the mountains | Sacred; the stoneskins shouldn't touch them | — |
| In the ice age | Sheltered below ground | Thrived and spread across the peaks |

## Cause

The Kurupal see the mountains as sacred and believe the stoneskins shouldn't touch them. During the [[Ice Age]] the stoneskins thrived and spread across the peaks while the Kurupal sheltered below, so to the Kurupal it looks as if the stoneskins used the freeze to seize sacred ground.
