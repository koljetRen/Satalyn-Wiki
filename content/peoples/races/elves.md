**Elves** are one of the five races [[Modingua]] chose and placed on the world in [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase five]].

## Homeland and migration

Like every race, the elves began in one homeland and migrated from it. The [[Ice Age]] hit them hard.

## In the modern day

Because of the ice age, elves are rare and secluded by the modern day.
