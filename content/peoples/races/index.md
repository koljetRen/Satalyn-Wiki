The five races [[Modingua]] chose and placed on the world in phase five, plus the ice age people they descend from.

## The five races

- [[Humans]] — the most widespread race, split into many cultures (see [[peoples/cultures/index|Cultures]]).
- [[Kurupal]] — small shapeshifting folk who live under the mountains; slowly dying out.
- [[Elves]] — hit hard by the ice age; rare and secluded today.
- [[Stoneskins]] — mountain dwellers who thrived in the ice age.
- [[Owl-Antlered Folk]] — rare, nocturnal hunters with owl heads and deer antlers.

## Related

- [[Ice Age People]] — the fallen civilisation of the ice age, ancestors of later peoples.
- [[Kurupal–Stoneskin Rivalry]] — the ancient feud over the mountains.

## Other peoples (ideas only)

The notes hint at other peoples, but none is confirmed:

- **Merfolk** — the world-creation notes mention "possible merfolk" as an example of a water-dwelling people who would favour the sea god ([[Ozefio]]), and say the sea god "may have chosen where the first merfolk lived".
- **Dwarves or goliaths** — the [[Ice Age People]] "could be the origin of dwarves or goliaths".

The notes' list of races ends with an empty sixth entry, so the list may still grow.
