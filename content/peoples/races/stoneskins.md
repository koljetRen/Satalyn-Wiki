**Stoneskins** are mountain dwellers, one of the five races [[Modingua]] placed on the world in [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase five]]. They are not frost giants.

## Homeland and migration

The stoneskins survived the [[Ice Age]] well. During the freeze they thrived and spread across the mountain peaks. They migrated widely and split into different cultures, living everywhere from icy peaks to mountains in tropical regions.

## Society

Stoneskins build cities of stone and ice on the highest peaks.

## Relations with other races

The [[Kurupal]], who live beneath the same mountains, hold the mountains sacred and believe stoneskins should not touch them. Because the stoneskins spread across the peaks while the Kurupal sheltered below during the ice age, the Kurupal see it as the stoneskins using the freeze to seize sacred ground. See [[Kurupal–Stoneskin Rivalry]].
