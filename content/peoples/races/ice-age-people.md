The **ice age people** were the mortals who lived through [[The Ice Age]], a fallen civilisation with naturally tough skin and the ancestors of later peoples, who have since faded into legend.

## Origins

In [[The Seven Phases of Creation|phase six]] ([[The Golden Age]]) the gifts of [[The Nine]] let mortals skip the hunter-gatherer stage, so they began farming and building cities straight away. Then phase seven's great freeze arrived. Fields froze over and the first cities were abandoned, so the ice age people were a fallen civilisation, not cavemen.

## Appearance

They had naturally tougher skin, and possibly stockier builds, to suit the cold.

## Life in the ice age

Glaciers limited where they could live. Lower sea levels may have opened land bridges that later flooded, a source of legends about lost homelands and drowned cities.

## After the ice

As the ice retreated they scattered, and different peoples branched off from them.

## In the modern day

The ice age people have faded into legend, but traces remain:

- northern families with tough skin;
- ruins in old glacier valleys;
- folk tales of frozen-age giants;
- scholars arguing over whether they were real.

## Ideas and open threads

- They could be the origin of **dwarves or goliaths**. (An idea in the notes; no such race is confirmed.)
- They may remember a warm golden age before the ice.
- [[The First Cult|The first cult]] of [[Magic|magic]] may have been an ice age people, which would put the oldest bloodlines in that era.

## Open questions

- Where were the ice age refuges on the map?
- How do the ice age people relate to the five races? Were they one race, or members of several? (The notes call them simply "mortals".)
