The **Xi council of elders** is the council of 17 elderly men that leads the [[Xi people|Xi]] people.

## Purpose

The council leads the Xi people. It decided both to invade the mainland and, decades later, to withdraw.

## Structure and ranks

- 17 elders, all elderly men.
- When one of the 17 dies, the elders elect a new elder from the men on the island.
- From the time Xi children are 3, the elders watch them, ready to pair two Xi youths for marriage when they are 15.

## History

- The Xi lived on a small tropical island that no one had discovered. Hearing rumours of a much larger land, the council decided the people would go and live there instead. They attacked the west coast and held it for a few decades (the [[Xi Empire]], 350–430 in the timeline).
- The council then decided the large kingdom wasn't worth maintaining, and many men were unhappy at being away from their families, so the elders pulled out.
- In [[The Runaways]] Book 2, the Xi offer sanctuary to [[Eric]]'s group for possessing and being connected to [[xiramite]]. The notes don't say the council made that decision.
