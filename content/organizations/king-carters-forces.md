**King Carter's forces** are the army, guards and officials who serve [[King Carter]] in [[The Runaways]], and the enemy of [[The Resistance]].

## Purpose

After the [[Fall of Fillyolia|fall of Fillyolia]], Carter sent his forces to reunite the villages so they would be strong against outside forces. They also hunt the resistance and keep order in Carter's prisons.

## Structure and ranks

The notes name no formal ranks for the army. Some known roles:

| Role | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King | [[King Carter]] | Rules from [[Carthollow]] |
| Leader in the forces | [[Brian]] | Secretly the resistance's top spy |

## The dungeon and the King's Trial

The [[Carthollow Dungeon]] has 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. [[Ulrich]] thinks he is in charge and is typically first on the scene to punish prisoners; [[Kayne]] is listed as main captain of the guard. Two guards work against the king: [[Frode]] is paid by Brian to let prisoners out, and [[Gunter]] is a double agent working with Brian to free prisoners.

The king holds [[The King's Trial]], a bracket tournament in which prisoners fight with wooden swords until one is disarmed or yields; the winner is promised freedom.

## History

- **Before the books:** one of Carter's force leaders attacked and ransacked a village that refused to join, killing [[Jane Barker]]'s mother and brother.
- **Book 1:** the army from Carthollow comes to [[Harrisonville]] and demands [[Eric]] and [[David]]. Later the army marches on [[Misty Phlats]], arriving sooner than expected, and the resistance flees.
- **Book 2:** Carter's armies attack [[Keyesport Vale]] and capture the island and everyone on it. Captured resistance members are held on an [[island prison]].
- **Book 3:** the resistance and its Xi allies march on Carthollow, and a massive battle follows.

> [!info] Canon note
> The capital is **Carthollow** in the 2026 sheet and **Carterville** in older notes.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - The raid on Jane's village was the force leader's own decision, not Carter's order.
> - [[David]] becomes a servant to Carter by giving him information about the resistance, and acts as his messenger in Book 3 (older plot).
> - Carter's aggression comes from [[Dherlin]]'s possession of him; see [[King Carter]].
