**The Resistance** is the rebel army in [[The Runaways]] that fights to dethrone [[King Carter]], and the force that [[Eric]] joins after escaping the [[Carthollow Dungeon]].

## Purpose

To overthrow King Carter, whom the resistance accuses of having stolen the throne from [[King Augo]], and to "dethrone King Carter from his evil ways" (Book 2 blurb). Members join for their own reasons: the [[Jane Barker|Barker]] family joined after their village was ransacked by Carter's forces, and [[Brian]] joined because he saw Carter as too dangerous to rule.

## Structure and ranks

The resistance has four levels of command (2021 leadership doc). Each level below the lead command "takes command in event of no leadership of level above present".

| Lead command | Leader, co-leader (general) | The whole resistance: leads it, makes its decisions and plans its movements | — | [[Zandar]], [[Elliott]], [[Aerelm]] | Zandar (leader), Elliott (2nd in command) |

> The 2024 cast list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md) moves several people from their 2021 ranks (sources/drive/runaways/resistance-leadership-personal.md): Scott and Kolt from 1st to 2nd level, Suew from 2nd to 3rd. It also calls Jaek "3rd level resistance leader (lieutenant)", though lieutenants are the 2nd level. The 2021 doc also notes some holders' fates (see Secrets).

## Bases and places

- The **resistance base** in the [[Southern Misty Mountains]], reached through [[Misty Phlats]], with its main cavern and the [[Training Grove]]. Weapons were kept in "holding trees" near the base.
- [[Mourncliff]], where the resistance has a safeguard and regroups after Misty Phlats.
- [[Keyesport Vale]], where it shelters at the end of Book 1.

## History

- **Book 1:** recruits are trained as soldiers at the base; the resistance scouts and buys weapons at [[Shadow's Edge]]. When King Carter's army marches on Misty Phlats sooner than expected, the resistance plans an escape toward Keyesport Vale: [[Samheir]] leads a frontal charge, [[Zandar]] and [[Elliott]] strike from the side to distract the main army, and the rest escape through the back passes. Eric's fireball over the valley kills many resistance men as well as enemies, and some members are upset by it. The groups regroup one by one at Mourncliff, then travel to [[Moonwick]] and take ships to Keyesport Vale.
- **Book 2:** King Carter's armies attack Keyesport Vale and capture the island; only a few members are hidden by Eric and Jane's magic. The survivors find sanctuary on [[Xi]], learn to fight like the Xi, recover the weapons from the old base, and attack the [[island prison]] where captured resistance members are held.
- **Book 3:** the resistance, joined with Xi forces, frees the rest of its prisoners and marches on Carthollow.

## Ideas and open threads

- The cast list's spare and "more medieval" names ([Rewis], [Samhier], [Cai], [Mador]) suggest several members may be renamed.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **The resistance began as a lie.** In the Fillyolia twist, [[King Augo]], greedy for the power Carter was gaining, started rumours of a resistance against him. [[Zandar]], "a nobody dreaming of power", offered to lead it and make it real, in return for becoming Augo's second-hand man, meaning to kill Augo once the throne was won. Together they spread the story that Carter had stolen the throne from Augo. "No good guys in this war"; "Resistance is part bad guys".
> - **Fates in the 2021 leadership doc:** Sam (dead), Brian (captured), Joseph Barker (captured). Joseph later dies in prison.
> - [[David]] secretly reports the location of the resistance base to the King (older plot).
> - At the fort in Book 2, all members except Eric, Jane, Lucas, Bella, Jaek, Kaz, Crisig and Crewt are killed or captured. Suew, Nad and Scott are among the prisoners freed from the island prison; Eric is captured in that attack.
> - **End:** in the battle of Carthollow Zandar tries to seize power and is killed by Elliott. After the battle the resistance is disbanded and all members are free to do what they want; Carter remains king and Augo is imprisoned.
