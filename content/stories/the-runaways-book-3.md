***The Runaways*, Book 3** is the third novel of [[The Runaways]], set in 1069/1070, which brings the resistance's war against [[King Carter]] to its final battle at [[Carthollow]]. It has no title yet.

## Premise

Following on from *[[Stomped in the Dust]]*, Book 3 carries the resistance's war against King Carter to his city of Carthollow, the setting of the series' final battle, while Eric keeps learning about the life he lost when he woke up in the dungeon.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> **Plot** (the 31 steps of the ideas doc, lightly reworded):
>
> 1. Eric is still dealing with flashbacks.
> 2. [[Crisig]] finds and frees Eric.
> 3. Crisig leads Eric to a small area where he finds [[Brian]], [[Kolt]] and [[Henri]].
> 4. Together the five escape.
> 5. [[Jane Barker|Jane]] tries to lead the Xi forces, but they aren't willing to follow her.
> 6. [[Lucas]] becomes Jane's voice in leading the Xi and resistance army.
> 7. Eric uses magic to visit Jane in a dream and tells her where they are ([[Sprake]], [[Dyken]]).
> 8. Jane and a small group go and get them.
> 9. Back on Xi, Eric is still learning about his past from nightmarish flashbacks.
> 10. The resistance soldiers keep learning to fight like the Xi.
> 11. Eric learns that he killed [[Eric's mother|his mother]] through uncontrolled magic, and that this is why he was put in prison.
> 12. Eric becomes even more depressed.
> 13. Jane comforts Eric and they share a special moment (a little kiss).
> 14. They find out Brian is Eric's father.
> 15. They decide on a plan to attack Carterville.
> 16. The full force attacks the [[Island Prison|prison island]] again and frees the rest of the resistance prisoners.
> 17. They march at full speed straight for Carterville.
> 18. Approaching Carterville, they see a messenger sent out, and are surprised to see that it is [[David]].
> 19. David has become a servant to Carter because of his willingness to give him information about the resistance.
> 20. They give David a message to relay to the king, then attack Carterville.
> 21. A massive battle begins.
> 22. [[Xander|Zandar]], seeing the battle partly going his way, decides he wants to take power, but sees Eric in his path first.
> 23. He approaches Eric to kill him, but David sacrifices himself to save Eric.
> 24. [[Elliott]] kills Zandar in defence of Eric.
> 25. Eric uses magic to call loudly for the fighting to stop.
> 26. Eric meets [[King Augo|Augo]], Carter and all the leaders in the middle, and they discuss what just happened. Augo tries to stab Carter, but Lucas cuts his hand off first.
> 27. The battle is over. Carter remains king, Augo is thrown in prison, and the resistance is disbanded, its members free to do what they want.
> 28. Eric and Jane both dream of [[Dherlin]] again; in Eric's, Dherlin threatens to kill him. Eric wakes to see Carter standing over him with a dagger.
> 29. Eric uses magic to disarm Carter, senses the evil presence in him, and uses a magic he has never heard of before to destroy Dherlin's presence.
> 30. They discuss what just happened with a few friends and decide it is best to go and put an end to this threat.
> 31. They decide [[Averill]] is the best place to start looking for Dherlin, and gather a group to go: Eric, Jane, Lucas, [[Scott Barker|Scott]], [[Jaek]], Crisig, [[Crewt]], [[Rykor]] and [[Liko]].
>
> **Eric and Jane.** After they reunite, Jane doesn't want to be separated again and, without knowing it, helps Eric through his depression and PTSD. As the depression lifts he has a flashback of killing his mother and wants nothing to do with anyone. Jane finds him overlooking the ocean and comforts him; he tells her everything, and she gives him a hug and a kiss. Thus their romance begins. They marry after the battle of Carterville and before leaving for Averill (what follows is on [[The Runaways]]).
>
> **Canon notes.**
> - The plot calls the city Carterville; the 2026 notes call it Carthollow.
> - David's sacrifice (steps 18–23) conflicts with the 2026 character sheet, where David "Dies in chapter 1" of Book 1. The character-development notes agree with the plot: David "ends up giving his life to save Eric's in the final battle".
> - Step 24 has Elliott kill Zandar. The character-development notes say Zandar tries to kill both Augo and Carter and "gets killed himself by Tahir"; the same notes give Elliott the alternate name Tahir, while the cast list gives him the alternate name Rewis.
> - The cast list (2024) calls King Augo "King of Keyesport Vale"; the plot-twist notes make him the rightful heir of [[Fillyolia]] and Carter's brother.
