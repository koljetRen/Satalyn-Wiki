**Draft passages** are the pieces of prose the author has written ahead for *[[The Runaways]]*, collected here word for word, including the original spelling and punctuation.

The first six come from the doc "Paragraphs for The Runaways - personal" (2021), listed as "Prewritten Paragraphs" in the author's links sheet. Their order and headings here are the wiki's; the doc gives them no titles. Two longer scenes from the Runaways ideas doc, which give away plot, are in the Secrets callout at the end.

## Liko's shield wall

[[Liko]] holds his place in a shield wall as the enemy hits the line.

> Liko charged into battle, following his commander. This battle was an important one. This wasn't training anymore, this was war. Soldiers he had trained with were going to die. Liko's squad reached the line predetermined for the shield wall. Liko stood over his squadmates, sliding his shield into position. Now all they could do was wait for the enemy to hit the line. Liko looked at his shield, then ran his eyes down the line of shields. This wall was the only thing standing between them and the enemy. If it held, they would win. If even one little part of the wall was broken, everyone on this side would suffer. Liko heard someone yell "Brace!" Liko dug his feet into the ground and felt something ram into his shield. All he had to do was make sure his shield didn't break from the wall and they would be safe.

## Rykor in battle

[[Rykor]] fights an enemy on the battlefield, in front of the Xi shield wall.

> Rykor stood up after ramming his sword into an enemy on the ground. He looked around, sweat dripping down his brow. His sweat trickled into his eyes and the cuts on his face from the battle. Everywhere Rykor looked, was death. The battlefield was colored with the blood of his friends and their enemies. Behind him was the infamous Xi shield wall, which was impenetrable. Rykor suddenly whipped his head around as he heard another enemy charge for him. The enemy, a shorter man weilding a short sword and a cracked, rectangular shield, was running at Rykor at a full sprint. Rykor held his circular shield to his chest, sword at the ready just behind it. Rykor stared his new opponent down as the unfortunate enemy came for him. The enemy raised his sword to attack Rykor and that's when Rykor moved. His shield exploded into motion towards the arm wielding the sword. The shield hit Rykor's enemy square in the wrist, causing the sword to slip out of the opponent's hand. The impact also caused the enemy to spin in the direction of the blow, allowing for Rykor's sword to slip past the shield and pierce the enemy in his side just below the ribs. Rykor pushed the sword further into the enemy's body and drove the now corpse to the ground. He pulled the bloodied sword out of the man's side. Rykor's opponent had never stood a chance.

## The silent camp

The group sits in silence after a battle, until [[Lucas]] asks what they will do now and Aerlem ([[Aerelm]]) answers.

> The group sat around camp, no one was talking. Silence filled the sky. It was so quiet even the animals weren't making a sound. No crickets chirping, no wolves howling, only silence. No wind licking the grass, no rocks wrestling down a mountain. Only silence. The sun sank down, slowly losing its strength. Finally, the sun bleeds out into the blue sky, turning it from the pretty caribbean blue to a mix of oranges, yellows, reds, and pinks. The sun had finally bled out, drowning under the horizon. Millions of stars took their places against the midnight black sky. The only evidence of the sun was the deep blue that slowly followed the sun into the horizon. Eventually, the group gathered together around a fire that someone had started. Silence still followed them, only the crackle of the flames from the fire broke the deathly silence. Finally, someone spoke. "What are we going to do now?" It was Lucas. Nobody answered him, the deadly battle still on the back of their minds. Aerlem finally spoke. "Man, if you gotta ask, you'll never know."

## Lucas and the sand

By a campfire, Lucas explains why he hates the texture of sand, and [[Eric]] teases him about it.

> "I hate the texture of sand." Everyone turned and looked at Lucas. The campfire light danced across his face, which had started to grow scruffy. It was the day after they had escaped from the island and traveled north along the coast. Lucas looked up and saw everyone looking at him, a few seeming like they wanted an explanation. "I don't like the texture of sand because it seems like it should be smooth with it being so close to the water, but sand is just small rocks. It feels rough when walking through it barefoot and if even a small amount gets in your boots, your feet start to slowly get grinded away." Most of the people around the campfire went back to staring deep into the night or at the ground. Almost everyone disregarded Lucas at that point. Eric was the exception. Having become good friends with Lucas over the past few months, Eric started making jokes about Lucas's hatred of sand. A few people chuckled at what Eric said. Lucas couldn't help but chuckle himself.

## Eric's mother

A description of [[Eric's mother]] and her care for the less fortunate.

> Eric's mother was known for always caring. She could always be found around those who were less fortunate than her. Whenever she passed someone sitting on the side of a road, she would always give them something, whether it was food, money, or something else she would always stop and give. When people asked her about this, she always replied that it was her duty. His father was a war veteran, one of the best, so Eric's mother always felt that while he was keeping the people as a nation safe, she should help with keeping individual people safe and cared for. This sometimes meant that she would go out of her way to help others. No matter what situation she was in or where she was, she would always stop and help someone. She helped them with a positive attitude, rather than performing these acts by making it seem like she was required to.

## The tiny arrowhead rocks

Eric finds hundreds of tiny arrowhead-shaped rocks in an odd circle of stone in the grass.

> The moment Eric found that teeny-tiny, little rock changed his life. The rock was shaped like an arrowhead, but it seemed way too small to be an arrowhead. The rock was about the size of a splinter of wood. He looked at the tiny rock for a few minutes, before kneeling down and looking for other pieces. Sure enough, he found hundreds of the little rocks, each shaped the same way. Eric motioned for Lucas to come and check them out.
>
> "This is what we are looking for," he said.
>
> "Good. We are on the right trail then," Eric replied.
>
> They gathered enough of the small rocks to fill 3 full pouches in their bags. As they walked away from the odd patch of stone in the grass, Eric kept looking back. What had caused the rocks to be split like that? What had caused the perfect circle of stone in the grass?

## Notes

- "Aerlem" in *The silent camp* is the spelling in the original; the cast list spells the wizard **Aerelm**.
- *Lucas and the sand* is set "the day after they had escaped from the island and traveled north along the coast" (compare [[Stomped in the Dust]]).
- In *The tiny arrowhead rocks*, both lines of dialogue seem to be given to Eric ("he said" after Eric has called Lucas over, then "Eric replied"); the original is kept as it is.
