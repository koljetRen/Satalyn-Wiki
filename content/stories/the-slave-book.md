***The Slave Book*** (working title; earlier drafts are titled *Slave Hunters*) is a planned one-volume spin-off of [[The Runaways]], starting in 1071, about [[Peter Snyder]], a boy king who is sold into slavery and joins the slave hunters.

## Premise

Peter Snyder, a prince of the kingdom of Snyder, becomes king at 15 when his family dies. Soon afterwards he is kidnapped in the night and sold to slavers taking a caravan across the [[Great Desert]]. Halfway across the desert he escapes, and is taken in by a slave hunter on a mission to find and free all slaves. He joins the mission, and after some years of slave hunting the slave hunters meet the group from *The Runaways*; see [[The Joining of the Series]].

## Structure

The author plans one book. The 2021 notes set out its frame:

- 1st chapter: a preview of the escape from the caravan
- 2nd chapter: a jump back to months before his parents' death
- Last chapter: they find [[Eric]] and company

The 2024 plot outline follows the same shape: it opens in the slave caravan and tells Peter's past in two flashbacks.

## Characters

- [[Peter Snyder]] — "King Kid", the protagonist
- [[King William Snyder VII]] — his father, king of Snyder
- [[William Snyder VIII]] — his elder brother
- [[The Head Helper]] — head of the council that helps Peter rule
- [[Jason]] — a boy from the author's handwritten slave-hunters pages, known only from fragments
- [[Eric]] — met at the end of the book

## Places

- [[Snyder]] — the nation of King William Snyder VII in the draft opening; the plot outline calls Peter "heir to the throne of [[Forin]]"
- The [[Great Desert]] — crossed by the slave caravan
- [[Aegryamis]] — on the east side of the Great Desert

## Revision notes

- **Versions.** "Slave Hunters Stuff" (2021): the 13-step premise and chapter plan. "Slave Hunters" (2021): the earlier draft opening. "The Slave Book, From the Runaways Series" (2024): the plot outline and the later draft opening. The 2024 version is followed here.
- **The opening.** Both drafts open with Peter at 15 "enjoying life", tell of his family's death 51 days before his 16th birthday, and end as he is seated at the head of his father's U-shaped council table. Around the table sit 22 people, from their early 20s to older than his grandfather would have been. There is an empty seat on each side of the head chair at the bottom of the U, twelve seats along the outside of each arm of the U, and one empty chair on each side.
- **Handwritten pages.** Four photos of handwritten notebook pages (`IMG_8222–8225.JPG`) are in the Slave Hunters folder but were not imported; text recognition only caught fragments, such as a draft about a boy named [[Jason]] whose parents left him a perfectly balanced knife.

> [!info] Canon note
> In the 2021 opening Peter is the **eldest** son and only his parents die; in the 2024 opening he is the **second** son and his elder brother William VIII dies with them. The draft opening calls the kingdom "the nation of Snyder", but the 2024 plot outline says "the throne of Forin". The 2024 outline says the family died in an accident on a trip; its draft opening says "no one knew how, only that they died together".

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> **Plot outline (2024), lightly reworded:**
>
> - Peter is in a slave caravan. He and three other slaves manage to escape.
> - One of the slaves dies from an arrow shot at him; another dies a few hours later.
> - Peter and the other man spend time in the desert, and Peter starts to think.
> - *Flashback:* Peter was a prince, heir to the throne of Forin. His father, mother and older brother went on a trip to visit something; an accident happened and they died. Peter was placed in the ruling seat at 15, with a council to help him.
> - Slave hunters find Peter and the other man. Despite their efforts, the other slave dies.
> - Peter joins the slave hunters because he doesn't want others to suffer the way he did. Then: "Stuff with slave hunters".
> - *Flashback part 2:* after Peter has been leader a little while, [[The Head Helper|the head helper]] wants the power. He kidnaps Peter in his sleep and hides him away, and makes people think Peter ran away; since there are no more heirs, he takes the king's place. Peter wakes up, is beaten and given to slavers — back to the start of the book.
> - Peter starts noticing some weird things happening around him. The slave hunters decide that the next time they are on the east side of the Great Desert, they should talk to a wizard at [[Aegryamis]].
> - They carry on doing slave-hunter things.
> - They meet "this dude named Eric (no idea who that could be)", who tells him "I'm a wizard Peter", and they join together on an "epic quest".
>
> **Premise (2021), the 13 steps:** (1) Kid becomes king at 15 after his parents' death. (2) Someone on the council plots against him. (3) The king kid is kidnapped in the night. (4) The council member takes control of the kingdom. (5) The king kid is sold to slavers crossing the Great Desert. (6) Halfway through the desert he escapes the slavers with three others. (7) One escapee dies shortly outside the camp. (8) Another dies of his injuries a few hours later. (9) On the second day, the king kid and the other escapee meet the slave hunter. (10) The slave hunter is on a mission to find and free all slaves. (11) The king kid and the other escapee join the slave hunter's mission. (12) After two or three years of slave hunting, they meet the group from *The Runaways*. (13) All the slave hunters join the Runaways on their mission.
>
> Canon note: in the 2021 premise the fourth escapee survives and joins the slave hunter with Peter; in the 2024 outline he dies despite the slave hunters' efforts.
