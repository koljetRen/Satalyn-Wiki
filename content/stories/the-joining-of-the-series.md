**The joining of the series** is the point, in the year 1074, where the main *[[The Runaways]]* story and its spin-off books come together.

## Premise

The author's timeline of the Runaways world ends: "1074 - Runaways and shoot off books join together". Two spin-offs lead into it: *[[The Slave Book]]* (from 1071) and *[[The Wizard Book]]* (from 1072).

From the slave-book side, the notes say how the join happens: after two or three years of slave hunting, [[Peter Snyder]] and the slave hunters meet the group from *The Runaways*, and all the slave hunters join the Runaways on their mission. The slave book's last chapter is planned as "find Eric and co".

## Open questions

- Is the joining a book of its own, or the end of the spin-offs?
- What is the Runaways' "mission" that the slave hunters join? See the Secrets callout.
- How does *The Wizard Book* join in? The notes don't say.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> In the 2024 slave-book outline, the meeting is with "this dude named Eric", who tells Peter "I'm a wizard Peter", and they "join together on epic quest".
>
> Book 3 ends with Eric and his friends setting out from [[Averill]] to put an end to [[Dherlin]], and the notes on Eric and Jane's relationship run on to the final quest against Dherlin, Scott's death, and the birth of their son at [[Lucal]] (see [[The Runaways]]). The notes don't say whether this quest is the "mission" or "epic quest" the slave hunters join in 1074; that is a reasonable reading, but not stated.
