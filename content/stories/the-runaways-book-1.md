***The Runaways*, Book 1** is the first novel of [[The Runaways]], set in 1067, in which [[Eric]] wakes up with no memory in the dungeon of [[Carthollow]], escapes, and joins the [[The Resistance|resistance]] against [[King Carter]].

## Premise

Eric wakes up in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] with no memory. There he meets his cellmate [[David]] and other prisoners and guards, among them [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Ulrich]], [[The Torturer|the torturer]] and [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], and learns of [[The King's Trial]]: a tournament the king set up so prisoners can fight for their freedom. Each prisoner gets a wooden sword and fights until one is disarmed or yields; in the bracket-style tournament the winner is promised freedom. The prisoners call it "the Trial" for short.

Once out of the dungeon, Eric makes his way to the resistance in the mountains, trains as a soldier, and begins to discover that he has magic. The book ends with King Carter's armies at [[Misty Phlats]], which Book 2's blurb says the resistance "narrowly" escaped.

## Revision notes

The first draft, "The Runaways - First Draft" (2023-03-22), uses older names (Carterville, Harrisonville) and has not been imported into the wiki sources. The author's list of edits for the rewrite, and the rewritten plot itself, are in the Secrets callout below because they give away the plot.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> **Edits for the Book 1 rewrite** (from the ideas doc, quoted as listed):
>
> - Draw out being in prison
> - Include David more once they make it to the resistance
> - Introduce Crisig (and possibly Crewt) earlier
> - Cut out James, Damon, and Martha
> - Rewrite the whole Harrisonvile subplot
> - Focus on detail
> - Write Eric and Jane's relationship as more of a friendship rather than as siblings
> - Build the culture of the area
> - Don't have Brian come with Eric and David, just let them out
> - When traveling from Harrisonville to the Southern Misty Mountains, have them follow a river by boat
>
> **Rewritten plot** (the 30 steps of the ideas doc, lightly reworded):
>
> 1. Eric wakes up in prison.
> 2. He meets his cellmate David.
> 3. After a few weeks, [[Brian]] sets them free and tells them to run to [[Harrisonville]], giving them a little money for provisions.
> 4. The escape to Harrisonville, hiding from King Carter's forces in a small cave in a forest.
> 5. In Harrisonville they try to lodge at the inn, but don't have enough money and are kicked out to the streets.
> 6. David steals money from people and gets the guard called on him.
> 7. They meet [[Scott Barker|Scott]], who recognizes them and leads them to [[Joseph Barker|Joseph]] and [[Jane Barker|Jane]]. The five stay together for a few nights while Eric and David are given better resources.
> 8. The army from Carterville comes and demands them, so Joseph makes them flee in the night, but stays behind himself.
> 9. They travel through the [[Aramore Forest]] to the Southern Misty Mountains.
> 10. While sleeping in the Aramore Forest, Eric and Jane get a dream about [[Dherlin]].
> 11. They reach [[Misty Phlats]] and stay at the inn, where they meet [[Crisig]] and [[Samheir]].
> 12. With Samheir they travel to the resistance base and meet [[Xander|Zandar]] and [[Elliott]].
> 13. Eric and David start training as soldiers; Eric meets and befriends [[Lucas]].
> 14. [[Aerelm]] visits the resistance and senses the magic in Eric.
> 15. Eric starts training as a wizard as well.
> 16. Some time between starting training and going to Shadow's Edge, Eric and Jane have important relationship-building in the mountains.
> 17. After a few weeks of training, in which Eric's skills grow very well, they are sent to [[Shadow's Edge]] to scout and buy weapons. Here David sneaks off unnoticed and sends the King a report about the resistance base.
> 18. They travel back to Misty Phlats after a few days and find reports of the army marching their way.
> 19. Eric is led to a pocket of [[xiramite]] and uses it to make his sword and a dagger.
> 20. [[Shetien]] identifies it as from the [[Xi Empire]] age, but doesn't know how it got to the Southern Misty Mountains.
> 21. Preparations are made to flee the resistance base.
> 22. The army reaches Misty Phlats quicker than expected.
> 23. A plan is drawn up to escape to [[Keyesport Vale]]: Samheir takes the frontal charge, and Zandar and Elliott come in from the side to distract the main army, while the rest of the resistance escapes through the back passes.
> 24. The battle starts.
> 25. A while into the battle, Eric goes "full avatar state mode" and fireballs the whole valley, putting himself into a death-like state.
> 26. Lucas, [[Jaek]] and [[Henri]] manage to pull his body away and carry it to the meeting point at [[Mourncliff]].
> 27. One by one the groups of resistance refugees reach Mourncliff and hear what Eric did; some cheer, while others are upset that he killed so many resistance men as well.
> 28. After a few days, Jane has a dream of Eric overlooking the valley; she comforts him and asks him to come back to the land of the living.
> 29. Jane wakes to find Eric still lifeless and is very sad, but later in the day he wakes up.
> 30. The whole resistance is together again; they travel to [[Moonwick]] and board ships to Keyesport Vale, where they spend a few days and then see ships on the horizon.
>
> **Xiramite idea** (not to be explained in Book 1; maybe in 2 or 3): xiramite is straight black when first forged, but cracks and lets its power out as it connects to its user. While Eric was unconscious, his conscience was learning from the xiramite, which formed the connection. Lucas notices Eric's sword cracking as they travel and worries it might be destroyed, because his own dagger never cracked; he doesn't know that this is because his dagger never formed a true connection with him and is still raw.
>
> **Canon note — the newer prison opening (2026).** The 2026 plot sheet changes the start of the book, and newer notes win for the main text: in the Carthollow Dungeon Eric meets David, Rann, Bardon, Ulrich, the torturer and Akio (in that order), learns of the King's Trial, and **watches David get beaten to death in a cafeteria skirmish**; the character sheet says David "Dies in chapter 1". Eric reaches the third round of the Trial before being defeated, and the tournament is what exposes [[Brian]] to Eric. Bardon is a resistance member who was captured on purpose, and teams up with [[Rykor]] to break Eric and Akio out; Bardon and Akio then travel alongside Eric. The 30-step plot above (2024) instead has Brian free Eric and David, and David survives into the resistance (and betrays it in step 17). The 2024 background-character list also has the prison guards [[Frode]] (paid by Brian to let prisoners out) and [[Gunter]] (a double agent working with Brian to free prisoners). The 2026 sheet calls the city Carthollow; the 2024 plot calls it Carterville.
