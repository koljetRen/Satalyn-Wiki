**Who's who in *The Runaways*** lists every named character of *[[The Runaways]]* and its spin-off [[The Slave Book|slave book]] in one place, with their role, culture and allegiance at the start of their story. Each name links to the character's own page.

"—" means the notes don't say. Culture is given only where the notes state it (for the slave-book cast, their homeland). Allegiance is where the character stands when the reader first meets them.

> [!info] Canon note
> Several names differ between notes; the newest spelling is used above and the others are in brackets. Elliott's alternate name is "Rewis" in the cast list and "Tahir" in the character notes. King Augo is "King of Keyesport Vale" in the cast list but "King [Argo?]" in the Book 2 blurb (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md`, `sources/drive/runaways/book-2-stomped-in-the-dust.md`). Peter Snyder is the second son in the 2024 outline but the eldest son in an earlier draft, and his kingdom is "the nation of Snyder" in one note and "the throne of Forin" in another (`sources/drive/slave-hunters/`). Willy Smith is the mayor of Harrisonville in the cast list and the tavern owner in the character notes.

> **Fates and twists**, from the character pages (mainly `sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`; newer notes as marked):
>
> | Name | Fate or twist |
> | --- | --- |
> | Eric | Killed his mother with uncontrolled magic, which is why he was imprisoned. Captured in the first island raid; escapes in Book 3. Learns Brian is his father. Marries Jane, destroys Dherlin's presence in Carter, later defeats Dherlin, and lives out his life in Carterville with Jane and their son Scott. |
> | Jane Barker | Romance with Eric begins in Book 3; marries him; stays at Lucal while pregnant; son named Scott. |
> | Lucas | Eric's half cousin. Cuts off Augo's hand when Augo tries to stab Carter. Joins the hunt for Dherlin. |
> | David | 2026 sheet: "dies in chapter 1", beaten to death in a dungeon skirmish. Older plot: betrays the resistance, becomes Carter's servant, and dies saving Eric from Zandar at Carterville. |
> | Brian | Eric's father. Listed "(captured)" in 2021; escapes with Eric in Book 3. |
> | Scott Barker | Captured, freed in the island raid; takes Dherlin's curse on Jane's unborn child and dies. |
> | Joseph Barker | Captured; dies in prison. |
> | King Carter | Possessed by Dherlin (the cause of his mood swings). In the Fillyolia twist he took the throne only after Augo declined it. Remains king after Book 3; Eric destroys Dherlin's presence in him. |
> | King Augo | In the Fillyolia twist, Carter's brother and rightful heir who declined the throne, then started the resistance out of greed. Tries to stab Carter; loses a hand; imprisoned. |
> | Dherlin | Killed Fillyolia's royal family; possesses Carter; curses Jane's child; defeated by Eric. |
> | Eric's mother | Killed by Eric's uncontrolled magic. |
> | Zandar | "A nobody dreaming of power" who leads the resistance for Augo, meaning to kill him. Tries to seize power at Carterville and is killed by Elliott ("by Tahir"). |
> | Elliott | Kills Zandar in Eric's defence. |
