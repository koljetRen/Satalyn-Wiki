***Stomped in the Dust*** is the second novel of [[The Runaways]], set between 1067 and 1069, in which King Carter's armies capture the resistance's refuge at [[Keyesport Vale]] and [[Eric]] must escape and rebuild the resistance.

## Premise

The book's back-cover blurb:

> After narrowly escaping the armies of King Carter at Misty Phlats, the resistance finds themselves at Keyesport Vale, home of King [Argo?]. But they aren't safe for long. King Carter's armies attack and capture the island and all of the men and women living there. Eric, now one of the leaders in the resistance, must find a way to escape the island and restart the force to dethrone King Carter from his evil ways. Along the way, Eric learns more about who he was before he woke up in the dungeon as well as his potential.

The "King [Argo?]" of the blurb is the king of Keyesport Vale, called [[King Augo]] in the cast list.

## Open questions

- The blurb calls Keyesport Vale an island and has Carter's armies capture "the island"; the plot outline speaks of a battle "at the fort" and Carter's forces taking "the city". Are these the same place?
- The blurb's "King [Argo?]" — the bracket and question mark are the author's own; is the name Augo?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> **Possible plot** (the 28 steps of the ideas doc, lightly reworded):
>
> 1. A large battle at the fort.
> 2. Eric and Jane use their magic together to hide a few resistance members: Eric, Jane, Lucas, Bella, Jaek, Kaz, Crisig and Crewt.
> 3. All the other resistance members are killed or captured. Eric sees [[David]] alongside the enemy soldiers.
> 4. King Carter's forces take over the city.
> 5. About a week later, Eric sees a ship on the horizon and uses magic to send it a message.
> 6. The ship comes and asks for more water, because it has run out.
> 7. The remaining resistance members board the ship.
> 8. They travel south to [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]].
> 9. They spend time there.
> 10. They meet a Xi warrior ([[Rykor]]), who tries to kill them for possessing [[xiramite]].
> 11. After seeing Eric wield his fully cracked sword, the warrior yields.
> 12. He tries to take only Eric, but Eric won't leave the rest of them.
> 13. Lucas shows his dagger, and the Xi warrior yields.
> 14. Together they all travel west to [[Xi]].
> 15. They are offered sanctuary on Xi for possessing, and being connected to, xiramite.
> 16. The group learns about the Xi people and how they fight.
> 17. They learn more about xiramite (cracking shows connection).
> 18. Lucas is taught how to connect.
> 19. They spend one to two months on Xi; Lucas becomes connected to his dagger.
> 20. Eric gains favour with the people, as he is a wizard and can wield xiramite: the first ever with Xi ancestry to control magic.
> 21. Eric tells them about the battle at the fort and at Misty Phlats.
> 22. Eric leads an expedition to the Southern Misty Peaks to retrieve all the weapons from the holding trees and the old resistance base.
> 23. They find out that most of the resistance prisoners are held on an island prison.
> 24. They return to Xi and gather a force to free the prisoners.
> 25. They attack the island and free some of the prisoners (the prisoners saved from Book 1, [[Suew]], [[Nad]] and [[Scott Barker|Scott]]), but Eric is captured.
> 26. Jane and Lucas lead the resistance force back to Xi and start plotting how to free Eric; they also find out that Crisig is missing.
> 27. Eric is thrown into a dark prison cell and becomes deeply depressed.
> 28. Eric starts having flashbacks of the time before he was put in prison the first time.
>
> **Characters and places.** Besides Eric, the outline features [[Jane Barker|Jane]], [[Lucas]], [[Bella]], [[Jaek]], [[Kaz]], [[Crisig]], [[Crewt]] and the Xi warrior [[Rykor]]. The route runs [[Keyesport Vale]] → [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]] → [[Xi]] → the [[Misty Peaks|Southern Misty Peaks]] and the old [[Resistance Base]] → the [[Island Prison|island prison]].
>
> The scene "Revealing the Xi Daggers" on [[Draft passages]] is a draft of steps 10–13.
>
> **Eric and Jane.** They begin the series written like siblings: best friends, with neither thinking of more. When Eric is captured at the end of Book 2, Jane realizes how much he means to her and that she has deeper feelings for him.
>
> **Canon notes.**
> - Step 3 has David alive and on the enemy's side. The 2026 character sheet says David "Dies in chapter 1" of Book 1.
> - Here the group first meets Rykor in Quino. The 2026 plot sheet has Rykor helping [[Bardon]] break Eric and Akio out of the Carthollow Dungeon in Book 1.
> - The cast list (2024) names [[Liko]], Rykor and [[Rhao]] as "New Xi/Resistance" members.
> - The draft passage about sand is set "the day after they had escaped from the island and traveled north along the coast", while the plot outline has them sail south to Quino.
