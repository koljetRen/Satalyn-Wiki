The **name bank** collects the spare names the author has jotted down for characters and places in [[Satalyn]] but not yet given to anyone. Names are copied exactly as written, including spellings.

The author's guidance on naming, from the notes: "Make characters more Medieval age named", and in the cast list, "[change name to make it more medieval]". For which real-world languages to draw names from in each region, see the [[peoples/cultures/index|culture pages]] (Aramore, Skarg, Dyken, Osisu, Aegryamis and Xi). The Xi naming rule is: names are usually two or three syllables; common men's names end in vowels, noble men's names in harsh consonants.
