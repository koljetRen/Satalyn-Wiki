A **dormant bloodline** is a family line in which the magic trait has lain hidden for generations and then, rarely, resurfaces in one person — as it did in [[Eric]] and [[Dherlin]].

## How it works

Magic weakens as a bloodline thins, until descendants can't cast at all (see [[Bloodline Magic]]). But "rarely a dormant trait resurfaces". The older *Runaways* notes describe the same thing: most powerful wizards are the children of two wizards, "but every once in a while there is an odd ball with a powerful, yet previously dormant dna strand (Eric, Dherlin, etc)".

So a dormant-bloodline wizard can be powerful, not just able to cast.

## Known cases

| Wizard | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [[Eric]] | Of Xi descent through an ancestor who broke the Xi empire's rule against foreign marriage; the notes call him "the first ever with xi ancestry to control magic". He starts with raw power he can't control. |
| [[Dherlin]] | The evil wizard of *The Runaways*, thought to be dead. |

## Open questions

- Is there anything in common between Eric's and Dherlin's lines?
