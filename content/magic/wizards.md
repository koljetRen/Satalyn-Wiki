**Wizards** are people who can wield magic, which in Satalyn means descendants of [[The First Cult|the first cult]] whose blood still carries enough of [[Magurkai]]'s gift to cast.

## Who can become a wizard

Only someone of the magic bloodline can become a wizard (see [[Bloodline Magic]]). The purer their bloodline, the more iron their blood can hold and the stronger their magic; descendants of a thinned line can't cast at all. Occasionally a dormant trait resurfaces in someone with no recent wizards in the family (see [[Dormant Bloodlines]]).

The older *Runaways* notes add that a wizard's capacity depends on their ancestors' ability, that the most powerful wizards are the children of two wizards, and that each wizard draws magic from one element: air, earth, water or fire.

## Training

The notes say little about how wizards train. In *[[The Runaways]]*, [[Eric]] is first noticed when the wizard [[Aerelm]] visits the resistance and senses the magic in him; Eric then "starts training as a wizard as well" alongside his training as a soldier, and grows his skills quickly over a few weeks. Raw power without control is a theme: Eric "starts out as having raw power and is unable to control emotions".

## The Council of Worldly Wizards

[[Aerelm]], a resistance wizard, sits on the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]]. The notes give no other details of the council.

## In the stories

A spin-off "wizard book" is planned to start in 1072 (see [[The Wizard Book]]). In the slave book, Eric meets [[Peter Snyder]] and the slave hunters and introduces himself as a wizard.
