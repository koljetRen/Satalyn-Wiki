**The first cult** were the 100 people of one village to whom [[Magurkai]], God of Magic, gave magic in person during [[The Golden Age|phase six]] of creation; every wizard descends from them.

## The gift

Magic was given to "its first cult" in phase six. Magurkai personally visited a small cult — "100 people from a village" — and gave them magic. This was a rare event: the gods are distant and rarely act on the world in person. He changed their bodies to store and produce more iron, since they cast by drawing on the iron in their blood. Only they and their descendants can wield magic (see [[Bloodline Magic]]).

> [!info] Canon note
> The older *Runaways* notes (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`) say the first wizards got their magic "from worshiping him" — through worship rather than a visit. The 2026 notes describe a personal visit; the two could both be true (a cult that worshipped him).

## The holy site

The place of Magurkai's visit is a holy site, possibly with a city that grew up around it (see [[Magic Holy Site]]). It is on the map's to-do list.
