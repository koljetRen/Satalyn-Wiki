**Overcasting** is casting more magic than a wizard's blood can sustain: it drains the iron that magic draws on and can cause fainting or death.

## What it does

Wizards cast by drawing on the iron in their blood (see [[Bloodline Magic]]). Overcasting drains that iron, and the result can be fainting or death. The notes count it as one of the two flaws of [[Magurkai]]'s gift; the other is the split between the magic-born and everyone else.

## Recovery

The 2026 notes don't say how iron is restored. The older *Runaways* rules say magical capacity is replenished by resting physically and mentally; this may still apply.

## Open questions

- How much casting is too much, and does it differ with the purity of the bloodline?
- Is recovery by rest (older notes) the way iron is restored?
