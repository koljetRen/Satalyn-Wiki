**Magic** in Satalyn is a gift of [[Magurkai]], the God of Magic, who gave it to a single village cult in the golden age; it passes down only by bloodline, and casters draw on the iron in their own blood to wield it.

## Where magic comes from

Magic was one of the gifts of phase six of creation, when [[Lebarka]], the Father of All, gathered the requests of [[The Nine]] and brought them to [[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, who made them real. In that phase, magic was "given to its first cult". [[Magurkai]] personally visited a small cult — 100 people from a village — and gave them magic. This was a rare event, since the gods are distant and seldom act in person. The site of that visit is a holy site ([[Magic Holy Site]]), possibly with a city that grew up around it.

## How it works

- **Iron in the blood.** Casters draw on the iron in their blood to cast. Magurkai changed the bodies of the first 100 so they could store and produce more iron. See [[Bloodline Magic]].
- **Bloodline only.** Only the first cult and their descendants can wield magic. The purer the bloodline, the more iron the blood can hold, and the greater the magic. As a line thins, magic weakens until descendants can't cast at all.
- **Dormant traits.** Rarely, a trait that has lain dormant resurfaces. See [[Dormant Bloodlines]].
- **Cost.** Overcasting drains iron and can cause fainting or death. See [[Overcasting]].

> [!info] Canon note
> An older set of magic rules in the *Runaways* notes (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`, "Runaways extra stuff → Magic") differs in its details. It says:
> - the first wizards got their magic from worshipping the god of magic;
> - magic wielders are determined "by dna", and each has a capacity to use magic based on their ancestors' ability;
> - the most powerful wizards are the children of two wizards, but every so often an "odd ball" appears with a powerful, previously dormant strand (Eric, Dherlin);
> - magical capacity is replenished by resting physically and mentally;
> - each wielder draws their magic from one specific element: air, earth, water or fire.
>
> The 2026 iron-in-the-blood model (`sources/claude-doc/world-creation-notes.md`) is used above. The two may be compatible — inherited "DNA" capacity matches the bloodline idea, and rest might be how iron is restored — so the older details (rest, the four elements, children of two wizards) may still hold. The author hasn't ruled on this.

## Magic and society

Magurkai's gift has a social flaw: the bloodline splits the world into the magic-born and everyone else. The notes list story hooks that grow from this:

- noble houses guarding their lineage;
- forbidden marriages;
- faked ancestry;
- resentment of the magic-born.

At least one place bans magic: see [[Magic Bans]]. The resistance wizard [[Aerelm]] sits on the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]] (see [[Wizards]]).

## Magic in the modern day

Because his bloodline has spread so widely, the god of magic may now have the most influence of all the gods. He went from touching one tiny cult to being the most present god. A possible hook: this reversal could stir up jealousy among the Nine.

## Magic in the stories

In *[[The Runaways]]*, [[Eric]] and [[Jane Barker]] are both wizards as well as fighters, and the resistance wizard [[Aerelm]] senses the magic in Eric. The evil wizard [[Dherlin]] is the series' great enemy. A later spin-off, the [[The Wizard Book|wizard book]], is planned to start in 1072.

## Ideas and open threads

- The first cult may have been an ice age people, which would put the oldest bloodlines in that era (see [[The Ice Age People]]).
- Each wielder's magic may come from one of four elements (older notes; see the canon note above).

## Open questions

- Where is the holy site of Magurkai's visit, and did a city grow up around it? (The map's to-do list includes "the magic holy site".)
- Do the older rules (rest, the four elements) still apply alongside the iron model?
- Do the Xi have magic? The *Runaways* notes call Eric "the first ever with xi ancestry to control magic".

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Feats of magic in the *Runaways* plots (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`):
> - **Book 1:** Eric and Jane dream about Dherlin while sleeping in the Aramore Forest. At [[Battle of Misty Phlats|Misty Phlats]], Eric "goes full avatar state mode and fireballs the whole valley", putting himself into a death-like state; Jane later dreams of Eric overlooking the valley and asks him to come back to the land of the living, and he wakes.
> - **Book 2:** Eric and Jane use their magic together to hide eight resistance members at the [[Battle of the Fort|fort]]. Eric uses magic to send a message to a ship on the horizon.
> - **Book 3:** Eric uses magic to visit Jane in a dream and tell her where he is (Sprake, Dyken). He uses magic to proclaim a loud "stop fighting" at Carterville, to disarm Carter, to sense the evil presence in him, and then "a magic he's never heard of before" to destroy Dherlin's presence.
> - Dherlin cast a spell on King Carter so he could possess him whenever he wanted (plot-twist idea). Later Dherlin tries to curse Jane's unborn child; the curse (to die quickly) hits Scott instead.
> - Eric killed his mother through uncontrolled magic, which is why he was put in prison.
