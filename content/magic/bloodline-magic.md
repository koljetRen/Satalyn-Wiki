**Bloodline magic** is the rule that magic in Satalyn can only be wielded by descendants of the 100 villagers [[Magurkai]] first gifted, who cast by drawing on the iron in their blood.

## The iron in the blood

When Magurkai, the God of Magic, gave magic to [[The First Cult|the first cult]], he made it so they draw on the iron in their blood to cast. To make this possible he changed their bodies to store and produce more iron.

- The purer the bloodline, the more iron the blood can hold, and so the greater the magic.
- Casting uses iron up: [[Overcasting]] drains it and can cause fainting or death.

## Inheritance

Only the first 100 and their descendants can wield magic. As the line thins, magic weakens, until descendants can't cast at all. Rarely, a dormant trait resurfaces in a later generation (see [[Dormant Bloodlines]]).

Since the golden age the bloodline has spread so widely that Magurkai, once the god of one tiny cult, may now be the most present god in the world.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older *Runaways* notes (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`) describe inheritance in terms of "dna": each wielder has a capacity based on their ancestors' ability to use magic, and the most powerful wizards are the children of two wizards. This fits the 2026 bloodline model and may still be true. The same notes say capacity is replenished by physical and mental rest, and that each wielder's magic comes from one element (air, earth, water or fire); the 2026 notes don't mention either.

## Consequences

The bloodline splits the world into the magic-born and everyone else — one of the hidden flaws of Magurkai's gift. Story hooks named in the notes: noble houses guarding their lineage, forbidden marriages, faked ancestry, and resentment of the magic-born.

## Ideas and open threads

- The first cult may have been an ice age people, which would put the oldest bloodlines in that era.

## Open questions

- How is iron restored after casting? (Older notes: by rest.)
- Can anyone tell how pure a person's bloodline is, or sense it? (In *The Runaways*, the wizard [[Aerelm]] "senses the magic" in [[Eric]].)
