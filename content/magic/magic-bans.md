**Magic bans** are laws forbidding magic; the only one in the notes is the ban on magic in Aerelm, from the author's D&D campaign.

## The Aerelm ban

A note from a D&D session says: "Magic is banned from Aerelm because the King is worried about power."

The note doesn't say which king, when the ban began, or how it is enforced.

> [!info] Canon note
> "Aerelm" is also the name of a resistance wizard in *The Runaways* — a member of the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]] (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md`). In the D&D note, "banned from Aerelm" reads like a place. The two have not been merged; see [[Aerelm]] for the wizard.

## Related

Magic follows bloodlines and splits the world into the magic-born and everyone else; the notes name "resentment of the magic-born" as a story hook (see [[Bloodline Magic]]).
