## Personality

Everyone whispers about him because of his past, although no one actually knows what he did to get thrown in prison.

## Role in the story

Stigr is one of the 14 prisoners [[Eric]] meets in the dungeon at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Stigr still appears in the rewrite is not settled.

- Shares cell 5 with [[Godfrey]].
