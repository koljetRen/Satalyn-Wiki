**Lucas** is a dual-sword-wielding soldier of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]] who becomes good friends with [[Eric]], and who carries a [[xiramite]] dagger from his mother.

## Appearance

By the time of the escape from the [[island prison]] his face has "started to grow scruffy".

## Personality

Lucas is good-humoured and says what's on his mind. Around a campfire after the escape from the island, he announces that he hates the texture of sand: it seems like it should be smooth, but it is just small rocks that grind your feet away. Eric teases him about it, and Lucas laughs along. After a deadly battle he is the one who breaks the silence: "What are we going to do now?"

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** Eric meets and befriends Lucas when he starts training as a soldier at the resistance base. At the battle at [[Misty Phlats]], Lucas, [[Jaek]] and [[Henri]] pull Eric's body away and carry it to [[Mourncliff]].
- **Book 2:** he is one of the few Eric and Jane hide with magic at the fort. In [[Quino]], when the Xi warrior [[Rykor]] wants to take only Eric, Lucas shows his dagger and the warrior yields. On [[Xi]] he is taught how to connect to xiramite, and over one to two months his dagger becomes connected to him.
- **Book 3:** when the Xi won't follow [[Jane Barker]], Lucas becomes her voice in leading the Xi and resistance army.

## Xiramite dagger

Lucas's dagger was a gift from his mother; "She said to reveal it only when it was necessary." It never cracked, because it never formed a true connection with him and stayed raw. Not knowing why, Lucas notices Eric's sword cracking and worries that it might be destroyed. When he shows the dagger, the Xi warrior says: "You have xiramite as well, but no connection."

## Relationships

- **[[Eric]]** — close friend.
- **[[Bella]]** — his girlfriend.
- **His mother** — gave him the dagger (see Secrets for the family tree).

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Lucas is Eric's half cousin.** In the 2025 family tree, Lucas's mother is the half sibling of [[Brian]] (Eric's father) through Brian's Xi-descended mother. In the older version, Lucas's and Eric's mothers were twin sisters of an Aramorean line with Xi blood, separated early in life; Lucas was the elder twin's child (see [[Eric]]).
> - At the meeting after the battle of Carthollow, [[King Augo]] tries to stab [[King Carter]], but Lucas cuts Augo's hand off first.
> - He is in the group that sets out for [[Averill]] to find Dherlin.
