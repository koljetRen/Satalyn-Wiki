**Samheir** (Sam) is the top archer of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]] and a 1st level resistance leader.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 6'1" |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]] Book 1 |
| Affiliation | [[The Resistance]] |
| Rank / role | Top archer; 1st level resistance leader (commander) |

> [!info] Canon note
> The cast list names him **Sam**, with "[Samhier]" as a more medieval replacement name; the Book 1 plot spells it **Samheir**.

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** [[Eric]] and his companions meet him at the inn in [[Misty Phlats]] (with [[Crisig]]), and he takes them to the resistance base. In the escape from Misty Phlats, Samheir leads the frontal charge.

## Ideas and open threads

- A D&D note gives a similar role to [[Brotz]], "a resistance member in the town, who guides them to the resistance base". It is not said whether they are the same person.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - The 2021 leadership doc lists him as "Sam (dead)".
