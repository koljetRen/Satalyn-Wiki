**King Augo** is the king of [[Keyesport Vale]] in [[The Runaways]], who claims to be the rightful ruler of the throne [[King Carter]] sits on.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 5'10" |

## Personality

Augo will do whatever he wants to regain the throne he claims. The notes add that "the resistance is part bad guys".

## Role in the story

At the end of Book 1 the resistance sails from [[Moonwick]] to Keyesport Vale. The Book 2 blurb calls it the "home of King [Argo?]", the author's own query on the name.

> [!info] Canon note
> The cast list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md) calls Augo simply "King of Keyesport Vale". The character-development notes in sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md call him the rightful ruler of Carter's throne; the full background of that claim is a plot twist (see Secrets). The Book 2 blurb (sources/drive/runaways/book-2-stomped-in-the-dust.md) writes "King [Argo?]".

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Fillyolia twist:** Augo and Carter were brothers in line for the throne of [[Fillyolia]], their father being the former king's younger brother. When [[Dherlin]] killed the king and his family, Augo became rightful ruler but declined, being used to an easy noble life, and passed the throne to Carter. Seeing Carter's growing power, Augo became greedy, started rumours of a resistance, and accepted [[Zandar]]'s offer to lead it. Together they spread the story that Carter had stolen the throne from Augo.
> - Zandar secretly meant to kill Augo once the throne was won.
> - At the meeting after the battle of Carthollow, Augo tries to stab Carter, but [[Lucas]] cuts his hand off first. Augo is thrown in prison.
