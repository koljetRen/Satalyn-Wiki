**Cassandra Smith** is the owner of the [[Harrisonville]] inn in [[The Runaways]], a woman "with a dark side", and the wife of [[Willy Smith]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 5'2" |

## Role in the story

Like her husband, she is plot-important because she helps reveal more of [[David]]'s backstory, "but nothing more". In the rewritten Book 1, [[Eric]] and David try to lodge at the inn but don't have enough money and are turned out onto the streets.

> [!info] Canon note
> Both character lists give "Maly Teray" as her more medieval name. The Book 1 rewrite notes say to "rewrite the whole Harrisonville subplot".
