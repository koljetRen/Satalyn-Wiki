**Dherlin** is the evil wizard of [[The Runaways]], who is thought to be dead.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 6'2" |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]]; dreams in Book 1 and Book 3 |
| Rank / role | Evil wizard |

## Background

- In the older magic notes, Dherlin is, like [[Eric]], an "odd ball" with a powerful, previously dormant magical strand, rather than the child of two wizards (see [[Bloodline magic]]).
- The timeline says an evil wizard started attacking the Great City in 1045 (see [[Dherlin's attacks on the Great City]]).
- In [[Aramore]], the auroras that people took as the Skyfather's blessing haven't been seen since "the desolation of the ground water from the evil wizard" (see [[Desolation of the ground water]]).

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** while sleeping in the [[Aramore Forest]], Eric and [[Jane Barker]] dream about Dherlin. The author's list of pictured sites includes a "Dherlin Dream".
- **Book 3:** Eric and Jane dream of him again (see Secrets).

## Open questions

- Is the evil wizard of the 1045 attacks and of the desolation of the ground water always Dherlin? The notes say "evil wizard" in those places.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - Dherlin attacked [[Fillyolia]] and killed the king and his entire family (the Fillyolia plot twist).
> - He cast a spell on [[King Carter]] to possess him whenever he wanted, causing Carter's mood swings.
> - At the end of Book 3, Eric's dream carries Dherlin's threat that he will kill Eric; Eric wakes to see Carter over him with a dagger, senses the evil presence in Carter and destroys it with a magic he has never heard of. Eric and his friends then set out to find Dherlin, starting at [[Averill]].
> - During that hunt Dherlin learns Jane is pregnant and tries to curse the child; [[Scott Barker]] takes the curse and dies. Eric and the others finally defeat Dherlin while Jane waits at [[Lucal]].
