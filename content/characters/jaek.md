**Jaek** is a resistance leader in [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]] and the younger brother of [[Kaz]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 5'8" |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]] (Books 1–3) |
| Affiliation | [[The Resistance]] |
| Rank / role | Lieutenant |

> [!info] Canon note
> The cast list calls Jaek a "3rd level resistance leader (lieutenant)", but the 3rd level is the sergeants; the 2021 leadership doc lists Jaek at the 2nd level (lieutenant).

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** at the battle at [[Misty Phlats]], Jaek, [[Lucas]] and [[Henri]] pull [[Eric]]'s body away and carry it to [[Mourncliff]].
- **Book 2:** he is one of the few Eric and Jane hide with magic at the fort.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - He is in the group that sets out for [[Averill]] to find Dherlin at the end of Book 3.
