**Eric** is the protagonist of [[The Runaways]], an [[Aramore|Aramorean]] warrior and wizard who wakes up in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] with no memory and rises to become one of the leaders of [[The Resistance]].

## Appearance

Eric has brown hair and a chipped front tooth.

## Personality

Eric starts out with raw power and is unable to control his emotions. After the battle at [[Misty Phlats]] he suffers from PTSD and nightmares about it, and he later has flashbacks from the time before he woke up in the dungeon. He is loyal to his friends: when a [[Xi warriors|Xi warrior]] offers to take him alone, he refuses to leave them behind ("Either we all come with you or none of us"). He jokes with [[Lucas]] about Lucas's hatred of sand.

## Background

Eric lived in [[Carthollow]], the home of [[King Carter]], and was arrested there. He wakes up in the city's dungeon with no memory of his life before. His mother was known for always caring for the less fortunate (see [[Eric's mother]]), and in one draft passage his father is described as a war veteran, "one of the best".

Eric carries [[Xi]] blood, which is why he can connect to [[xiramite]]. His magic comes from what the older notes call a powerful, previously dormant strand in his ancestry, the same rare case as [[Dherlin]] (see [[Bloodline magic]]).

## Role in the story

### Book 1

- Eric wakes up in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] with no memory. He meets [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Ulrich]], [[The Torturer|the torturer]] and [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], in that order, and learns of [[The King's Trial]]. Ulrich is the guard who kicks him.
- He fights in the King's Trial and reaches the third round before being defeated. The tournament is what brings him to [[Brian]]'s attention.
- Brian frees him and David and tells them to run to [[Harrisonville]], giving them a little money for provisions. They hide from [[King Carter's forces]] in a small cave in a forest.
- In Harrisonville they meet [[Scott Barker]], who leads them to [[Joseph Barker]] and [[Jane Barker]]. When the army comes for them they flee through the [[Aramore Forest]], where Eric and Jane dream about [[Dherlin]], to [[Misty Phlats]] (meeting [[Crisig]] and [[Samheir]]) and on to the resistance base, where they meet [[Zandar]] and [[Elliott]].
- Eric trains as a soldier and befriends [[Lucas]]. When the wizard [[Aerelm]] visits the base, he senses the magic in Eric, and Eric begins training as a wizard as well. He grows his skills "super well" over a few weeks.
- He and Jane build their friendship in the mountains. After a scouting and weapon-buying trip to [[Shadow's Edge]], Eric is led to a pocket of [[xiramite]] and uses it to make his sword and a dagger. [[Shetien]] identifies the stone as coming from the age of the [[Xi Empire]].
- In the battle at [[Misty Phlats]], Eric "goes full avatar state mode" and fireballs the whole valley, putting himself into a death-like state. Lucas, [[Jaek]] and [[Henri]] carry his body to the meeting point at [[Mourncliff]]. Some of the resistance cheer what he did; others are upset that he killed many resistance men as well. After a few days Jane dreams of him overlooking the valley and asks him to come back to the land of the living, and later that day he wakes.

> [!info] Canon note
> Most of the Book 1 outline above follows the "Book 1 rewritten plot" in sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md. The newer 2026 sheet (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md) changes parts of the dungeon chapters; the details are in the Secrets callout.

### Book 2

- After the resistance reaches [[Keyesport Vale]] and [[King Carter's forces]] take it, Eric — "now one of the leaders in the resistance" — must find a way off the island and restart the force. Along the way he learns more about who he was before the dungeon and about his potential.
- Eric and Jane use their magic together to hide a few resistance members. Eric later uses magic to send a message to a passing ship, and the survivors sail south to [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]].
- There a Xi warrior, [[Rykor]], tries to kill them for carrying xiramite, but yields after seeing Eric wield his fully cracked sword. The group is offered sanctuary on [[Xi]].
- Eric gains favour with the Xi as a wizard who can wield xiramite: the first person of Xi ancestry ever to control magic. He leads an expedition to the [[Southern Misty Mountains|Southern Misty Peaks]] to bring back the weapons from the holding trees and the old resistance base, then leads a Xi force to the [[island prison]] to free resistance prisoners.

## Relationships

- **[[Jane Barker]]** — his closest friend. The rewrite notes say to write them as best friends rather than as siblings; their relationship deepens over the series.
- **[[Lucas]]** — good friend from training.
- **[[David]]** — his cellmate in the dungeon and fellow runaway.
- **[[Bardon]] and [[Akio Stormshark]]** — fellow prisoners. In the newest notes, Bardon and Eric become a trusted pair in the resistance, and Akio travels with them.
- **[[Brian]]** — the resistance spy who sets him free.
- **[[Eric's mother]]** — see her page.

Eric and Lucas both carry [[Xi]] blood, which is why both can bond with [[xiramite]]. How they are related is a plot secret (see below).

## Ideas and open threads

- **Xiramite connection:** for Eric (not to be explained in Book 1, maybe Book 2 or 3), his mind was learning from the xiramite while he was unconscious after Misty Phlats, which formed his connection with the sword. Lucas notices the sword cracking and worries it will be destroyed, because his own dagger never cracked.
- **Draft scenes:** in "The Gladiator Battle", Eric fights a black, possessed figure in an arena; his xiramite blade pulses and shatters his opponent's steel sword. In another passage he finds hundreds of tiny arrowhead-shaped rocks in a perfect circle of stone in the grass, and says to Lucas, "This is what we are looking for." See [[Draft passages]].
- Book 1 rewrite edits: draw out the time in prison; don't have Brian come with Eric and David, just let them out; follow a river by boat from Harrisonville to the Southern Misty Mountains.
- A heading "Eric's Depression" exists in the notes but is empty.
- Character list note: "change name to make it more medieval".

## Open questions

- Eric makes "his sword and a dagger" from the xiramite pocket, but Lucas's xiramite dagger was a gift from Lucas's mother. Is Eric's dagger a separate weapon, and what happens to it?
- Which family tree is canon (see Secrets)?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Family tree.** Eric and Lucas are related, and both have Xi blood. Two versions:
>   - *Newer (2025, sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-ideas-personal.md):* Eric and Lucas are **half cousins**. Brian's mother had Xi ancestors. Brian's father took Brian away from his mother shortly after his birth; she then had another child with someone else, and that half sibling of Brian is Lucas's mother.
>   - *Older (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md):* during the Xi Empire, a Xi soldier broke the rule against mating with foreigners and had a child with an Aramorean woman. Her descendants struggled to keep children alive, and the line survived with only one surviving child each generation, until a woman had twin girls who both lived. The twins were separated early in life and each had a single child: Lucas to the elder, Eric to the younger.
>   - The main text follows neither in detail; the 2025 note is newer by date, but the CLAUDE.md source order ranks *Runaways ideas* higher. Logged for the author.
>
> ```
> Newer version (2025)
>
>   Brian's father ── Brian's mother (Xi ancestry) ── another man
>                  │                                │
>                Brian                  half sibling (Lucas's mother)
>                  │                                │
>                Eric  ········ half cousins ······ Lucas
> ```
>
> - **Brian is Eric's father**, though neither of them knows it until Book 3 (Book 3 plot step 14). Brian senses a connection between himself and Eric, which is part of why he risks his life to free him.
> - **Eric killed his mother** with uncontrolled magic, and that is why he was put in prison. He learns this in Book 3 through nightmarish flashbacks, and it deepens his depression; when the flashback comes he wants nothing to do with anyone.
> - **End of Book 2:** in the attack on the island prison Eric is captured. He is thrown in a dark cell, becomes deeply depressed, and starts having flashbacks of his life before the dungeon.
> - **Book 3:** [[Crisig]] finds and frees him and leads him to [[Brian]], [[Kolt]] and [[Henri]]; the five escape. Eric uses magic to visit Jane in a dream and tell her they are in [[Sprake]], [[Dyken]], and she brings a small group to get them. Jane finds him overlooking the ocean, comforts him, and they share a kiss — the start of their romance.
> - In the battle of Carterville, [[Zandar]] tries to kill Eric; [[David]] sacrifices himself to save him and [[Elliott]] kills Zandar. Eric uses magic to proclaim a loud stop to the fighting.
> - Afterwards Eric and Jane dream of Dherlin; Eric's dream carries a threat that Dherlin will kill him, and he wakes to see [[King Carter]] standing over him with a dagger. Eric disarms Carter with magic, senses the evil presence in him, and uses a magic he has never heard of to destroy Dherlin's presence. He then sets out for [[Averill]] with Jane, Lucas, Scott, Jaek, Crisig, Crewt, Rykor and Liko to hunt Dherlin.
> - Eric and Jane marry after the battle of Carterville and before leaving for Averill. When Jane can no longer travel, she stays at [[Lucal]] while Eric goes on to defeat Dherlin. Their son, born as they leave Lucal, is named Scott after Jane's brother. After a few years in Lucal they return to Carterville to live out their lives.
> - **Newest notes (2026):** in the dungeon Eric watches [[David]] get beaten to death in a cafeteria skirmish (David "dies in chapter 1"), and [[Bardon]], who was captured intentionally, teams up with [[Rykor]] to break Eric and [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]] out. This replaces the older version in which Brian frees Eric and David together.
