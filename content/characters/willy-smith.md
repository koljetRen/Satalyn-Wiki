**Willy Smith** is the kind and accepting mayor of [[Harrisonville]] in [[The Runaways]], and the husband of [[Cassandra Smith]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 6'2" |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]] Book 1 |
| Rank / role | Mayor of Harrisonville (cast list); owner of the tavern in Harrisonville (character notes) |

## Role in the story

Willy is plot-important because he helps reveal more of [[David]]'s backstory, "but nothing more".

> [!info] Canon note
> The cast list calls him the mayor of Harrisonville; the character-development notes call him the owner of the tavern there (both in older sources). Both lists give "Giles Teray" as his more medieval name. The Book 1 rewrite notes say to "rewrite the whole Harrisonville subplot".
