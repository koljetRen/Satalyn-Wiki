**Akio Stormshark** is an [[Osisu|Osisuan]] fisherman turned mercenary with a drinking problem, a prisoner in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in [[The Runaways]] who becomes a key member of [[The Resistance]].

## Appearance

Akio has long black hair worn in a loose braid and golden-brown skin. Flowing tattoos run along his arms and chest. He has several scars across his face and is missing a piece of his top left ear.

## Personality

Fearless (his mood in the prison roster).

## Background

Akio was a fisherman before becoming a mercenary. His surname follows the Osisuan custom of taking a last name when choosing an expertise: a fisherman may be named Stormshark after one of the region's sharks (see [[Stormshark]]). He was caught while drunk. He has been in this prison for a few months, but has experience in [[The King's Trial|the King's Trials]] from other prisons.

## Role in the story

Akio is the last of the dungeon's people [[Eric]] meets, after [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer|the torturer]]. He travels alongside Eric and Bardon and becomes a key member of the resistance. The older roster says he joins the resistance in Book 2.
