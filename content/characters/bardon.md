**Bardon** is a prisoner in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in [[The Runaways]] who travels with [[Eric]] and becomes one of his most trusted companions in [[The Resistance]].

## Appearance

Bardon has shaggy black hair, a scruffy black beard and a scar above his left eye.

## Personality

Proud (his mood in the prison roster).

## Background

In the 2024 roster, Bardon is a former soldier who was charged with treason.

## Role in the story

Bardon is the third person Eric meets in the dungeon, after [[David]] and [[Rannulfer|Rann]]. He travels alongside Eric, and the two become a trusted pair in the resistance. The older roster says he joins the resistance in Book 2.

> [!info] Canon note
> The 2024 roster (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-background-characters.md) makes him a former soldier charged with treason who joins the resistance in Book 2. The 2026 sheet (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md) gives him a different reason for being in the dungeon (see Secrets).

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **2026 version:** Bardon is a resistance member who was captured intentionally. He teams up with [[Rykor]] to break Eric and [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]] out of the dungeon.
