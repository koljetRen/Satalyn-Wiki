The **Xi warriors** are the fighting men of [[Xi]] who shelter and train [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]]; three of them, [[Liko]], [[Rykor]] and [[Rhao]], join the cast as "new Xi".

## Who they are

- Every Xi man trains for war from the age of 8, after team-building exercises from the age of 3, and strives to bring honour to his family through military might. Adults watch men duel for sport when not at war. (See [[Xi]] for the culture.)
- In war they wear a pauldron spanning both shoulders and the upper chest, a Spartan-style helmet, and thick leather on arms and legs, leaving chest and stomach bare.
- Their **shield wall** is "infamous" and "impenetrable".

## Role in the story

- **Book 2:** a Xi warrior (Rykor) confronts [[Eric]]'s group in [[Quino]] for carrying [[xiramite]]. The group is offered sanctuary on Xi for possessing, and being connected to, xiramite. They learn about the Xi people and how they fight, and Eric gains favour as the first person of Xi ancestry to control magic. Eric gathers a Xi force to attack the [[island prison]].
- **Book 3:** resistance soldiers continue to learn to fight like the Xi. [[Jane Barker]] tries to lead the Xi forces, but they won't follow her, so [[Lucas]] becomes her voice.
- The author's site slides include a "Xi Training arena dream".
