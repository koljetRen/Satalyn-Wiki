> [!info] Canon note
> Spelled **Xander** in the cast list, the 2021 leadership doc, the sites slides and the plot-twist note; **Zandar** in the Book 1 and Book 3 plots and the character-development notes (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md). The wiki uses Zandar, the spelling of the higher-ranked source.

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** [[Samheir]] brings [[Eric]] and his companions to the resistance base, where they meet Zandar and [[Elliott]]. When the army reaches [[Misty Phlats]], the escape plan has Samheir lead a frontal charge while Zandar and Elliott come in from the side to distract the main army, so the rest of the resistance can escape through the back passes.
- He presents himself as a good man trying to do what is right.
- The author's site slides include a "View outside resistance base/Xander".

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Zandar's betrayal:** he is "a nobody" who leads the resistance because he wants power. In the Fillyolia twist, he came to [[King Augo]] and offered to lead the resistance and make it real, in return for becoming Augo's second-hand man, intending to kill Augo once the throne was won. Together they spread the story that Carter had stolen Augo's throne.
> - In the battle of Carterville, seeing the battle partly in his favour, he decides to take power, but sees Eric in his path first. He moves to kill Eric; [[David]] sacrifices himself to save Eric, and [[Elliott]] kills Zandar in Eric's defence. The character notes say he tries to kill both Augo and Carter, revealing who he really is, and is killed "by Tahir" (Elliott's alternate name).
