**Skari** is a guard at the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in *[[The Runaways, Book 1]]*: a noble whom the civilians love.

## Personality

Skari is a noble who thinks too much of himself and whom all the civilians love. He doesn't really care about anything.

## Role in the story

Skari is one of the eight guards of the prison where [[Eric]] wakes up at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Skari still appears in the rewrite is not settled.
