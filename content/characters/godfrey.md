## Personality

The guards' favourite. He does what people ask as long as it doesn't get him in trouble.

## Role in the story

Godfrey is one of the 14 prisoners [[Eric]] meets in the dungeon at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Godfrey still appears in the rewrite is not settled.
