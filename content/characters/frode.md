**Frode** is a guard at the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in *[[The Runaways, Book 1]]*: a farmer stuck on guard duty.

| | |

| Quirk | Reluctantly corrupt |

## Personality

Frode just wants to go home to his farm, but instead is stuck watching prisoners.

## Role in the story

Frode is one of the eight guards of the prison where [[Eric]] wakes up at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Frode still appears in the rewrite is not settled.

- Culture, appearance and fate are not in the notes.
