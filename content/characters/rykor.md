**Rykor** is a [[Xi people|Xi]] swordsman in [[The Runaways]], the Xi warrior who confronts [[Eric]]'s group in [[Quino]] over their [[xiramite]] and later fights alongside [[The Resistance]].

## Role in the story

- **Book 2:** in Quino, a Xi warrior (Rykor) tries to kill the group for possessing xiramite. When he sees Eric wield his fully cracked sword, he yields. He tries to take only Eric — "You are Xi, so you must come with me" — but Eric won't leave his friends. When [[Lucas]] shows his dagger, the warrior yields again, and they all travel west to Xi. He speaks in broken English at first.
- A draft battle passage shows Rykor's fighting style: with a circular shield held to his chest and sword just behind it, he waits for an attacker, knocks the sword from his hand with a blow of the shield to the wrist, and runs him through below the ribs. Behind him stands "the infamous Xi shield wall, which was impenetrable".

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - In the 2026 notes, [[Bardon]] teams up with Rykor to break Eric and [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]] out of the [[Carthollow Dungeon]].
> - He is in the group that sets out for [[Averill]] to find Dherlin at the end of Book 3.
