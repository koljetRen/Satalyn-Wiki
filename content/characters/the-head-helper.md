**The head helper** is the leading member of the royal council that helps the young king [[Peter Snyder]] rule in *[[The Slave Book]]*. The notes give him no name.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Appears in | [[The Slave Book]] |
| Rank / role | Head of the king's council |

## Background

When Peter becomes king at 15, he is given a council to help him rule. The council of his father, [[King William Snyder VII]], had 22 members, from people in their early 20s to people older than Peter's grandfather would have been.

## Open questions

- He has no name in the notes.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> After Peter has been king for a little while, the head helper wants the power for himself. He kidnaps Peter in his sleep and hides him away, then makes people think Peter ran away. Because there are no more heirs, he takes the king's place. Peter is beaten and given to slavers. (2024 plot outline.)
>
> The 2021 premise tells the same story more briefly: "Someone on council plots against him", the boy king is kidnapped in the night, the council member takes control of the kingdom, and the boy is sold to slavers crossing the [[Great Desert]].
