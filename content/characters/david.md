**David** is [[Eric]]'s cellmate in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] and the first named character Eric meets in [[The Runaways]], a man who "will do whatever it takes to clear his name".

| Rank / role | Prisoner; runaway with Eric |

## Appearance

David has long, light brown hair and a scarred, rugged face.

## Personality

David starts out stuck up, caring only about himself, and grows humble over the story.

## Background

[[Willy Smith]] and [[Cassandra Smith]] of [[Harrisonville]] help reveal more of David's backstory. The notes don't yet say what it is.

## Role in the story

- He is Eric's roommate in the dungeon and the first person Eric meets after waking.
- In the rewritten Book 1 plot, [[Brian]] frees the two of them and they run to Harrisonville together. When they can't afford the inn, David steals money from people and gets the guard called on him. They then meet [[Scott Barker]], travel with the Barkers to the resistance base, and David trains as a soldier alongside Eric.
- The rewrite notes say to "include David more once they make it to the resistance".

> [!info] Canon note
> The newest notes (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, 2026) give David a much shorter role than the older plot outlines in sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md. Details are in the Secrets callout.

## Open questions

- What is David's backstory, and what name is he trying to clear?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Newest version (2026):** David "dies in chapter 1": Eric watches him get beaten to death in a cafeteria skirmish in the dungeon.
> - **Older version (runaways-ideas.md):** David betrays the resistance. While at [[Shadow's Edge]] he sneaks off and sends a report to the King about the resistance base. At the fort in Book 2, Eric sees him alongside the enemy soldiers. David becomes a servant to [[King Carter]] because of his willingness to give him information about the resistance. In Book 3 he comes out of Carterville as a messenger, and the resistance gives him a message for the king. In the final battle, when [[Zandar]] moves to kill Eric, David sacrifices himself to save him. His arc runs from selfish to humble, ending in "giving his life to save Eric's in the final battle".
