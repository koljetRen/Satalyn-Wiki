**Eric's mother** is the unnamed mother of [[Eric]] in [[The Runaways]], known for always caring for others.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]] (in Eric's flashbacks and backstory) |

## Personality

She could always be found around people less fortunate than herself. Whenever she passed someone sitting by the road she would stop and give them something — food, money or something else. When asked why, she said it was her duty: her husband was a war veteran, "one of the best", and she felt that while he kept the nation safe, she should help keep individual people safe and cared for. She helped with a positive attitude, never as if she were required to.

## Open questions

- Her name is not given.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Eric killed his mother** with uncontrolled magic, and that is why he was put in prison. He learns this in Book 3 through flashbacks.
> - In the older family tree (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md), she is the younger of two separated twin sisters descended from a Xi soldier and an Aramorean woman, and the elder twin is [[Lucas]]'s mother. In the 2025 tree, Eric's Xi blood comes through his father [[Brian]] instead.
