**William Snyder VIII** is the elder son of [[King William Snyder VII]] and the elder brother of [[Peter Snyder]] in *[[The Slave Book]]*.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Born / died | Dies with his parents, 51 days before Peter's 16th birthday |
| Appears in | [[The Slave Book]] |
| Rank / role | Prince, first heir to the throne |

## Background

As the elder son, William VIII is first heir to the throne, ahead of Peter. He died together with his father and mother; no one knew how. The plot outline says the three of them died in an accident on a trip. With his death Peter, at 15, became king.

> [!info] Canon note
> William VIII appears only in the later *Slave Book* (2024). In the earlier opening draft (2021), Peter is the eldest son and has no elder brother.
