## Role in the story

A draft passage follows Liko into his first real battle: "This wasn't training anymore, this was war." He takes his place in the shield wall, knowing that if even one part of it breaks, everyone behind it will suffer, and braces as the enemy hits the line.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
