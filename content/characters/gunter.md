**Gunter** is a guard at the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in *[[The Runaways, Book 1]]*: a timid guard.

## Personality

Gunter is timid; the author's "quirk" for him is *woodsperson*.

## Role in the story

Gunter is one of the eight guards of the prison where [[Eric]] wakes up at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Gunter still appears in the rewrite is not settled.

## Open questions

- Culture, appearance and fate are not in the notes.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Gunter is a double agent along with [[Brian]], and works with Brian to free prisoners. (2024 background-character list.)

> Canon note: in the older Book 1 plot, Brian sets [[Eric]] and [[David]] free from the prison; in the newer 2026 plot notes, [[Bardon]] teams up with [[Rykor]] to break Eric and Akio out. Whether Gunter still has this role in the rewrite is open.
