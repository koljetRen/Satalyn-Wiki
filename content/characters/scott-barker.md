**Scott Barker** is one of the top scouts of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]], a resistance leader and the older brother of [[Jane Barker]].

> [!info] Canon note
> The cast list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md) makes Scott a 2nd level leader. The older 2021 leadership doc (sources/drive/runaways/resistance-leadership-personal.md) lists him at the 1st level (commander).

## Personality

Scott wants revenge on [[King Carter]] for what happened to his family (see [[Jane Barker]]). He sees Jane as a little girl and won't let her do anything risky. His view changes when he learns that everything [[Lucas]] is doing to lead the [[Xi]] is really coming from Jane.

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** in [[Harrisonville]], Scott recognises [[Eric]] and [[David]] and leads them to Joseph and Jane.
- **Book 2:** he is among the prisoners held on the [[island prison]] (see Secrets).
- The bounty hunter [[James]] is "infamous for not failing a job (unless it involves Scott)".

## Relationships

- **[[Jane Barker]]** — his younger sister, whom he is protective of.
- **[[Joseph Barker]]** — his father.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - Scott is captured; he is one of the prisoners freed when Eric's force attacks the island prison in Book 2.
> - He is in the group that sets out for [[Averill]] to find Dherlin.
> - When [[Dherlin]] tries to curse Jane's unborn child, Scott sacrifices himself and takes the curse instead. The curse is to die quickly, and despite everyone's efforts to cure him, he dies. Jane and Eric name their son Scott after him.
