**Aerelm** is a wizard of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]] who sits on the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 6'2" |
| Appears in | [[The Runaways]] |
| Affiliation | [[The Resistance]]; [[Council of Worldly Wizards]] |
| Rank / role | Resistance wizard; lead command (2021 leadership doc) |

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** Aerelm visits the resistance base and senses the magic in [[Eric]], after which Eric starts training as a wizard as well.
- In a draft passage, the group sits in silence at a campfire after a deadly battle. When [[Lucas]] asks, "What are we going to do now?", Aerelm (spelled Aerlem) finally answers: "Man, if you gotta ask, you'll never know."

> [!info] Canon note
> Spelled **Aerelm** in the cast list and plot, **Aerlem** in the leadership doc and the draft passage. A D&D note says "Magic is banned from Aerelm because the King is worried about power" (sources/drive/notes-from-dnd-about-my-world.md), which reads like a place. It is not clear whether that note means this character; it is not merged here.
