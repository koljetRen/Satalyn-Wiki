**Joseph Barker** is an older spy in [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]], and the father of [[Jane Barker|Jane]] and [[Scott Barker|Scott]].

| Family | [[Jane Barker]] and [[Scott Barker]] (children); his wife and an elder son died when their village was ransacked |

## Background

Joseph's family joined the resistance after his wife and elder son were killed when [[King Carter's forces]] ransacked their village (see [[Jane Barker]]).

## Role in the story

Joseph is only seen in Book 1, but is mentioned a lot throughout the series. In [[Harrisonville]], Scott brings [[Eric]] and [[David]] to him and Jane, and they stay together for a few nights while he helps get them better resources. When the army from Carthollow comes and demands them, Joseph makes the others flee in the night but stays behind himself.

He is one of the few who let Jane be strong and lead.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - The 2021 leadership doc lists him as "(captured)". The characters later find out that he died in prison.
