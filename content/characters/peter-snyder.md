**Peter Snyder** is the protagonist of *[[The Slave Book]]*, a prince who becomes king at 15, is sold into slavery and joins a slave hunter's mission to free slaves.

## Personality

At 15 Peter is enjoying life: it is "the last year before adult responsibilities" hit him. He has become a great swordsman, so his father isn't worried about him in fights, and no one dares fight him anyway because he is royal. After his own enslavement he joins the slave hunters "because he doesn't want others to suffer the same way he did".

## Background

Peter is the second son of King William Snyder VII and so second heir to the throne. His father agreed to counsel him on running the kingdom from his 16th birthday, so that he would be ready to take the throne at 18 "if need be".

Fifty-one days before Peter's 16th birthday, both his parents and his elder brother William VIII died together; no one knew how. Peter was taken out of the last 50 days of his childhood and brought into his father's council room, where 22 councillors sat around a U-shaped table, and was placed in the largest seat at its head. He became king at 15, with the council to help him.

> [!info] Canon note
> The earlier opening draft (*Slave Hunters*, 2021) makes Peter the **eldest** son and heir, with only his parents dying; the later *Slave Book* (2024) makes him the **second** son and adds the elder brother, William VIII. The newer version is used above. The 2021 premise also says simply that he becomes king "due to parents death". The 2024 plot outline says his father, mother and brother died in an accident on a trip "to visit something", while its draft opening says no one knew how they died.

## Role in the story

*[[The Slave Book]]* opens with Peter as a slave in a caravan crossing the [[Great Desert]], and tells how the boy king came to be there in flashbacks. After escaping the caravan he joins the slave hunters. Later he meets [[Eric]] and his companions, and the slave hunters join them.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> **The kidnapping.** After Peter has been king a little while, the head helper of his council wants the power. He kidnaps Peter in his sleep and hides him away, then makes people think Peter ran away; as there are no more heirs, the head helper takes the king's place. Peter wakes, is beaten, and is given to slavers.
>
> **The escape.** In the caravan, Peter and three other slaves escape. One is killed by an arrow; another dies a few hours later. Peter and the remaining man spend time in the desert, and Peter starts to think. Slave hunters find them, but despite their efforts the other slave dies. Peter joins the slave hunters.
>
> **Magic.** Peter starts noticing "some weird things happening around him", and the slave hunters decide to consult a wizard at [[Aegryamis]] the next time they are on the east side of the Great Desert. When they meet Eric, Eric tells him (in the author's joke line) "I'm a wizard Peter", and they set off together on an "epic quest".
>
> Canon note: in the 2021 premise, the other escapee survives, joins the slave hunter with Peter, and they hunt slaves for two or three years before meeting the Runaways group; there the rescuer is a single "slave hunter" on a mission to find and free all slaves. The 2024 outline (used above) has the other slave die and speaks of slave hunters in the plural.
