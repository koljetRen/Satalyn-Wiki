**Shetien** is the historian of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]]. Shetien is mute.

## Role in the story

In Book 1, after [[Eric]] makes his sword and a dagger from a pocket of [[xiramite]], Shetien identifies the stone as coming from the age of the [[Xi Empire]], but doesn't know how it got to the [[Southern Misty Mountains]].
