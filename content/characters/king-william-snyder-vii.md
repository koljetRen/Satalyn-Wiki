**King William Snyder VII** is the king of [[Snyder]] and the father of [[Peter Snyder]], whose sudden death opens *[[The Slave Book]]*.

## Background

William VII had agreed to counsel his son Peter on running the kingdom once Peter turned 16, so that Peter would be ready to take the throne at 18 if need be. He was not worried about Peter in fights, as Peter had become a great swordsman. He ruled with a council that met around a U-shaped table in his council room.

Fifty-one days before Peter's 16th birthday, William VII, his queen and his elder son [[William Snyder VIII]] died together; no one knew how. The 2024 plot outline says they died in an accident while on a trip to visit something.

> [!info] Canon note
> In the earlier opening draft (2021), Peter is William VII's eldest son and only the king and queen die.
