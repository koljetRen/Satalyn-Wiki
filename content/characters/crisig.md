**Crisig** is a scout of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]], the son of the bartender at [[Misty Phlats]] and the brother of [[Crewt]].

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** [[Eric]] and his companions meet him at the inn in Misty Phlats. The rewrite notes say to introduce Crisig (and possibly Crewt) earlier.
- **Book 2:** he is one of the few Eric and Jane hide with magic at the fort. After the attack on the [[island prison]], the resistance finds out Crisig is missing.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Book 3:** Crisig finds and frees Eric from his cell, and leads him to [[Brian]], [[Kolt]] and [[Henri]]; the five escape together.
> - He is in the group that sets out for [[Averill]] to find Dherlin.
