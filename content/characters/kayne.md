**Kayne** is a guard at the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in *[[The Runaways, Book 1]]*: the main captain of the guard.

## Personality

Kayne is the main captain of the guard, who likes to have fun while being serious.

## Role in the story

Kayne is one of the eight guards of the prison where [[Eric]] wakes up at the start of [[The Runaways]]. In the author's plan for the prison scene there are 10 cells, 14 prisoners and 8 guards. The newer plot notes (2026) name only [[Eric]], [[David]], [[Rannulfer|Rann]], [[Bardon]], [[Akio Stormshark|Akio]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer]] in the dungeon scenes, so whether Kayne still appears in the rewrite is not settled.

## Open questions

- Kayne is the "main captain of guard" (2024), while the 2026 notes describe [[Ulrich]] as the guard "who thinks he's in charge of the dungeon". The two fit together, but the chain of command is not spelled out.
- Culture, appearance and fate are not in the notes.
