Everyone with a name, from the heroes of [[The Runaways]] to the guards of the [[Carthollow Dungeon]]. For the groups they belong to, see [[organizations/index|Organizations]].

## Main cast of *The Runaways*

- [[Eric]] — the protagonist; an Aramorean warrior and wizard who wakes in the dungeon with no memory
- [[Jane Barker]] — scout and wizard of the resistance; Eric's closest friend
- [[Lucas]] — dual-sword soldier of the resistance; carries a xiramite dagger from his mother
- [[David]] — Eric's cellmate in the Carthollow Dungeon
- [[Brian]] — the resistance's top spy, who also holds a leading post in King Carter's forces
- [[Scott Barker]] — one of the resistance's top scouts; Jane's older brother
- [[Joseph Barker]] — an older resistance spy; father of Jane and Scott
- [[King Carter]] — the king who rules from Carthollow, whom the resistance fights to dethrone
- [[King Augo]] — king of Keyesport Vale
- [[Dherlin]] — the evil wizard, thought to be dead
- [[Eric's mother]] — Eric's unnamed mother, known for always caring for others
