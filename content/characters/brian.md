**Brian** is the top spy of [[The Resistance]] in [[The Runaways]], a 1st level resistance leader who also holds a leading position in [[King Carter's forces]], and the man who sets [[Eric]] free from the [[Carthollow Dungeon]].

## Background

Brian is a leader in King Carter's forces, so he sees who [[King Carter]] is as a person. He joined the resistance because he viewed Carter as too dangerous to be on the throne because of his bipolar moods.

In the dungeon he works with the guards [[Gunter]], a double agent, to free prisoners, and he pays the guard [[Frode]] to let prisoners out.

## Role in the story

- **Book 1:** [[The King's Trial]] is what brings Eric to Brian's attention. Brian senses a connection between himself and Eric, and that Eric will be influential in the fight against Carter, so he risks his life to set Eric (and [[David]]) free. He tells them to run to [[Harrisonville]] and gives them a little money for provisions. In the rewrite he does not travel with them, he just lets them out.
- **Book 3:** Eric finds him in captivity together with [[Kolt]] and [[Henri]] (see Secrets).

## Relationships

- **[[Eric]]** — the prisoner he frees (see also Secrets).

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Brian is Eric's father**, though neither of them knows it until Book 3, when Eric finds out.
> - **Family:** Brian's mother had Xi ancestors. Brian's father took him away from his mother shortly after his birth. She later had another child with someone else, and that half sibling is [[Lucas]]'s mother, making Eric and Lucas half cousins (sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-ideas-personal.md). An older note traces Eric's Xi blood through his mother instead (see [[Eric]]).
> - A draft passage says Eric's father was "a war veteran, one of the best" (sources/drive/runaways/paragraphs-for-the-runaways-personal.md).
> - The 2021 leadership doc lists Brian as "(captured)". In Book 3 [[Crisig]] frees Eric and leads him to a small area where he finds Brian, Kolt and Henri, and the five escape together.
> - **Newest notes (2026):** Bardon and Rykor break Eric out of the dungeon; the King's Trial still "exposes Brian to Eric".
