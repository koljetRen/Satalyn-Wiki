**Ulrich** is a guard of the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in [[The Runaways]] who thinks he's in charge of the dungeon, and the guard who kicks [[Eric]].

## Appearance

A battle-worn face lined with scars, a scratchy beard and brown eyes.

## Personality

Ulrich makes sure the prison is run right and gets upset when someone does something out of order. He is typically the first one on the scene to punish prisoners.

## Role in the story
