**Rannulfer** (Rann) is the "dungeon jester" of the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] in [[The Runaways]], a prisoner who only appears in the dungeon.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Culture / race | [[Aramore people\|Aramorean]] |

| Rank / role | Prisoner |

## Appearance

Rann has a patchy brown beard but is bald on top, with yellow teeth, several of them missing.

## Personality

He is the weirdo who goes up and talks to anyone and everyone, and acts "kinda sus".

## Role in the story
