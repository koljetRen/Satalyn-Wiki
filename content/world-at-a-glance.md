**Satalyn** is a fantasy world made by [[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, for [[Lebarka]], the Father of All. It is the setting of the novel series *[[The Runaways]]* and of the author's [[The D&D Campaign|D&D campaigns]]. ("Satalyn" is a placeholder name.) This page is a one-page introduction for a new reader or player; every heading links to the full pages.

> [!note] The present day
> Unless a page says otherwise, "now" on this wiki is **around the year 1067**, when *The Runaways* begins. See the [[Timeline of Satalyn|timeline]] for everything before and after.

## The world in brief

- A medieval world of kingdoms, city-states, island chiefdoms and deserts, where most people marry and start families young.
- Twelve gods are known worldwide. They designed the world, but they are distant, and worship of them has faded.
- Magic is real but rare: only people born into one ancient bloodline can use it.
- Five races live here. Humans are the most widespread; the others are rarer and live apart.
- Around 1067, [[King Carter]] rules from the city of [[Carthollow]], and a rebel [[The Resistance|resistance]] fights to dethrone him.

## How the world was made

Every world in [[The Multiverse|the multiverse]] is built by the Supreme Creator through [[Seven Phases of Creation|seven phases]], following the rules of the [[Book of Creation]]. On Satalyn:

1. **Phases 1–4** made the sun, the world, its [[The Three Moons|three moons]], the sky, the sea, the land and the seasons.
2. **Phase 5** brought life, and the five races chosen by [[Modingua]], the Mother of All.
3. **Phase 6**, [[The Golden Age|the golden age]], made real the gifts of [[The Nine]]. Mortals skipped the hunter-gatherer stage and built the first cities.
4. **Phase 7** began with a great freeze: [[The Ice Age|the ice age]] of about 1,000 years, which buried the first cities and scattered the [[The Ice Age People|ice age people]]. In phase seven the Creator steps away from the world.

## The gods

The [[The Pantheon|pantheon]] (still unnamed) has twelve deities known worldwide. Each god has a little power, and only within their own domain. Each of the Nine gave mortals a gift that pushed them forward, and each gift has a flaw its god didn't foresee.

| God | Known as | Gift to mortals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Euiklatz]] | Supreme Creator | Builds every world |
| [[Lebarka]] | Father of All, God of Life | Asked for the world; passes on the gods' requests |
| [[Modingua]] | Mother of All, Goddess of Motherhood and love | Chose the five races |
| [[Dauviti]] | God of the Dead | Death |
| [[Stomell]] | God of the Skies | Predictable weather; his daughters are the three moons |
| [[Ozefio]] | Goddess of the Seas | Shipbuilding and fishing |
| [[Felderg]] | God of the Earth | Plants and farming |
| [[Gehflaun]] | Goddess of Intelligence | Reading, writing and study |
| [[Dreplagen]] | God of Strength | Strength, speed and mobility |
| [[Krarkur]] | God of Deceit | Persuasion, negotiation and cunning |
| [[Klist]] | Goddess of Music | Sound, music and languages |
| [[Magurkai]] | God of Magic | Magic, through a bloodline |

The three elder gods are honoured everywhere in a formal, distant way; the Nine are worshipped region by region. People once worshipped through [[Worship Through Dance and Song|dance and song]], above all at the [[Creation Festival]], but by the modern day this is rare ([[Why Worship Faded]]), and the gods answer only now and then ([[The Gods in the Modern Day]]). People also tell of [[Sacred Creatures|sacred creatures]], one for each god, with a whale's body, an elk's antlers and owl-like fins. Smaller cults and regions keep [[Lesser and Regional Gods|lesser gods]] of their own.

## Magic

- **Where it comes from.** In the golden age, [[Magurkai]] visited 100 villagers, [[The First Cult|the first cult]], and gave them [[Magic|magic]].
- **Who can use it.** Only their descendants: casters draw on the iron in their blood, and the purer the bloodline, the stronger the magic ([[Bloodline Magic]]). Rarely, a long-[[Dormant Bloodlines|dormant bloodline]] wakes up.
- **The cost.** [[Overcasting]] drains the blood's iron and can cause fainting or death.
- **In society.** The bloodline splits the world into the magic-born and everyone else. At least one place bans magic ([[Magic Bans]]). Trained casters are [[Wizards|wizards]]; some sit on the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]].
- **Xiramite.** The Xi metal [[Xiramite|xiramite]] cracks as it bonds with its wielder, and only those with Xi blood can wield it.

## The present day (around 1067)

- **King Carter's realm.** After [[The Fall of Fillyolia|the fall of Fillyolia]], villages tried to set up kingdoms of their own, and [[King Carter]] sent [[King Carter's forces|his forces]] to reunite them so they would be strong against outside forces. He rules from [[Carthollow]], whose [[Carthollow Dungeon|dungeon]] holds his prisoners.
- **The resistance.** [[The Resistance]], led by [[Zandar]], says Carter stole the throne from [[King Augo]] of [[Keyesport Vale]], and fights to put the rightful ruler back. It hides in the Southern Misty Mountains, reached through [[Misty Phlats]].
- **The evil wizard.** [[Dherlin]], an evil wizard, is thought to be dead.
- **Faith.** Worship of the gods has faded; in Aramore the old [[Skyfather and the Elemental Spirits|Skyfather faith]] is one "no one seems to follow anymore", and the auroras have not been seen since the desolation of the ground water.
- **Money.** Five coins are in use, valued by the metal they are lined with ([[Coinage]]):

| Coin | Lined with | Worth (in Parce) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parce | Copper | 1 |
| Darceo | Silver | 25 |
| Reso | Electrum | 800 |
| Augist | Gold | 1,250 |
| Pagon | Platinum | 80,000 |
