## Places

- **Carthollow vs Carterville:** the 2026 sheet calls King Carter's city Carthollow; the older plot notes and first draft say Carterville. Wiki uses Carthollow with Carterville as an alias — confirm (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Aegryamis vs Aegyramis:** the 2026 sheet spells it Aegryamis; the Runaways ideas doc and the slave book spell it Aegyramis. Wiki uses Aegryamis (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md, sources/drive/slave-hunters/the-slave-book.md).
- **Misty Peaks vs Southern Misty Mountains / Southern Misty Peaks:** are these one range (with "Southern" naming part of it) or two? The wiki treats them as one page (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Forin vs Snyder:** Peter Snyder's realm is "the throne of Forin" in the slave-book outline but "the nation of Snyder" in both draft openings. Wiki uses Forin — which is it? (sources/drive/slave-hunters/the-slave-book.md, sources/drive/slave-hunters/slave-hunters.md).
- **Keyesport Vale's king:** "King Augo, King of Keyesport Vale" (character list) vs "King [Argo?]" (Book 2 blurb) vs Augo as rightful heir of Fillyolia (plot twist) (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md, sources/drive/runaways/book-2-stomped-in-the-dust.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Book 2's "fort" and "city":** are they at Keyesport Vale? The blurb has Carter capture the island; the plot list has "large battle at the fort" then "King Carter's forces take over the city" (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Xi "undiscovered":** the Pi AI conversation calls Xi "a small tropical island that no one has discovered", yet the Xi Empire held a west coast for decades and the Runaways sail there. Is it still unknown to most mainlanders in 1067? (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Which "west coast" did the Xi Empire attack, and where are the Xi City Ruins?** (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md, sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-sites-personal.md).
- **Aramore, Carter's kingdom and Fillyolia:** is King Carter's realm Aramore, Fillyolia (as the plot twist implies), or something else? Is Carthollow its capital? (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Great City:** is it a proper name? Where is it — in Fillyolia? (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md).
- **Aerelm as a place:** the D&D note "Magic is banned from Aerelm because the King is worried about power" may name a place; Aerelm/Aerlem is otherwise a resistance wizard. Flagged on the places index, not merged (sources/drive/notes-from-dnd-about-my-world.md).
- **Harrisonville people:** Willy Smith is "mayor of Harrisonville" in the character list but "owner of tavern in Harrisonville" in the character-development notes; Cassandra Smith is the inn owner. Who runs what? (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Region of each Book 1 location:** the notes don't say which region Harrisonville, Misty Phlats, Mourncliff, Moonwick or Shadow's Edge are in, or whether the Aramore Forest is in Aramore.
- **Name-only places:** Shadow Bay, Dyke Hills, the North Sea, the North Spine, Cadnitz, Averill, Lucal have no location or description. Is Shadow Bay linked to Shadow's Edge? Are the Dyke Hills linked to Dyken?
- **Thrymfell and Northwatch:** is Thrymfell a settlement or the forest itself? Is Northwatch in Skarg, and is it a town or a fort? (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md).
- **Osisu's climate** and the Shadow Bay climate are blank in the World Regions sheet.
- **Unexplained sites:** Wolf Camp Clearing, Drakes alley, Magical Rock (sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-sites-personal.md); "the holding trees" in the Southern Misty Peaks (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
- **Which island?** The draft campfire passage has the group "escaped from the island and traveled north along the coast": Keyesport Vale, the island prison, or another? (sources/drive/runaways/paragraphs-for-the-runaways-personal.md).
- **Where is Eric held in Book 3** before Crisig frees him and they turn up in Sprake? (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
