- **Dauviti's title:** God of the Dead (2026) vs God of the Underworld (older god list) — same sources.
- **Modingua's title:** "Goddess of Motherhood, love, fertility and reproduction" (2026) vs "Goddess of Mothership" (older list, assumed typo for Motherhood) — same sources.
- **Felderg's domain:** God of the Earth, but the 2026 notes are "now leaning towards a God of the Harvest". Which? — sources/claude-doc/world-creation-notes.md.

- **Pantheon name / festival name and month / Stomell's daughters' names:** all still unnamed — sources/claude-doc/world-creation-notes.md.
- **Which of the Nine chose badly; did other gods visit mortals; how dances differ by region; how the other gods are faring today:** open in the 2026 notes — sources/claude-doc/world-creation-notes.md.
- **Osisu ocean goddess vs Ozefio:** is Osisu's ocean goddess Ozefio under a regional name, or a separate lesser goddess? — sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md.

- **The second "major religion":** the older notes mention a religion "scattered around the world, but mainly in the mountains and areas separated from the main population". Which faith is it (the Skyfather spirits? a Kurupal or stoneskin faith?) — sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md.
- **Skyfather and the auroras:** the auroras stopped after "the desolation of the ground water from the evil wizard". Is that Dherlin? What are the other elemental spirits? — sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md.
- **Xi prayer:** Xi women pray to "the God's" (2026 sheet) vs "their God" (older notes), while the Xi religion is ancestor worship. To whom do they pray? — sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md, sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md.
