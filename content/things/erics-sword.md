**Eric's Sword** is the [[Xiramite]] sword [[Eric]] forges in *[[The Runaways]]* Book 1, which cracks as it connects to him until crimson light flows through its black blade.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Sword |

| Made | Book 1 (1067), from a pocket of xiramite in the [[Southern Misty Mountains]] |

## Description

Like all xiramite, it starts straight black. As it connects to Eric it cracks, and once fully cracked a crimson red colour flows through "the small black canyons on the blade", seeming to pulse. In a fight it pulses rapidly, "as if it were filled with adrenaline". The author's location deck has a slide titled "Eric's Sword".

## In the stories

- **Book 1:** Eric is led to a pocket of xiramite and uses it to make his sword and a dagger. [[Shetien]] identifies the metal as from the Xi empire age. On their travels [[Lucas]] notices the sword cracking and worries it might be destroyed, since his own dagger never cracked.
- **Book 2:** in [[Quino]], a Xi warrior, [[Rykor]], challenges the group for possessing xiramite. When he sees Eric wield his fully cracked sword, he yields — the moment of the draft scene "Revealing the Xi Daggers".
- **Draft scene "The Gladiator Battle":** Eric fights a black figure "possessed with some evil power" in an arena. The opponent's steel blade shatters against the black and red sword, which goes on into the shadowy body; the figure screeches and vanishes. The notes don't say which book this scene belongs to.
