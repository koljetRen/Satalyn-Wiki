**Lucas's Dagger** is the [[Xiramite]] dagger that [[Lucas]] carries in *[[The Runaways]]*, a gift from his mother, which stays black and uncracked until he learns to connect with it on Xi.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Dagger |
| Material | [[Xiramite]] |
| Wielder | [[Lucas]] |
| Origin | A gift from his mother |

## Description

For most of Book 1 the dagger is "raw": it never cracked, because it never formed a true connection with Lucas.

## In the stories

- **Book 1:** when Lucas sees [[Eric's Sword]] cracking, he worries the sword might be destroyed, since his dagger never cracked. He doesn't know why.
- **Book 2:** when the Xi warrior [[Rykor]] will take only Eric, Lucas slowly draws the dagger and holds it out. Rykor gasps: "You have xiramite as well, but no connection." Lucas says: "It was a gift from my mother. She said to reveal it only when it was necessary." Rykor yields, and the group travels to Xi. There Lucas is taught how to connect, and after one to two months he becomes connected to his dagger.

## Open questions

- In Book 1, Eric makes "his sword and a dagger" from the xiramite he finds. Is that a second dagger, or is this one?
