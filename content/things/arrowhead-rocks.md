**The arrowhead rocks** are tiny stones, each shaped like an arrowhead and about the size of a splinter of wood, that [[Eric]] finds in their hundreds in a perfect circle of stone in a draft passage of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Description

Each rock is shaped like an arrowhead but far too small to be one — "about the size of a splinter of wood". Hundreds lie together, all shaped the same way, in "the odd patch of stone in the grass", which is a perfect circle.

## In the stories

In the draft passage, "The moment Eric found that teeny-tiny, little rock changed his life." Eric shows [[Lucas]] the rocks, and the two agree this is what they were looking for and that they are on the right trail. They fill three pouches with the rocks. As they walk away, Eric wonders what split the rocks like that and what made the perfect circle of stone.
