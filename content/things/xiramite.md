**Xiramite** is a metal of the [[Xi]] that is straight black when first forged but cracks, letting its power out, as it forms a connection with its wielder — and only those with Xi blood can wield it.

## Description

Fresh xiramite is "straight black when first forged". As it connects to its user it cracks, and its power shows through the cracks. On a fully connected blade, a crimson red colour flows through "the small black canyons on the blade", and it can seem to pulse; in a fight it pulses rapidly, "as if it were filled with adrenaline".

## Properties

- **Connection.** Xiramite forms a connection with its user. Cracking is the sign of that connection: "cracking shows connection". A piece that has never formed a true connection stays uncracked — "raw".
- **Xi blood.** "Xiramite can only be wielded by those with Xi blood." Those without Xi blood can carry it, but the notes say it can't be wielded by them.
- **Learning to connect.** A connection can be taught. In *The Runaways*, the Xi teach [[Lucas]] how to connect with his dagger, and after one to two months it connects.
- **Strength.** In a draft scene, Eric's cracked xiramite sword keeps going after his opponent's steel blade shatters against it.

## Where it's found

The notes connect xiramite with the Xi empire age (350–430; see [[The Xi Empire]]). In *The Runaways*, Eric is led to a pocket of xiramite in the [[Southern Misty Mountains]]; the resistance historian [[Shetien]] identifies it as being from the Xi empire age but doesn't know how it got there.

## Xiramite and the Xi

The Xi treat xiramite as theirs. In Book 2 a Xi warrior, [[Rykor]], tries to kill Eric's group for possessing it, then yields on seeing Eric's fully cracked sword and Lucas's dagger. The group is offered sanctuary on Xi "for possessing and being connected to xiramite", and learns more about the metal there.

Xi blood itself is rare outside Xi: in the time of the empire the Xi had a rule against mating with anyone foreign, and Xi blood "doesn't mix with blood that is foreign to them" — such children have a very low chance of surviving past birth. Eric and Lucas both carry Xi blood.

## In the stories

- **Book 1:** Eric finds the pocket of xiramite and uses it to make his sword and a dagger; Shetien identifies it as Xi empire age. As they travel, Lucas notices Eric's sword cracking and worries it might be destroyed, because his own dagger never cracked. He doesn't know this is because his dagger never formed a true connection with him.
- **Book 2:** Rykor's challenge; sanctuary on Xi; Lucas's dagger connects.
- A slide in the author's location deck is titled "Eric's Sword".

## Open questions

- In Book 1 Eric makes "his sword and a dagger" from the xiramite he finds. Is that dagger Lucas's? Lucas says his dagger was a gift from his mother.
- How is xiramite mined and forged, and is it still made on Xi?
- What happens if someone without Xi blood tries to wield it?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **How Eric and Lucas got their Xi blood** (two versions). The *Runaways* ideas doc (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`) traces it through their mothers, twin girls descended from the one Xi soldier who broke the rule against mating with foreigners (see [[The Xi Empire]]); this version is used as the main one, following the canon order in CLAUDE.md.
> - Why Eric's sword connected (not to be explained in Book 1, maybe in Book 2 or 3): while Eric was unconscious after Misty Phlats, "his conscience was learning from the xiramite, causing that connection to form".
> - The other version of the Xi bloodline (`sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-ideas-personal.md`): Brian is Eric's father; Brian's mother's ancestors were Xi; Brian's father took Brian away from his mother shortly after his birth; she had another child with someone else, and that half-sibling is Lucas's mother. So Eric and Lucas are half cousins with Xi blood through Brian's mother.
