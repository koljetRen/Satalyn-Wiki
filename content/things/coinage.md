The **coinage** of Satalyn is a set of five coins — the Parce, Darceo, Reso, Augist and Pagon — whose value depends on the metal they are lined with.

## The coins

Coins "have value based on materials they have been lined with". Each coin matches one of the D&D coin metals.

| Coin | Lined with | D&D equivalent | Value in Parce |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Parce** | Copper | Copper piece | 1 |
| **Darceo** | Silver | Silver piece | 25 |
| **Reso** | Electrum | Electrum piece | 800 |
| **Augist** | Gold | Gold piece | 1,250 |
| **Pagon** | Platinum | Platinum piece | 80,000 |

## Conversions

The basic rates:

- 1 Darceo = 25 Parce
- 1 Augist = 50 Darceo
- 1 Reso = 32 Darceo
- 1 Pagon = 64 Augist

Full table (read across: one coin in the left column is worth this many of the coin at the top). Figures are the author's, which round some values.

| 1 coin = | Parce | Darceo | Reso | Augist | Pagon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Parce** | 1 | 0.04 | 0.0012 | 0.0008 | 0.000012 |
| **Darceo** | 25 | 1 | 0.03 | 0.02 | 0.0003 |
| **Reso** | 800 | 32 | 1 | 0.64 | 0.0096 |
| **Augist** | 1,250 | 50 | 1.56 | 1 | 0.016 |
| **Pagon** | 80,000 | 3,200 | 100 | 64 | 1 |

> [!note]
> One figure in the notes doesn't match the rest: the notes give 1 Reso = 0.0096 Pagon, but 1 Pagon = 100 Reso makes 1 Reso = 0.01 Pagon exactly (800 Parce ÷ 80,000 Parce). The other small figures are rounded: exactly, 1 Parce = 0.00125 Reso and 0.0000125 Pagon, 1 Darceo = 0.03125 Reso and 0.0003125 Pagon, 1 Augist = 1.5625 Reso and 0.015625 Pagon.

Unlike in D&D, the Reso (electrum) is worth more than half an Augist (gold): 32 Darceo against the Augist's 50.

## In the stories

In *The Runaways* Book 1, [[Brian]] gives Eric and David "a little bit of money for provisions", but in [[Harrisonville]] they don't have enough to lodge at the inn. The notes don't name the coins involved.
