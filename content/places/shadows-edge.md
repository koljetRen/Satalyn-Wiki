**Shadow's Edge** is a place a few days' travel from [[Misty Phlats]], where the resistance sends Eric and others to scout and buy weapons in Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## In the stories

After a few weeks of training at the [[Resistance base|resistance base]], Eric's group is sent to Shadow's Edge to do some scouting and buy weapons. They travel back to Misty Phlats after a few days. "View of Shadow's Edge" is one of the Runaways sites the author pictured; the next slide in that list is "Drakes alley".

## Open questions

- Where is "Drakes alley", and is it named after [[Drake]], the resistance's bow supplier?
- Is Shadow's Edge a town or a city, and is it connected to [[Shadow Bay]]?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> At Shadow's Edge, [[David]] sneaks off unnoticed and sends a report to the king about the resistance base.
