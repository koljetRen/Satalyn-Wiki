**The magic holy site** is the place where [[Magurkai]], God of Magic, visited a village in person and gave magic to the first cult of magic users.

## History

Magurkai visited 100 people from a village and gave them magic, changing their bodies to store and produce more of the iron in their blood that they draw on to cast. All [[Bloodline magic|bloodline magic]] descends from them. This was a rare event, since the gods are distant. The site of the visit is a holy site.
