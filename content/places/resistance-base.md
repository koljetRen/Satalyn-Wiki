**The resistance base** is the hidden headquarters of [[The Resistance|the resistance]] in the Southern [[Misty Peaks|Misty Mountains]], reached from [[Misty Phlats]], in Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Hidden base |
| Region | Southern [[Misty Peaks\|Misty Mountains]] |
| Ruler / leader | [[Xander\|Zandar]] and [[Elliott]] |

## Layout

The author's list of Runaways sites includes the **resistance main cavern**, two views outside the base (one with [[Jane Barker|Jane]], one with [[Xander]]) and the [[Training Grove]]. The base has back passes through which the resistance escapes during the [[Battle of Misty Phlats]].

## In the stories

In the rewritten Book 1 plot, [[Samheir]] takes Eric and his companions from Misty Phlats to the base, where they meet Zandar and Elliott. Eric and [[David]] train as soldiers, and Eric befriends [[Lucas]]. The wizard [[Aerelm]] visits, senses magic in Eric, and Eric begins training as a wizard too. When King Carter's army marches on Misty Phlats, the resistance prepares to flee the base.

In the Book 2 plot, Eric leads an expedition back to the Southern Misty Peaks to retrieve all the weapons from "the holding trees" and "the old resistance base".

## Ideas and open threads

- A D&D session note says "Brotz is a resistance member in the town, who guides them to the resistance base". It doesn't name the town or say whether this is the same base.
