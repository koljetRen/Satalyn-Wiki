**Keyesport Vale** is an island ruled by [[King Augo]], where the resistance takes refuge at the end of Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]* and which [[King Carter]]'s armies capture in Book 2.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Island |
| Ruler / leader | [[King Augo]], "King of Keyesport Vale" |

## In the stories

During the [[Battle of Misty Phlats]], the resistance plans its escape to Keyesport Vale. After regrouping at [[Mourncliff]], it sails from [[Moonwick]] to Keyesport Vale, where it spends a few days before seeing ships on the horizon.

The Book 2 blurb (*Stomped in the Dust*) says the resistance finds itself at Keyesport Vale, "home of King [Argo?]", but isn't safe for long: King Carter's armies attack and capture the island and all the men and women living there, and Eric must find a way to escape the island. The possible Book 2 plot opens with a large battle at "the fort", after which King Carter's forces take over "the city".

> [!info] Canon note
> The character list calls Augo "King of Keyesport Vale" (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md); the Book 2 blurb writes "King [Argo?]", unsure of the name (sources/drive/runaways/book-2-stomped-in-the-dust.md).

## Open questions

- Are "the fort" and "the city" of the Book 2 plot at Keyesport Vale?
- Is "the island" Eric's group escapes from in the draft campfire passage Keyesport Vale or the [[Island prison|island prison]]?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - In the Book 2 plot, Eric and Jane use their magic together to hide a few resistance members (Eric, Jane, Lucas, Bella, Jaek, Kaz, Crisig and Crewt); all the others are killed or captured, and Eric sees [[David]] alongside the enemy soldiers. About a week later, Eric uses magic to signal a ship, which needs more water, and the survivors sail south on it to [[Quino]].
> - The plot-twist notes make Augo the rightful heir to the throne of [[Fillyolia]] and Carter's brother, rather than (or as well as) King of Keyesport Vale.
