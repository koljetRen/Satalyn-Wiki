**Aegryamis** is a desert region of Satalyn with a single fertile river, ruled by two royal families, the homeland of the [[peoples/cultures/aegryamis-people|Aegryamis]] people.

## Geography

Aegryamis is a desert with only one fertile river. Its two towns stand on opposite sides of the river. The [[Akyethis Tree]] grows here: it grows tall and branches heavily despite the harsh climate and little water, and it withstands the sandstorms. In the slave-book outline, the slave hunters plan to visit a wizard at Aegryamis the next time they are on the east side of the [[Great Desert]].

## People and culture

- **Government:** two royal families, one for each town. When a king dies, the other family becomes the ruling family and elects a king from its eligible men (every married male in the direct line).
- **Cities:** skyscraper-like buildings of stone and Akyethis wood, to save as much fertile land as possible. Lower and middle classes live in them, reached by stairs up the outside; rich families have their own estates. Only the two royal palaces are built on the river. Domes mark important buildings (palaces, temples, schools). Cities are laid out on a grid, with markets close to the palaces.
- **Skills:** irrigation, architecture and luxury goods.
- **Religion:** two [[Aegryamis brother gods|brother gods]] were given the desert of Aegryamis as a punishment by the other gods. With only one fertile river between them, they went from bitter enemies to allies and settled on the dual monarchy. An older note counts this as one of the two major religions besides the worldwide pantheon.
- **Names:** Aegryamis names draw on Basque, Bengali, Catalan, Czech, Malay, Nepali, Slovak, Slovene, Turkish, Urdu, Yoruba and Zulu names.
