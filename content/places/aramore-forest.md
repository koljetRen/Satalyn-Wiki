**The Aramore Forest** is a forest of Satalyn that [[Eric]] and his companions cross on their way from [[Harrisonville]] to the Southern [[Misty Peaks|Misty Mountains]] in Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## In the stories

In the rewritten Book 1 plot, the group travels through the Aramore Forest after fleeing Harrisonville. While sleeping there, Eric and [[Jane Barker|Jane]] both dream about [[Dherlin]]. The Aramore Forest is one of the Runaways sites the author pictured.
