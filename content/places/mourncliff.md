**Mourncliff** is the meeting point where the scattered resistance regroups after the [[Battle of Misty Phlats]] in Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## People

- [[Fahyior]] is the resistance's safeguard in Mourncliff.

## In the stories

One by one, the groups of resistance refugees reach the meetup point at Mourncliff after escaping [[Misty Phlats]]. Once the whole resistance is together, it travels on to [[Moonwick]] to take ship for [[Keyesport Vale]].

Mourncliff is one of the city names on the author's list of city names.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> [[Lucas]], [[Jaek]] and [[Henri]] carry Eric's lifeless body to Mourncliff. As the refugees arrive they hear what Eric did at Misty Phlats; some cheer, others are upset that he killed so many resistance men too. After a few days, [[Jane Barker|Jane]] dreams of Eric overlooking the valley, comforts him and asks him to come back to the land of the living. She wakes to find him still lifeless, but later that day he wakes up.
