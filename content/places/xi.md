**Xi** is a small tropical island of Satalyn, home of the warrior [[peoples/cultures/xi-people|Xi]] people and once the seat of the [[Xi Empire]].

## Geography

An older note describes Xi as "a small tropical island that no one has discovered". Xi villages are built as harbours or on the side of a mountain. In the Book 2 plot, the group travels west from [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]] to reach Xi.

## People and culture

- **Government:** a council of 17 elders leads the people. When an elder dies, the others elect a new one from the men on the island.
- **Villages:** very compact, with ancient Japanese-style homes and walls of stone bricks around the village. Every family home has a shrine to its ancestors.
- **Way of life:** men train for war from the age of 8; children start team-building exercises at 3. The elders pair young people for marriage at 15.
- **Religion:** [[Xi ancestor worship|ancestors watch over the people]].

See [[peoples/cultures/xi-people|Xi culture]] for the full culture.

## History

The Xi heard rumours of a land much larger than their island, and the council of elders decided to go and live there. They attacked the west coast of that land and ruled an empire there, the [[Xi Empire]] (350–430). After a few decades they decided such a large kingdom wasn't worth keeping, and many men were unhappy at being away from their families, so the council pulled out.

## In the stories

In the Book 2 plot of *[[The Runaways]]*, the remaining resistance members travel to Xi with the Xi warrior [[Rykor]] and are offered sanctuary because [[Eric]] and [[Lucas]] possess and are connected to [[xiramite]]. They spend one to two months there, learning about the Xi and how they fight, and Lucas connects to his dagger. Eric gains favour as a wizard who can wield xiramite. Xi remains the group's base into Book 3, where resistance soldiers keep learning to fight like the Xi.

The author's list of Runaways sites includes a "Xi Training arena dream" and the [[Xi City Ruins]].
