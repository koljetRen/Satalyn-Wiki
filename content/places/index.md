Regions, cities, mountains, seas and the other places of Satalyn. See [[Map progress]] for the state of the world map and [[Runaways sites]] for the locations the author pictured for *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Regions and realms

- [[places/aramore|Aramore]] — dry prairie kingdom, homeland of the Aramoreans and of Eric
- [[places/skarg|Skarg]] — rainy coastal forest of town councils and longhouses
- [[places/dyken|Dyken]] — desert city-states with an elected president
- [[places/osisu|Osisu]] — a volcanic island chain led by chiefs and shamans
- [[places/aegryamis|Aegryamis]] — a desert river land under two royal families
- [[places/xi|Xi]] — the warrior island, once seat of the Xi Empire
- [[Cadnitz]] — the land where Quino lies
- [[Fillyolia]] — a fallen kingdom
- [[Forin]] — the Snyder kingdom of the slave book

## Unused city names

The author's list of city names in the Runaways notes includes four names not yet given to any place: **Houghtanaba**, **Rayspan**, **Weasbourn** and **Waorig**. (The same list holds Mourncliff, Moonwick and Keyesport Vale, which are in use.)

## Names that may be places

- **Aerelm:** a D&D note says "Magic is banned from Aerelm because the King is worried about power". In the Runaways notes, Aerelm (also Aerlem) is a resistance wizard on the [[Council of Worldly Wizards]]. The note may mean a place, so it is flagged here rather than merged; see [[Aerelm]].
- **Bardon's city:** the Claude doc names "Bardon's city" as one of the human cultures, alongside Osisu. It is not named.
