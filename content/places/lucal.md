**Lucal** is a place named in the later plot notes of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Open questions

- What and where is Lucal?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> When Jane's pregnancy is too far along for her to join the final quest against [[Dherlin]], she stays at Lucal while the rest go to attack him. Eric returns to her there after defeating the evil wizard. As they start to leave Lucal, Jane goes into labour; they name their son Scott, after her brother who gave his life for the baby. After a few years in Lucal they travel back to [[Carthollow|Carterville]].
