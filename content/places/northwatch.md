**Northwatch** is a place in the north, named in the notes on [[Skarg]]'s religion as a stronghold of the old faith.

## People and culture

More people in Skarg than in [[places/aramore|Aramore]] still believe in the old faith of the [[Skyfather]] and the elemental spirits, "especially those up north in [[Thrymfell]] or Northwatch".
