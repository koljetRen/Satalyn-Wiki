**The Misty Peaks** are an alpine mountain range of Satalyn. Their southern part, called the Southern Misty Mountains or Southern Misty Peaks in the Runaways notes, holds [[Misty Phlats]] and the [[Resistance base|resistance base]].

## Geography

The World Regions sheet lists the Misty Peaks as alpine. The Book 1 plot reaches the Southern Misty Mountains from [[Harrisonville]] through the [[Aramore Forest]]; a rewrite note says the group should instead follow a river by boat from Harrisonville to the mountains. The author pictured the "Foothills of Southern Misty Peaks" as one of the Runaways sites.

## In the stories

In Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*, [[Eric]] and his companions come to [[Misty Phlats]] and from there to the resistance base, where Eric trains as a soldier and wizard. [[Eric]] and [[Jane Barker|Jane]] grow closer in the mountains. Eric is led to a pocket of [[xiramite]] and makes his sword and a dagger from it; [[Shetien]] identifies it as from the [[Xi Empire]] age but doesn't know how it got to the Southern Misty Mountains. The resistance flees the area during the [[Battle of Misty Phlats]].

In the Book 2 plot, Eric leads an expedition back to the Southern Misty Peaks to retrieve all the weapons from "the holding trees" and the old resistance base.

> [!info] Canon note
> The 2026 sheets call the range "Misty Peaks". The older Runaways plot notes say "Southern Misty Mountains" and "Southern Misty Peaks" (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md, sources/drive/runaways/the-runaways-sites-personal.md). This page assumes they are the same range, with "Southern" naming its southern part.
