**Thrymfell** is a place in the north of [[Skarg]], and the **Thrymfell Forest** is where the [[Thrym Pine]] grows.

## Geography

The [[Thrym Pine]] grows in the Thrymfell Forest in Skarg. It has exceptional durability, is very receptive to magic, and has a medicinal resin.

## People and culture

More people in Skarg than in [[places/aramore|Aramore]] still believe in the old faith of the [[Skyfather]] and the elemental spirits, "especially those up north in Thrymfell or [[Northwatch]]".
