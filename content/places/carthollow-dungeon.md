**Carthollow Dungeon** is the prison in [[Carthollow]] where [[Eric]] wakes up with no memory at the start of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Layout

The prison has 10 cells. The background-character notes also mention a cafeteria, where a skirmish breaks out. The "Dungeon" is the first of the Runaways sites the author pictured.

## In the stories

Eric wakes in the dungeon with no memory. He meets David, Rann, Bardon, Ulrich, the Torturer and Akio, in that order, and learns of [[The King's Trial]], a bracket tournament in which prisoners fight with wooden swords for the promise of freedom. Akio already has experience of the King's Trials from other prisons. Eric reaches the third round before he is defeated.

> [!info] Canon note
> The rewritten Book 1 plot (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) has Eric spend "a few weeks" in prison, and a rewrite note says to "draw out being in prison". The older notes call the city Carterville.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **David's fate:** the 2026 sheet has Eric watch David get beaten to death in a cafeteria skirmish in chapter 1 (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md). The older plot has David escape with Eric and live into Book 3 (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md).
> - **The escape:** in the 2026 sheet, [[Bardon]] is a resistance member who was captured on purpose and teams up with [[Rykor]] to break Eric and Akio out. In the older plot, [[Brian]] sets Eric and David free after a few weeks and tells them to run to [[Harrisonville]], giving them a little money. Bardon's background entry says he is a former soldier charged with treason who joins the resistance in Book 2.
> - The King's Trial is what exposes Brian to Eric.
> - Guard [[Frode]] is paid by Brian to let prisoners out; guard [[Gunter]] is a double agent working with Brian to free prisoners.
