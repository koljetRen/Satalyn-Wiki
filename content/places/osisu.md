**Osisu** is an island nation of Satalyn built on a chain of volcanoes, the homeland of the [[peoples/cultures/osisu-people|Osisuan]] people.

## Geography

The islands are a chain of volcanoes, some active and some dormant, with beaches and jungle. The queen shaman lives on the most active volcanic island. The [[Stormshark]] lives off the Osisu and Dyken coasts.

## People and culture

- **Government:** each island is led by a chief and shaman pair. The queen shaman, who represents Osisu's goddess, picks the chiefs, and the chiefs meet together to lead Osisu as a whole. The queen shaman is chosen by all the islands' shamans, with the help of their goddess.
- **Villages:** village centres lie along the beach, with stone buildings (volcanic rock, decorated with wood inside) stretching into the jungle. Large wooden docks hold most of the marketplaces. Each island has a large Mayan-style pyramid that houses the shaman and chief. Only bedchambers and food stores have roofs, because the people welcome the rain.
- **Skills:** ocean navigation, tattooing, fishing, canoe carving and music. Fishermen are called Seacasters.
- **Religion:** the people worship an ocean goddess.
- **Names:** last names are chosen with a person's expertise and come from something in the region, such as the fisherman's name Stormshark. Names draw on Estonian, Hawaiian, Maori, Swahili, Thai, Near Eastern and Hindu names, with African and Khmer last names.
