**Moonwick** is the place where the resistance boards ships for [[Keyesport Vale]] at the end of Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## In the stories

Once the whole resistance has regrouped at [[Mourncliff]], it travels to Moonwick and boards ships to Keyesport Vale.

Moonwick is one of the city names on the author's list of city names.
