**Skarg** is a rainy coastal forest region of Satalyn, the homeland of the [[peoples/cultures/skarg|Skargan]] people.

## Geography

Skarg is a coastal forest with a lot of rain. The [[Thrymfell|Thrymfell Forest]], where the [[Thrym Pine]] grows, is in Skarg. The culture notes mention [[Thrymfell]] and [[Northwatch]] as places "up north".

## People and culture

- **Government:** each town is ruled by a council of elders made up of the 11 oldest capable members of the town. For a nationwide decision, each town council elects 2 representatives to go to the capital on the town's behalf.
- **Towns:** Norse-style longhouses built in a circle around the town centre, where a large totem pole depicts one of the elemental spirits as humans picture it. A wall of timbers surrounds the town.
- **Skills:** shipbuilding, woodworking and storytelling.
- **Religion:** the same elemental-spirit faith as [[places/aramore|Aramore]], led by the [[Skyfather]], but more people still believe it, especially up north in Thrymfell and Northwatch.
- **Names:** Skargan names draw on Danish, Dutch, Finnish, Icelandic, Irish, Limburgish, Norwegian, Russian, Swedish, Ukrainian, Welsh, Norse (mythology and ancient) and Germanic names.

See [[peoples/cultures/skarg|Skargan culture]] for the full culture.
