## Planned layers

The map is planned in three layers: before the [[Ice age|ice age]], during it, and after it, leading to the present day.

## Related ideas from the notes

- Lower sea levels during the ice age may have opened land bridges that later flooded, a source of legends about lost homelands and drowned cities.
- Ruins in old glacier valleys are among the traces of the [[Ice age people|ice age people]] in the modern day.
- Where the ice age refuges were is still an open question in the Claude doc.
