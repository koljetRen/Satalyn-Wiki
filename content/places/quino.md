**Quino** is a place in [[Cadnitz]] where the surviving resistance members land in Book 2 of *[[The Runaways]]* and meet the Xi warrior [[Rykor]].

## In the stories

In the possible Book 2 plot, the survivors sail south to Quino in Cadnitz and spend time there. A Xi warrior, Rykor, tries to kill them for possessing [[xiramite]], but yields after seeing Eric wield his fully cracked sword and Lucas show his dagger. Together they all travel west to [[Xi]].
