**Dyken** is a dry desert region of Satalyn made up of city-states, the homeland of the [[peoples/cultures/dyken-people|Dyken]] people.

## Geography

Dyken is dry desert. It has a coast: the [[Stormshark]] lives off the Osisu and Dyken coasts.

## People and culture

- **Government:** each city is a city-state ruled by a council of judges. At a meeting no man from the city is denied entrance or a voice. Every 5 years each city-state puts forward one representative, and all the judges of all the city-states meet behind closed doors to elect one of them president of Dyken.
- **Cities:** ancient Athens-style marble buildings. Temples are the largest buildings, topped with gold-lined domes. Amphitheatres, temples and other gathering places stand at the centre of each city, and rectangular homes spiral outward from them.
- **Skills:** philosophy, architecture and trade.
- **Religion:** every city has a temple that is also a school for children and adults. [[Gehflaun]], Goddess of Intelligence, is central to Dyken worship, and the Dyken feel most strongly how [[Krarkur]] twisted intelligence into castes.
- **Names:** Dyken names draw on Albanian, Armenian, Belarusian, Bosnian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Galician, Georgian, Greek, Hebrew, Indonesian, Italian, Macedonian, Portuguese, Spanish, Egyptian and Greek and Roman (mythology and ancient) names.

See [[peoples/cultures/dyken-people|Dyken culture]] for the full culture.
