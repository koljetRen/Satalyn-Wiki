**The Great Desert** is a large, windswept sandy desert in Satalyn, crossed by slave caravans.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Desert; geographical feature |
| Climate | Large, windswept sandy desert |
| Real-world inspiration | The Sahara Desert |

## Geography

The Great Desert is listed both as a world region and as a geographical feature. In the slave-book outline, [[Aegryamis]] is visited "on the east side of the great desert".

## In the stories

In the slave book ([[Slave Hunters]]), [[Peter Snyder]] is sold to slavers crossing the Great Desert. Halfway across, he escapes the caravan with three others, and on the second day he and the last surviving escapee meet a slave hunter who is on a mission to find and free all slaves.
