**Harrisonville** is a town in *[[The Runaways]]* where [[Eric]] and [[David]] take refuge after escaping prison in Book 1, and where they meet the [[Jane Barker|Barker]] family.

| --- | --- |

## People

- [[Willy Smith]] (also called Giles Teray): the kind and accepting mayor of Harrisonville in the character list; the owner of a tavern in Harrisonville in the character-development notes. He helps reveal David's backstory.
- [[Cassandra Smith]] (also called Maly Teray): owner of the Harrisonville inn "with a dark side", and Willy's wife. She also helps reveal David's backstory.

## In the stories

In the rewritten Book 1 plot, Eric and David flee to Harrisonville from the prison, hiding from King Carter's forces in a small cave in a forest on the way. In Harrisonville they try to lodge at the inn but can't afford it and are turned out onto the streets. David steals money and has the guard called on him. [[Scott Barker|Scott]] recognises them and takes them to [[Joseph Barker|Joseph]] and [[Jane Barker|Jane]], and the five stay together for a few nights. When the army from Carterville comes and demands them, Joseph sends them away in the night and stays behind. They flee through the [[Aramore Forest]] towards the Southern [[Misty Peaks|Misty Mountains]].

Harrisonville and the "Cave" are both among the Runaways sites the author pictured.

> [!info] Canon note
> Harrisonville comes from the older Book 1 canon (the first-draft manuscript and the older plot notes). The author's rewrite notes say to "rewrite the whole Harrisonville subplot", cut James, Damon and Martha, and have the group follow a river by boat from Harrisonville to the Southern Misty Mountains (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md). The 2026 sheet doesn't mention Harrisonville.
