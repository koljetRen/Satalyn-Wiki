**Averill** is a place named at the end of the Book 3 plot of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Open questions

- What and where is Averill?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> After Eric destroys [[Dherlin]]'s presence in King Carter, he and his friends decide to put an end to Dherlin, and agree that Averill is the best place to start looking for him. The group that sets out: Eric, Jane, Lucas, Scott, Jaek, Crisig, Crewt, Rykor and Liko. Eric and Jane marry before they leave for Averill.
