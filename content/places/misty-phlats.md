**Misty Phlats** is a town in the Southern [[Misty Peaks|Misty Mountains]], the gateway to the [[Resistance base|resistance base]] and the site of the [[Battle of Misty Phlats]] at the end of Book 1 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Town |
| Region | Southern [[Misty Peaks\|Misty Mountains]] |

## Places in the town

- **The inn**, where Eric and his companions stay. Its bartender is the father of the resistance scouts [[Crisig]] and [[Crewt]]; [[Cerise]] is their mother.

## In the stories

In the rewritten Book 1 plot, the group makes its way to Misty Phlats and stays at the inn, where they meet Crisig and [[Samheir]]. Samheir takes them on to the resistance base. Later, returning to Misty Phlats after a few days at [[Shadow's Edge]], they find reports of King Carter's army marching their way. The army reaches Misty Phlats sooner than expected. The resistance plans to escape towards [[Keyesport Vale]]: Samheir leads a frontal charge, [[Xander|Zandar]] and [[Elliott]] strike the main army from the side, and the rest escape through the back passes. The survivors regroup at [[Mourncliff]].

The Book 2 blurb opens with the resistance having "narrowly escap[ed] the armies of King Carter at Misty Phlats". Misty Phlats is one of the Runaways sites the author pictured.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> During the battle, Eric goes into a full "avatar state" and fireballs the whole valley, killing many resistance men as well as enemies and putting himself into a death-like state. [[Lucas]], [[Jaek]] and [[Henri]] carry his body to Mourncliff. The resistance leadership notes list Sam (Samheir) as dead.
