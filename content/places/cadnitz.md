**Cadnitz** is a region of Satalyn where the town of [[Quino]] lies.

## In the stories

In the Book 2 plot of *[[The Runaways]]*, the surviving resistance members sail south to Quino in Cadnitz and spend time there before travelling west to [[Xi]].
