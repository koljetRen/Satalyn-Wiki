**Forin** is the kingdom of the Snyder royal family, the homeland of [[Peter Snyder]] in the slave book ([[Slave Hunters]]).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Kingdom |
| Ruler / leader | King [[King William Snyder VII\|William Snyder VII]]; later his son Peter |

## People and culture

The king rules with a council. The draft opening describes the council room: a U-shaped table with the king's seat, the largest, at its head, an empty seat on each side of it, and 12 seats down the outside of each arm of the U, with 22 council members in all, from their early 20s to old age.

## In the stories

Peter Snyder, son of King William Snyder VII, is placed on the throne at 15 after his family dies together, with a council to help him.

> [!info] Canon note
> The slave-book outline calls the realm "the throne of Forin"; both draft openings call it "the nation of Snyder" (sources/drive/slave-hunters/the-slave-book.md, sources/drive/slave-hunters/slave-hunters.md). This page uses Forin, from the outline, and keeps Snyder as an alias.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> After Peter has ruled a little while, the head helper of his council kidnaps him in his sleep, makes people think he ran away, and, with no heirs left, takes the king's place. Peter is beaten and given to slavers.
