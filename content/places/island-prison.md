**The island prison** is the prison on an island where [[King Carter]]'s forces hold most of the captured resistance members in Books 2 and 3 of *[[The Runaways]]*.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Prison on an island |

## In the stories

In the possible Book 2 plot, while retrieving weapons from the Southern [[Misty Peaks]], Eric's expedition finds out that most of the resistance prisoners are held on an island prison. They return to [[Xi]], gather a force and attack the island, freeing some of the prisoners, among them [[Suew]], [[Nad]] and [[Scott Barker|Scott]].

## Open questions

- Where is the island, and what is it called?
- Is it the island the group escapes from in the draft campfire passage ("the day after they had escaped from the island and traveled north along the coast")?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Eric is captured during the Book 2 attack on the island. In Book 3, the full resistance and Xi force attacks the prison island again and frees the rest of the resistance prisoners before marching on [[Carthollow|Carthollow]].
