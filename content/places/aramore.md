**Aramore** is a region and kingdom of Satalyn with a dry prairie climate, the homeland of the [[peoples/cultures/aramore|Aramorean]] people and of [[Eric]], the protagonist of *[[The Runaways]]*.

## Geography

Aramore is prairie country, dry but not as dry as a desert. [[Colored Corn]], corn of various colours that can withstand the cold temperatures of the north, is listed as a plant of Aramore. The [[Aramore Forest]], which [[Eric]] and his companions cross in Book 1, shares the region's name.

## People and culture

- **Government:** a kingdom ruled by a king, whose successor is his oldest son. Each city is ruled by a court of nobles, larger or smaller depending on the size of the town. If a king dies with no successor, one of the nobles becomes king by popular vote, with points counted for each city.
- **Cities:** Tudor-style homes and buildings. Each city has a stone keep, with the noble homes inside the innermost wall around it. Larger cities have rings of stone walls: the further in, the richer the residents (usually lower nobles and merchants). Rich farmers live inside the outermost, shorter wall, and other farmers live outside it.
- **Skills:** horse breeding and riding, metalwork, storytelling, trade and dueling. Sport dueling and horse races are the biggest sporting events; townspeople gather in taverns to hear bards' stories and songs.
- **Religion:** an ancient faith in elemental spirits led by the [[Skyfather]], which almost no one follows any more. People once held festivals when the auroras appeared, but the auroras haven't been seen since the [[Desolation of the ground water|desolation of the ground water]] by the evil wizard.
- **Names:** Aramorean names draw on Breton, English, French, Frisian, German, Hungarian, Lithuanian, Polish, Romanian, Scottish, Celtic (mythology and ancient), Anglo-Saxon and Arthurian names.

See [[peoples/cultures/aramore|Aramorean culture]] for the full culture.

## Settlements

- [[Carthollow]], home of [[King Carter]], is an Aramorean city.

## History

During the [[Xi Empire]], a Xi soldier broke the Xi rule against mixing with foreigners and had a child with an Aramorian woman. [[Eric]] and [[Lucas]] descend from that line.

## In the stories

Eric and several of the people he meets in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] ([[Rannulfr]], [[Bardon]], [[Ulrich]] and [[The Torturer|the Torturer]]) are Aramorean.
