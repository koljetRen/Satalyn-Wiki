## In culture

[[osisu-people|Osisuans]] take their last names from something in their region when they choose their expertise, and a fisherman may be named **Stormshark** after this shark. [[Akio Stormshark]], the Osisuan fisherman turned mercenary in *[[The Runaways]]*, carries the name.
