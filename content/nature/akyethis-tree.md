The **Akyethis Tree** is a tall, strong tree of [[Aegryamis]] that thrives despite the desert's harsh climate and scarce water.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Tree |
| Region | [[Aegryamis]] |
| Properties | Grows tall and produces many branches despite a harsh climate with little water; strong enough to withstand sandstorms |

## Uses

The [[aegryamis-people|people of Aegryamis]] build their tall, skyscraper-like buildings from stone and Akyethis, so that they can preserve as much fertile land as possible.
