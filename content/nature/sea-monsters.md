**Sea monsters** are creatures of the tales told by coastal peoples.

## In legend

Coastal peoples tell tales of sea monsters. The sea belongs to [[Ozefio]], Goddess of the Seas: sailors pray to her for safe passage, and lost ships are said to have lost her favour.

## Open questions

- What do the sea monsters look like, and are there different kinds?
- Which coasts tell these tales?
- Are the sea monsters related to the [[Sacred Creatures]]?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Ozefio's pets live in the seas, and they are what inspire the coastal tales of sea monsters.
