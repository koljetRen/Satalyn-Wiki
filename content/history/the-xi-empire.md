**The Xi Empire** (350–430) was the period when the [[Xi people|Xi]], a warrior people from a small tropical island, crossed the sea and ruled part of the mainland's west coast before their council of elders chose to withdraw.

## Background

The Xi lived on a small tropical island that "no one has discovered". Their leaders, a council of 17 elderly men, heard rumours of a land much larger than their own and decided the Xi would go and live there instead.

## What happened

The Xi left their island and attacked the west coast. After a few decades the council decided it wasn't worth trying to maintain such a large kingdom, and many of the men had grown sad from not seeing their families. So the council of elders pulled out.

While the empire ruled, the Xi had a rule against mating with anyone foreign to Xi. Xi blood "doesn't mix with blood that is foreign to them": when it does, a child has a very low chance of surviving past birth.

## Aftermath

- **A hidden Xi bloodline.** One soldier broke the rule and had a child with an Aramorian woman. Her descendants always struggled to have children survive long, and for generations the line survived with only one surviving child. Then a woman had two children who lived: twin girls, separated early in life. Each had a single child — [[Lucas]] to the elder, [[Eric]] to the younger. See [[Xiramite]] for the other version of this story.
- **Xiramite.** A pocket of [[Xiramite]] in the [[Southern Misty Mountains]] dates from the Xi empire age; how it got there is unknown.
- **Ruins.** The author's location slides include "Xi City Ruins".

## Open questions

- The timeline gives the empire 80 years (350–430), but the Xi notes say the council pulled out "after a few decades". Did the conquest itself take part of that time?
- How was an island "that no one has discovered" also the seat of an empire on the mainland? Was it forgotten again after the withdrawal?
- Where are the Xi City Ruins — on Xi, or on the mainland coast?
- What does the year count (350, 1067…) start from?
