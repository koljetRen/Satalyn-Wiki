The **Battle of the Fort** is the large battle that opens *[[Stomped in the Dust|The Runaways Book 2: Stomped in the Dust]]*, in which [[King Carter's Forces|King Carter's forces]] defeat [[The Resistance|the resistance]] and take the city where it had taken refuge — by the Book 2 blurb, [[Keyesport Vale]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Between 1067 and 1069 (start of *The Runaways* Book 2) |
| Place | "The fort", in the city where the resistance sheltered — apparently [[Keyesport Vale]] |
| Sides | [[The Resistance]] against [[King Carter's Forces]] |
| Result | King Carter's forces take the city; nearly all the resistance killed or captured |

## Background

After [[Battle of Misty Phlats|Misty Phlats]], the resistance sailed from [[Moonwick]] to [[Keyesport Vale]], home of [[King Augo]], where it spent a few days before seeing ships on the horizon (end of Book 1).

## What happened

The Book 2 blurb: "King Carter's armies attack and capture the island and all of the men and women living there. Eric, now one of the leaders in the resistance, must find a way to escape the island and restart the force to dethrone King Carter."

The Book 2 plot outline gives the battle as "Large battle at the fort". All but a handful of the resistance were killed or captured, and King Carter's forces took over the city.

## Aftermath

About a week later, [[Eric]] saw a ship on the horizon and sent it a message. The ship came in asking for more water, as it had run out, and the remaining resistance members boarded it and travelled south to [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]]. Most of the captured resistance members were later found to be held on [[The Island Prison|an island prison]] (see [[Raids on the Island Prison]]). Eric tells the Xi about "the battle at the fort and Misty Phlats".

## Open questions

- Is the fort in Keyesport Vale? The plot outline says only "the fort" and "the city"; the blurb says Carter's armies capture "the island". Is Keyesport Vale an island?
- What happened to King Augo in the battle? (In Book 3 he is alive and meets Carter.)
