**The Runaways years** are 1067 to 1074, the span of the three *[[The Runaways|Runaways]]* books and their two spin-offs, from [[Eric]] waking in [[Carthollow Dungeon|King Carter's dungeon]] to the joining of the series.

## Overview

| Year | What happens |
| --- | --- |

| 1045 | (Background) An evil wizard starts attacking [[The Great City]] — see [[Dherlin's Attacks on the Great City]] |
| **1067** | *[[The Runaways Book 1]]* starts |

## 1067: Book 1

- Eric wakes in the dungeon of [[Carthollow]] with no memory. There he learns of [[The King's Trial]], a tournament in which prisoners fight with wooden swords for their freedom; Eric reaches the third round, and the tournament exposes him to [[Brian]].
- Eric escapes, reaches [[Harrisonville]], and flees with [[Jane Barker|Jane]] and others through the [[Aramore Forest]] to the [[Southern Misty Mountains]].
- At [[Misty Phlats]] he meets the resistance, trains as a soldier and, after the wizard [[Aerelm]] senses his magic, as a wizard.
- After a scouting trip to [[Shadow's Edge]], Eric forges [[Eric's Sword|his sword]] from a pocket of [[Xiramite]].
- **[[Battle of Misty Phlats]]**: King Carter's army attacks; the resistance escapes, regroups at [[Mourncliff]], and sails from [[Moonwick]] to [[Keyesport Vale]], where ships appear on the horizon.

> [!info] Canon note
> How Eric gets out of prison differs between notes. The older Book 1 plot (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`) has Brian free Eric and David after a few weeks and send them to Harrisonville. The April 2026 sheet (`sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md`) has [[Bardon]], a resistance member captured on purpose, team up with [[Rykor]] to break out Eric and [[Akio Stormshark|Aiko]] (Akio). Both sheets agree Carthollow (Carterville in older notes) is King Carter's city.

## 1067–1069: Book 2

- **[[Battle of the Fort]]**: King Carter's forces capture the city where the resistance sheltered; only a few escape.
- The survivors sail south to [[Quino]] in [[Cadnitz]], where the Xi warrior [[Rykor]] challenges them over their xiramite, then yields. They travel west to [[Xi]], are offered sanctuary, and spend one to two months learning Xi ways of fighting; [[Lucas]] connects with [[Lucas's Dagger|his dagger]].
- Eric leads an expedition back to the Southern Misty Mountains and learns that most captured resistance members are held on [[The Island Prison|an island prison]].
- **First of the [[Raids on the Island Prison]]**: some prisoners are freed.

## 1069/1070: Book 3

- The resistance soldiers on Xi train to fight like the Xi. Lucas becomes Jane's voice in leading the Xi and resistance army, since the Xi won't follow her.
- **Second raid on the island prison**: the rest of the prisoners are freed.
- **[[Battle of Carthollow]]** (Carterville): the final battle of the trilogy.
- Afterwards a small group sets out for [[Averill]] to end a threat (see secrets).

## 1071–1074: the spin-offs

- **1071:** the slave book begins. [[Peter Snyder]], a boy king overthrown by a member of his council, is sold to slavers crossing [[The Great Desert]], escapes, and joins a slave hunter on a mission to free all slaves. After two or three years of slave hunting they meet the group from *The Runaways*, and the slave hunters join them.
- **1072:** the wizard book begins (no details in the notes).
- **1074:** the *Runaways* and the spin-off books join together.

## Open questions

- Book 3 is dated "1069/1070". Does it start in 1069 and end in 1070?
- Where do the post-Book 3 events (the quest from Averill, the stay at [[Lucal]]) fall in 1070–1074? Are they a fourth book, or part of the joined series?
- The slave book starts in 1071, and its hero meets Eric after two or three years of slave hunting — i.e. around 1073–1074, matching the 1074 join?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Plot twists and later events, from `sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md` unless noted:
> - **Book 1:** David sends a report to the king about the resistance base from Shadow's Edge. At Misty Phlats Eric fireballs the whole valley, killing many resistance men too, and lies in a death-like state until Jane reaches him in a dream.
> - **Book 2:** Eric sees David among the enemy at the fort. In the first island raid Eric is captured, falls into depression and starts having flashbacks.
> - **Book 3:** Crisig frees Eric, who escapes with Brian, Kolt and Henri to Sprake in Dyken and calls Jane through a dream. Eric learns he killed his mother through uncontrolled magic, which is why he was imprisoned, and that Brian is his father. Jane and Eric share a first kiss. At Carthollow, David (now Carter's servant) dies saving Eric from Zandar, who tries to seize power; Elliott kills Zandar. Lucas cuts off Augo's hand when Augo tries to stab Carter. Carter stays king, Augo is imprisoned, and the resistance is disbanded. That night Dherlin, possessing Carter, tries to kill Eric; Eric destroys Dherlin's presence in Carter.
> - **After Book 3:** Eric and Jane marry before leaving for Averill. On the quest Jane becomes pregnant; Dherlin tries to curse the child, and Scott takes the curse instead and dies. Jane stays at [[Lucal]] while the rest go to defeat Dherlin. Eric defeats the evil wizard and returns; as they leave Lucal, Jane gives birth to a boy, named Scott after her brother. After a few years in Lucal they return to Carthollow for the rest of their lives.
> - The 2026 sheet's David "dies in chapter 1" (`sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md`) conflicts with every David event above.
