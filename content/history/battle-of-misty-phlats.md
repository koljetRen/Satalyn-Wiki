The **Battle of Misty Phlats** was the attack by [[King Carter's Forces|King Carter's army]] on [[The Resistance|the resistance]] at [[Misty Phlats]] in the [[Southern Misty Mountains]] in 1067, which the resistance narrowly escaped — the climax of *[[The Runaways Book 1]]*.

## Background

The resistance's base lay in the Southern Misty Mountains near Misty Phlats. Returning from a scouting and weapon-buying trip to [[Shadow's Edge]], [[Eric]]'s group found reports of the army marching their way. The resistance prepared to flee the base, but the army reached Misty Phlats quicker than expected.

## What happened

The resistance drew up a plan to escape the area and make for [[Keyesport Vale]]:

1. [[Samheir]] takes the frontal charge;
2. [[Xander]] and [[Elliott]] come in from the side to distract the main army;
3. meanwhile the rest of the resistance escapes through the back passes.

The battle started, and the resistance broke away. The details of the fighting are in the secrets below.

## Aftermath

One by one, the groups of resistance refugees reached the meeting point at [[Mourncliff]]. Once the whole resistance was together, it travelled to [[Moonwick]] and boarded ships to Keyesport Vale. The blurb of Book 2 opens: "After narrowly escaping the armies of King Carter at Misty Phlats, the resistance finds themselves at Keyesport Vale."

Eric later tells the Xi about the battle at Misty Phlats (Book 2), and has nightmares about it.
