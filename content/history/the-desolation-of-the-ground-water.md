**The desolation of the ground water** was a disaster caused by "the evil wizard", after which the auroras were no longer seen over [[Aramore]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Not recorded (before 1067) |
| Place | Not recorded; its effects were felt in [[Aramore]] |
| People involved | "The evil wizard" — presumably [[Dherlin]] |
| Result | The auroras have not been seen since |

## What happened

The only mention is in the notes on Aramorean religion: "the auroras haven't been seen since the desolation of the ground water from the evil wizard".

## Aftermath

The people of Aramore once held traditions and festivals when the auroras appeared, believing them to be the [[Skyfather]] blessing them through dance. The notes speak of those festivals in the past tense, and call Aramore's old spirit religion one "that no one seems to follow anymore".
