The **raids on the island prison** were two attacks by [[The Resistance|the resistance]] and its [[Xi]] allies on [[The Island Prison|the island prison]] where King Carter's forces held captured resistance members — the first in *The Runaways* Book 2, the second in Book 3.

## Background

After the [[Battle of the Fort]], most of the captured resistance members were taken to an island prison. [[Eric]] learned this while leading an expedition from [[Xi]] to the [[Southern Misty Mountains]] to recover the weapons from the "holding trees" and the old resistance base.

## The first raid (Book 2)

Eric returned to Xi and gathered a force to free the prisoners. They attacked the island and freed some of them, among them [[Suew]], [[Nad]] and [[Scott Barker|Scott]]. [[Jane Barker|Jane]] and [[Lucas]] led the force back to Xi.

## The second raid (Book 3)

With the resistance soldiers now trained to fight like the Xi, the combined Xi and resistance army attacked the prison island again at full force and freed the rest of the resistance prisoners. It then marched at full speed on [[Carthollow|Carthollow]] (see [[Battle of Carthollow]]).
