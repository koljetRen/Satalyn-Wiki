**The King's Trial** is a tournament set up by [[King Carter]] that lets prisoners fight for their freedom; [[Eric]] fights in it in the [[Carthollow Dungeon]] at the start of [[The Runaways Book 1]].

| | |

| Weapons | A wooden sword for each prisoner |
| Prize | The winner is promised freedom |
| Also called | "The Trial" (prisoners' name for it) |

## Rules

Each prisoner is given a wooden sword and fights until one is disarmed or yields. The bracket runs until the championship, and the winner is promised freedom. The prisoners call it "the Trial" for short.

## In The Runaways

Eric learns of the Trial after waking in the dungeon with no memory. He reaches the third round before he is defeated. The tournament is what exposes [[Brian]] to Eric.

[[Akio Stormshark]] has been in the Carthollow prison only a few months, but already has experience of the King's Trials from other prisons.
