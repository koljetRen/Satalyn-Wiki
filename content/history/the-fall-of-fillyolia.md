**The Fall of Fillyolia** was the collapse of the kingdom of [[Fillyolia]] some time before *[[The Runaways]]* begins, after which villages tried to build their own kingdoms and [[King Carter]] set out to reunite them.

| | |
| --- | --- |

| Place | [[Fillyolia]] |
| People involved | The royal family of Fillyolia; [[King Carter]]; [[King Augo]] |
| Result | Fillyolia broken up; King Carter's campaign to reunite the villages |

## What happened

The notes say little about the fall itself in the open: see the secrets below.

## Aftermath

After the fall, some villages tried to build up kingdoms of their own. King Carter sent his forces to try to reunite the villages, to make them strong against outside forces. [[Jane Barker]]'s village declined the offer from the leader of the force sent to it, and that leader attacked and ransacked the village. Jane's mother and older brother died, and Jane's family joined the resistance, believing King Carter had ordered the attack.

The resistance, led by [[Zandar]], says that Carter stole the throne from [[King Augo]] and that it only wants to put the rightful ruler back on it.

> [!info] Canon note
> King Augo's role differs between notes. The cast list (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-character-info-personal.md`) calls him "King of Keyesport Vale"; the character-development list in `sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md` calls him "Rightful ruler to the throne that King Carter sits on", and the plot-twist idea makes him heir to Fillyolia (see secrets). The Book 2 blurb calls the king of Keyesport Vale "King [Argo?]".

## Open questions

- When did Fillyolia fall? Is it connected to the evil wizard's attacks on the Great City from 1045 (see [[Dherlin's Attacks on the Great City]])? Was the Great City Fillyolia's capital?
- Is King Carter's kingdom (ruled from [[Carthollow]]) the successor of Fillyolia?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> From the "Crazy plot twisting idea" (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`) — an idea, not yet settled canon:
> - "No good guys in this war." Augo and Carter were brothers in line for the throne of Fillyolia, because their father was the former king's younger brother.
> - [[Dherlin]] attacked Fillyolia and killed the king and his entire family, making Augo the rightful ruler. Used to an easy life of nobility, Augo didn't want the responsibility and declined, passing the throne to Carter. Carter showed humility and took it, wanting to help rebuild after the disaster, and rebuilt the kingdom with his own hands.
> - Dherlin learned of this and cast a spell on Carter so he could possess him whenever he wanted. Carter began to suffer from "bipolar": possessed, he was power-hungry and rude; himself, he was humble.
> - Seeing Carter's growing power, Augo grew greedy and started rumours of a resistance. Zandar, "a nobody dreaming of power", offered to lead it and make it real in return for becoming Augo's second-hand man, intending to kill Augo once he had the throne. Augo and Xander spread the story that Carter had stolen the throne from Augo. With his life now threatened, Carter fought back in defence; when Dherlin's possession took over, he attacked to keep his seat.
> - Jane's village was ransacked on the orders of the local force leader, not King Carter (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`, "Jane's background").
> - The twist note says Augo and Carter were brothers; CLAUDE.md's list of known conflicts records "brother/cousin".
