The **timeline of Satalyn** runs from the [[Seven Phases of Creation|seven phases of creation]], through [[The Golden Age|the golden age]] and [[The Ice Age|the ice age]], to the [[The Xi Empire|Xi Empire]] of 350–430 and the years of *[[The Runaways]]*, 1067–1074. It gathers every dated or datable event in the notes on one page; each entry links to the page that holds the detail.

> [!note] How to read the dates
> Only the years from 350 onwards come from the author's own timeline. Everything before that is **relative**: the phases of creation have no length in years, and the ice age is measured only in spans ("about 1,000 years"). The notes don't say how the ice age lines up with the year count, or what year 1 counts from. Dates marked **(undated)** or **(relative)** are placed by the order of events, not by a year in the notes.

## Creation

[[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, builds [[Satalyn]] for [[Lebarka]], the Father of All, following the laws of the [[Book of Creation]]. Each phase "takes as long as the Supreme Creator decides": not millions of years, and not one literal day. No mortals existed to count the time, so in legend the phases are simply ages.

| Phase | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Light | (relative) First | The solar system forms: a young sun, the world, three bare moons and the other planets. |
| 2. Sky and waters | (relative) | A bubble of water bursts to leave the sky, and one ocean covers the world. Earthquakes; the first tectonic plates. |
| 3. Land | (relative) | The continents break through the ocean; the plates settle and drift, raising mountains. |
| 4. Climate and seasons | (relative) | The world is tilted and set spinning, giving seasons, winds, currents and weather. |
| 5. Life | (relative) | Plants and animals are made. [[Modingua]], the Mother of All, chooses five races — [[Humans]], [[Kurupal]], [[Elves]], [[Stoneskins]] and the [[Owl-Antlered Folk]] — and each starts from one homeland. |
| 6. The requests | (relative) | The gifts of [[The Nine]] are made real. This is [[The Golden Age]] (below). |
| 7. The Creator steps away | (relative) Begins with the great freeze | The Supreme Creator's influence becomes minimal to none. It begins with [[The Ice Age]] (below). |

## The golden age (phase six)

(relative — length not recorded)

- Lebarka gathers the requests of [[The Nine]], adds a few of his own, and brings them to the Supreme Creator, who makes them real.
- [[Magurkai]] visits 100 villagers, [[The First Cult|the first cult]], and gives them [[Magic|magic]]; the place of his visit becomes the [[Magic Holy Site|magic holy site]].
- [[Stomell]]'s three daughters are placed into the bare moons, becoming [[The Three Moons|the three moons]].
- [[Felderg]] colours the plant life into forests, deserts, tundra and snowy peaks.
- The other gifts — death ([[Dauviti]]), shipbuilding and fishing ([[Ozefio]]), reading and writing ([[Gehflaun]]), strength ([[Dreplagen]]), persuasion ([[Krarkur]]), sound and languages ([[Klist]]) — let mortals skip the hunter-gatherer stage. They farm and build the first cities at once.
- The world is mild, with no ice caps.

See [[The Golden Age]].

## The ice age (phase seven)

(relative — about 1,000 years, plus spread and retreat)

| Stage | How long | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The freeze spreads | 100–150 years | Ice sheets cover the north and south, slowly enough that people can flee. Fields freeze and the first cities are abandoned. |
| The ice age | About 1,000 years | Glaciers limit where people live; lower seas may open land bridges that later flood. The [[Ice Age People\|ice age people]] are a fallen civilisation, not cavemen. |
| The retreat | 150–200 years | The ice draws back to its present-day extent. |

During the ice age:

- the [[Elves]] are hit hard (by the modern day they are rare and secluded);
- the [[Stoneskins]] thrive and spread across the peaks;
- the [[Kurupal]] shelter below the mountains, the root of the [[Kurupal–Stoneskin Rivalry|Kurupal–stoneskin rivalry]].

As the ice retreats the ice age people scatter, and different peoples branch off from them. The races migrate and split into cultures — the ice age drove much of this migration. See [[The Ice Age]] and [[The Ice Age People]].

> [!note] The gap after the ice age
> The notes don't say how long passed between the end of the ice retreat and the year 350, or whether year 1 marks any event.

## Between the ice age and the modern era

(undated)

- **The fading of worship.** As people gathered into bigger cities, the old [[Worship Through Dance and Song|dance-and-song worship]] slowly faded. See [[Why Worship Faded]].
- **The spread of magic.** [[Magurkai]]'s bloodline spread so widely that he may now be the most present god (see [[The Gods in the Modern Day]]).

## The Xi Empire (350–430)

- **350:** the [[Xi]], a warrior people from a small tropical island, leave their island and attack the mainland's west coast, on the decision of their [[Xi Council of Elders|council of 17 elders]].
- **350–430:** the Xi rule part of the west coast. They forbid mating with anyone foreign to Xi.
- **By 430:** the council decides the kingdom is not worth keeping, and the Xi withdraw to their island. (The Xi notes say "after a few decades"; see the open questions on [[The Xi Empire]].)
- **From the empire's time:** a pocket of [[Xiramite]] in the [[Misty Peaks|Southern Misty Mountains]]; how it got there is unknown.

See [[The Xi Empire]].

## Open questions

- What does the year count start from? How many years passed between the end of the ice age (and the ice's retreat) and 350?
- How long did each phase of creation, and the golden age, last in years?
- When did Fillyolia fall, and when did the desolation of the ground water happen? Are either of them the same as the 1045 attacks on the Great City?
- When did the resistance form, and when did King Carter begin his reunification?
- Book 3 is dated "1069/1070": does it start in 1069 and end in 1070? Where do the events after Book 3 fall in 1070–1074?
- The slave book's hero meets the Runaways group "after 2 or 3 years of slave hunting", which would be about 1073–1074 if he joins the hunters in 1071. Is that the 1074 join?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> - **Phase seven:** the Nine didn't know that every world's seventh phase begins with a great freeze, so they planned their gifts for a mild world about to freeze. Lebarka almost certainly knew and didn't warn them (`sources/claude-doc/world-creation-notes.md`).
> - **The fall of Fillyolia (plot-twist idea):** [[Dherlin]] attacked Fillyolia and killed the king and his entire family. Augo, the rightful heir, declined the throne and passed it to Carter, who rebuilt the kingdom. Dherlin then cast a spell to possess Carter, and Augo and Zandar started the resistance on the story that Carter stole the throne. If this stands, the fall of Fillyolia is one of Dherlin's attacks, and it comes before the resistance and the reunification (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`).
> - **Before 1067:** Eric killed his mother through uncontrolled magic, which is why he was imprisoned (`sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md`).
> - **1067:** David sends the king a report on the resistance base from Shadow's Edge (older plot). In the 2026 sheet David instead "dies in chapter 1" in the dungeon (`sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md`).
> - **1069/1070:** Eric, captured in the first island raid, escapes with Brian, Kolt and Henri to Sprake, learns Brian is his father, and at Carterville Zandar's betrayal ends with Elliott killing him. Carter stays king, Augo is imprisoned, and Eric destroys Dherlin's presence in Carter.
> - **Between 1070 and 1074 (undated):** Eric and Jane marry and set out from Averill to hunt Dherlin; Scott dies taking Dherlin's curse on Jane's unborn child; Jane waits at Lucal while Eric defeats Dherlin; their son Scott is born; after a few years in Lucal they return to Carterville. The notes don't say which years or book this belongs to.
