**The Golden Age** is the mild, gentle age of phase six of creation, when the gifts of [[The Nine]] were made real and the first mortals skipped straight to farming and building cities, before the great freeze of [[The Ice Age]].

## Background

In phase five, the Supreme Creator, [[Euiklatz]], made simple plants and animals and placed the humanoid races that [[Modingua]], the Mother of All, asked for. Phase six, "the requests", followed.

## What happened

[[Lebarka]], the Father of All, asked the Nine for their requests, added a few of his own, and brought them to the Supreme Creator, who made them real. This phase covers every request other than life itself. Among them:

- **Magic** was given to its first cult (see [[Magic]]).
- **The moons:** [[Stomell]]'s three daughters were placed into the three bare moons.
- **The landscapes:** [[Felderg]] coloured and shaped the simple plant life into forests, deserts, tundra and snowy peaks.
- **The other gifts** of the Nine: death ([[Dauviti]]), shipbuilding and fishing ([[Ozefio]]), plants and farming (Felderg), reading, writing and study ([[Gehflaun]]), strength, speed and mobility ([[Dreplagen]]), persuasion and cunning ([[Krarkur]]), sound, music and languages ([[Klist]]), and magic ([[Magurkai]]). See each god's page for the gift and its flaw.

Each gift pushed mortals far ahead, so they skipped the hunter-gatherer stage and started farming and building cities right away. Each gift also carried a hidden flaw its god didn't foresee, and because the Nine didn't coordinate, some gifts clashed, like Ozefio's ships and Stomell's storms.

The world was "still mild and gentle, a golden age". There were no ice caps at the end of phase six.

## Aftermath

The Nine came from the lowest caste of gods and didn't know that every world's seventh phase begins with a great freeze. They planned their gifts for a mild world that was about to freeze, which is where many of their mistakes come from. The first cities rose — then the freeze hit, fields froze over and the early cities were abandoned (see [[The Ice Age]]).
