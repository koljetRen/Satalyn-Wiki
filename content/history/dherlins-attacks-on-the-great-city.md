**Dherlin's attacks on the Great City** began in 1045, when an evil wizard started attacking [[The Great City]] — the earliest event of the modern era in the *[[The Runaways|Runaways]]* timeline after [[The Xi Empire]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Date | From 1045 |
| Place | [[The Great City]] |
| People involved | An evil wizard — taken to be [[Dherlin]] |
| Result | Not recorded |

## What happened

The timeline of the Runaways world has one line for this: "1045 – Evil wizard starts attacking Great City."

The timeline doesn't name the wizard. [[Dherlin]] is the only evil wizard in the notes — "evil wizard who is thought to be dead" — so the attacks are listed here under his name.

## Aftermath

By 1067, when *The Runaways* begins, Dherlin is thought to be dead.
