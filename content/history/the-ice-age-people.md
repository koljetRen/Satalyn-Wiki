**The ice age people** are the mortals who lived through [[The Ice Age]] — a fallen civilisation with naturally tough skin, and the ancestors of later peoples, who have since faded into legend.

## Who they were

Mortals existed during the ice age and are the ancestors of later peoples. They were not cavemen: the gifts of [[The Golden Age|phase six]] had already let the first cities rise, and when the freeze hit, fields froze and those early cities were abandoned. The ice age people are a fallen civilisation.

## Appearance

They had naturally tougher skin, and possibly stockier builds, to suit the cold.

## Life in the ice age

Glaciers limited where they could live. Lower sea levels may have opened land bridges that later flooded, a source of legends about lost homelands and drowned cities.

## After the ice

As the ice retreated they scattered, and different peoples branched off from them.

## In the modern day

They have faded into legend, but traces remain:

- northern families with tough skin;
- ruins in old glacier valleys;
- folk tales of frozen-age giants;
- scholars arguing over whether they were real.

## Ideas and open threads

- They could be the origin of dwarves or goliaths.
- They may remember a warm golden age before the ice.
- [[The First Cult|The first cult]] of magic may have been an ice age people, which would put the oldest bloodlines in that era.

## Open questions

- Which of the five races were they — one, or several? (The notes call them simply "mortals".)
- Where were their refuges?
