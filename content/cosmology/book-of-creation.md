The **Book of Creation** is the Supreme Creator's book of the natural laws that all creation follows. It belongs to [[Euiklatz]], and every world in [[The Multiverse|the multiverse]], [[Satalyn]] included, is made by its rules.

## What it says

- The natural laws in the Book roughly match how Earth formed.
- The Book sets out [[Seven Phases of Creation|seven phases]] that every world goes through, a blend of Earth's natural history and the seven days of Genesis.
- By the rules of the Book, every world's seventh phase begins with a great freeze. On Satalyn this is the [[Ice Age]].

## Who knew its rules

The great freeze is common knowledge at the rank of beings who rule worlds. [[The Nine]] came from the lowest caste and did not know this rule, so they planned their gifts for a mild world that was about to freeze. This is where many of their mistakes come from. [[Lebarka]], the Father of All, almost certainly knew, since he had asked for a world and would have seen the rules in action.
