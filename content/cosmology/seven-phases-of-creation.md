The **seven phases of creation** are the stages that every world goes through, as set out in the [[Book of Creation]]. They are a blend of Earth's natural history and the seven days of Genesis. On [[Satalyn]], [[Euiklatz]] the Supreme Creator built the world through them at the request of [[Lebarka]], the Father of All.

| Phase | Name | Who shapes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Light | The Supreme Creator alone |
| 2 | Sky and waters | The Supreme Creator alone |
| 3 | Land | The Supreme Creator alone |
| 4 | Climate and seasons | The Supreme Creator alone |
| 5 | Life | The Supreme Creator with the Father of All; races chosen by the Mother of All |
| 6 | The requests | The Supreme Creator, making real the requests of the Nine and the Father of All |
| 7 | The Creator steps away | — (begins with the great freeze) |

## Timing

Each phase takes as long as the Supreme Creator decides. It is not millions of years, and it is not one literal day. No mortals existed to count the time, so in legend the phases are simply ages.

**No phase creates the stars.** They already exist: each star is the creation of another being of the Father of All's caste who asked the Supreme Creator for a world of their own (see [[The Multiverse]]).

**Phases 1 to 4** are built by the Supreme Creator alone. The Father of All's only input is where things are placed.

## Phase 1: Light

The whole solar system forms. A young sun is lit, and the world, three bare moons (later [[The Three Moons]]) and the other planets pull together from the same dust and fire.

## Phase 2: Sky and waters

The Supreme Creator wraps the bare rock world in a great bubble of water. The bubble bursts, leaving behind the sky and atmosphere, and its water pours down until one ocean covers the whole surface. The rock is still settling, so the world shakes with earthquakes. The tectonic plates begin to form, and the first landmasses start to take shape.

## Phase 3: Land

The land breaks through the ocean and the continents take shape. The constant earthquakes die down as the tectonic plates settle into large plates. The world is still active, though: the big plates now drift slowly, colliding and raising mountains.

## Phase 4: Climate and seasons

The Creator tilts the world and sets its spin, which gives it seasons, winds, ocean currents and weather patterns, and so its climates.

## Phase 5: Life

For the first time, the Supreme Creator asks the Father of All what changes he wants, and they fine-tune the land together. Following his rules for what lives in each climate, the Creator makes simple plant life and animals. Then he makes the humanoid races that [[Modingua]], the Mother of All, asks for, and places them around the world.

Five races were placed in this phase: [[Humans]], the [[Kurupal]], [[Elves]], [[Stoneskins]] and the [[Owl-antlered folk]]. Each starts from one homeland, then migrates and splits into cultures as it spreads.

## Phase 6: The requests

The Father of All asks [[The Nine]] for their requests, adds a few of his own, and brings them to the Supreme Creator, who makes them real. This phase covers every request other than life itself.

- [[Magic]] is given to its first cult in this phase, by [[Magurkai]].
- [[Stomell]]'s three daughters are placed into the bare moons, becoming [[The Three Moons]].
- [[Felderg]] colours and shapes the simple plant life into forests, deserts, tundra and snowy peaks.

Each gift pushes mortals far ahead, so they skip the hunter-gatherer stage and start farming and building cities right away. Each gift also has a hidden flaw its god didn't foresee. The world is still mild and gentle: this is the [[Golden Age]].

## Phase 7: The Creator steps away

The Supreme Creator's influence on the world becomes minimal to none. By the rules of the Book, every world's seventh phase begins with a great freeze, so the [[Ice Age]] arrives right after life does.

- There are no ice caps at the end of phase six.
- Once the freeze begins, ice sheets spread over the north and south within 100 to 150 years: fast, but slow enough that people can flee rather than die instantly.
- The ice age lasts roughly 1,000 years, far shorter than Earth's, because it is a designed part of creation.
- After it ends, the ice takes another 150 to 200 years to retreat to its present-day extent.

The first cities rise in phase six, then the freeze hits. Fields freeze over and early cities are abandoned, so the [[Ice Age People|ice age people]] are a fallen civilisation, not cavemen.

## Ideas and open threads

- The Father of All adds "a few" requests of his own in phase six; which ones is still to be filled in from the author's notes on the gods.

## Open questions

- Which requests in phase six were the Father of All's own?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> The Nine came from the lowest caste and didn't know that every seventh phase begins with a great freeze, so they planned their gifts for a mild world that was about to freeze; this is where many of their mistakes come from. The Father of All almost certainly knew. He didn't warn them partly because he assumed they already knew (the freeze is common knowledge at his rank), and partly as a quiet test of whether they could really rise to a higher caste.
