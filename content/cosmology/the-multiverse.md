**The multiverse** is the whole of creation, ruled by [[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, with a caste system of lesser supreme beings beneath him. [[Satalyn]] is one of the worlds in it.

## The castes

Beneath the Supreme Creator, the lesser supreme beings are ranked in castes:

| Caste | Who they are |
| --- | --- |
| Supreme Creator | [[Euiklatz]], the God of the Eternities. Rules the multiverse and is the only one who builds anything. |
| Rulers of galaxies | Beings who rule galaxies. |
| Rulers of worlds | Beings who rule worlds, such as [[Lebarka]], the Father of All. |
| The lowest caste | Beings with little power or standing. [[The Nine]] came from this caste. |

## Asking for a world

A being who becomes prestigious enough may ask the Supreme Creator for a world of their own. His one condition is that the world's people must worship him as the creator.

Every world is made by the same natural laws, set down in the [[Book of Creation]], and goes through the same [[Seven Phases of Creation|seven phases]]. The stars in Satalyn's sky are not made in any of Satalyn's phases: each star is the creation of another being of the Father of All's caste who asked for a world of their own.

## How Satalyn was made

[[Lebarka]], the God of Life, asked for a world, and the Supreme Creator granted him an empty region of space. The Supreme Creator also gave him a partner, [[Modingua]], the Mother of All. The Father and Mother then lifted nine deities from the lowest caste, [[The Nine]], giving them a chance they would never otherwise have had.

Only the Supreme Creator builds. The Father of All passes requests up the chain on behalf of himself and the other gods. See [[Seven Phases of Creation]].

## Open questions

- Do mortals on Satalyn know about the castes, or only the creation story told at the [[Creation Festival]]?
