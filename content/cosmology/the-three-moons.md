**The three moons** of [[Satalyn]] are the three daughters of [[Stomell]], God of the Skies.

## Origin

The three moons formed as bare moons in phase one of the [[Seven Phases of Creation|seven phases of creation]], alongside the world, the sun and the other planets. In phase six, Stomell's three daughters were placed into the bare moons.
