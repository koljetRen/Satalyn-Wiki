**Klist**, Goddess of Music, is one of [[The Nine]]: the goddess who gave the world sound, including creatures' voices, music in the wind and thunder, and languages.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Music and sound |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Sound, music and languages |
| Flaw | Words can also wound and spread lies |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Klist gave the world sound, including creatures' voices, music in the wind and thunder, and languages. She inspires people to make instruments.

## The flaw

Words can also wound and spread lies, which hands [[Krarkur]] his best tool.

## Worship

She is a natural fit for [[Worship Through Dance and Song|dance-and-song worship]].

## Ideas and open threads

- Idea: language lets everyone cooperate from the start.
