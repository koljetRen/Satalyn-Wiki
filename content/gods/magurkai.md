**Magurkai**, God of Magic, is one of [[The Nine]]: the deity from whom all [[Magic|magic]] comes, who visited 100 people from a village and gave them magic.

| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

In phase six of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], Magurkai personally visited 100 people from a village, a small cult, and gave them magic. This was a rare event, since the gods are distant. The site of that visit is a holy site (see [[Magic Holy Site]]), possibly with a city that grew up around it.

The villagers draw on the iron in their blood to cast, so he changed their bodies to store and produce more iron. Only they and their descendants can wield magic (see [[Bloodline Magic]]). The purer the bloodline, the more iron the blood can hold, and so the greater the magic. As the line thins, magic weakens until descendants can't cast at all, though rarely a dormant trait resurfaces.

## The flaw

[[Overcasting]] drains iron and can cause fainting or death, and the bloodline splits the world into the magic-born and everyone else.

## In the modern day

Magurkai may have the most influence of any god today, because his bloodline has spread so widely. He went from touching one tiny cult to being the most present god. See [[The Gods in the Modern Day]].

## Ideas and open threads

- This reversal could stir up jealousy among the Nine.
- The first cult may have been an [[Ice Age People|ice age people]], which would put the oldest bloodlines in that era.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways notes (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) say "the deity from which all magic originals [originates]" and that the first wizards got their magic "from worshiping him". They also describe magic as passed down by DNA, with each wielder's capacity based on their ancestors' ability, drawn from one of four elements (air, earth, water, fire) and replenished by physical and mental rest. These details may still be compatible with the iron-in-the-blood model.

## Open questions

- Did the first wizards gain magic by worshipping Magurkai (older note), or did he choose a village and visit it (2026)? Possibly both.
