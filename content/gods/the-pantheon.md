**The pantheon** is the worldwide religion of [[Satalyn]]: twelve deities known everywhere, made up of the Supreme Creator, the Father of All, the Mother of All and [[The Nine]]. It does not have a name yet.

## How the gods relate to mortals

- The gods are mostly **distant**. They designed the world but never built it with their own hands; only the Supreme Creator builds.
- Each god keeps a small amount of power, and only inside their own domain. The god of love can't end a drought.
- The Supreme Creator, Father and Mother are honoured everywhere, in a formal, distant way.
- The Nine are worshipped by region, as in the Greek pantheon. Some places worship one god above the others, and some form cults for these gods.
- Everyday worship takes the form of [[Worship Through Dance and Song|dance and song]].
- Each race tends to picture the gods in its own image, while the [[Sacred Creatures|sacred creatures]] and shared symbols are common to every culture.

Other, lesser deities exist too, worshipped by small cults or indigenous peoples, but only these twelve are known worldwide. See [[Lesser and Regional Gods]].
