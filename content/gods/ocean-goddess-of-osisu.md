The **ocean goddess of Osisu** is the goddess worshipped by the [[Osisu people|Osisu]] island people, represented on the islands by the queen shaman.

## Worship

- The women of Osisu are the religious leaders. Women are recognised as sacred and each is treated as royalty.
- The **queen shaman** represents Osisu's goddess. She picks the chief of each island, and the chiefs she selects are believed to be chosen by the goddess. The chiefs meet together to lead Osisu as a whole.
- The queen shaman is chosen from all the shamans of the islands, who meet together and, with the help of their goddess, determine the new queen shaman. She lives on the most active volcanic island.
- Each island is led by a chief and shaman pair. Each island shaman may appoint one of the single young women of the island as her apprentice and the future shaman.
- Men get tattoos to show their calling in life and their achievements.
