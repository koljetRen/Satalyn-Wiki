**Dreplagen**, God of Strength, is one of [[The Nine]]: the god who gave mortals the ability to build strength, speed and mobility.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Strength |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Building strength, speed and mobility |
| Flaw | Strength led to the first conflicts and wars; bodies pushed hard wear down |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Dreplagen believes strength beats intelligence. He gave mortals the ability to build strength, speed and mobility, working with [[Felderg]] on food and nourishment.

## The flaw

Strength let people take what they wanted, leading to the first conflicts and wars. He also didn't realise that bodies pushed hard would wear down with injury and exhaustion. [[Krarkur]] later corrupted the gift of strength, as he did [[Gehflaun]]'s gift of intelligence.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) gives him "power to all physical strength".
