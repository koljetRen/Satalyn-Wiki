**Krarkur**, God of Deceit, is one of [[The Nine]]: the god of persuasion, negotiation and cunning, who corrupted the gifts of intelligence and strength.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Deceit |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Persuasion, negotiation and cunning |
| Flaw | His gift makes lies possible |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Krarkur's gift is persuasion, negotiation and cunning: the skills behind trade, diplomacy and leadership, which also make lies possible.

He corrupted the gifts of [[Gehflaun]] (intelligence) and [[Dreplagen]] (strength), tempting people into social tiers and spreading hate and competition. He twisted intelligence into castes and discrimination, which is felt most strongly among the [[Dyken]]. [[Klist]]'s gift of words, which can wound and spread lies, hands him his best tool.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) calls Krarkur "God of War", with "power to all who are a part of war". The 2026 notes make him the God of Deceit.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Like [[Dauviti]], Krarkur came from the top of the lowest caste and knew exactly what he was doing.
