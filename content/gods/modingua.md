**Modingua**, the **Mother of All**, is the Goddess of Motherhood, love, fertility and reproduction, who decides which humanoid races are placed on [[Satalyn]]. She is the partner of [[Lebarka]], the Father of All, and one of the twelve deities of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]].

## Domain and gift

[[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, gave Modingua to the Father of All as his partner. Love belongs to Modingua, not to one of [[The Nine]].

In phase five of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], the Supreme Creator made the humanoid races she asked for and placed them around the world. She chose five: [[Humans]], the [[Kurupal]], [[Elves]], [[Stoneskins]] and the [[Owl-antlered folk]].

She loves every race equally. Tensions between races come from life in the world, not from divine favouritism.

## Worship

Honoured everywhere, in a formal, distant way, with dances shared by the whole world (see [[Worship Through Dance and Song]]).

## In the modern day

Modingua wants to be more involved. The world is still medieval and most people marry and start families young, but a few races may be marrying later or having fewer children, which saddens her. This is still open. See [[The Gods in the Modern Day]].

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) calls her "Goddess of Mothership", almost certainly a typo for Motherhood, with the note "Mother of All".
