**Euiklatz**, the God of the Eternities, is the Supreme Creator who rules over [[The Multiverse|the multiverse]]. He is one of the twelve deities of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]] known worldwide on [[Satalyn]].

## Domain and role

Beneath Euiklatz are lesser supreme beings in a caste system (see [[The Multiverse]]). A being who becomes prestigious enough may ask him for a world of their own. His one condition: its people must worship him as the creator.

All creation follows the natural laws in his [[Book of Creation]]. Only the Supreme Creator builds anything; the other gods pass their requests to him.

## The creation of Satalyn

[[Lebarka]], the God of Life, asked Euiklatz for a world, and Euiklatz granted him an empty region of space. He also gave Lebarka a partner, [[Modingua]], the Mother of All.

Euiklatz built the world alone in phases one to four of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], asked Lebarka what changes he wanted in phase five, made the requests of [[The Nine]] real in phase six, and stepped away in phase seven, when his influence on the world became minimal to none.

## Worship

The Supreme Creator, the Father and the Mother are honoured everywhere, in a formal, distant way. The whole world shares dances for them (see [[Worship Through Dance and Song]]). At the [[Creation Festival]] the worship leader sings of the Father of All passing each request to the Supreme Creator.
