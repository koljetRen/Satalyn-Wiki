**Dauviti**, God of the Dead, is one of [[The Nine]]: the god who gave every creature the ability to die.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | The dead |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Death |
| Flaw | None in truth; mortals see death itself as his flaw |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Dauviti gave every creature the ability to die. Mortals plead for mercy in the afterlife, so each culture's beliefs about death have a chance of coming true.

## The flaw

He has no real flaw: death is necessary. Mortals see creating death as his flaw.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) calls him "God of the Underworld", with "regards to all who have experience with death".

## Open questions

- What does the afterlife look like, and how does pleading for mercy work?

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> Dauviti came from the top of the lowest caste, understood the rules of the [[Book of Creation]], and knew exactly what he was doing when he chose death.
