The **brother gods of Aegryamis** are two brother gods who were given the desert of [[Aegryamis]] by the other gods as a punishment, and whom the Aegryamis people worship.

## Story

With only one fertile river in the desert, the two brothers went from bitter enemies to allies. To determine who leads the people, they decided on the dual monarchy: Aegryamis has two royal families, one for each town on opposite sides of the river, and the ruling family switches between them with every king.

## Worship

Each ruling family has a trusted advisor, who serves as the priest and mouthpiece of one of the brothers. Aegryamis priests wear silk robes with gold edging. Domes mark places of importance in Aegryamis cities, temples among them.
