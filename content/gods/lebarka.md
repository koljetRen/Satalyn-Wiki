**Lebarka**, the God of Life, is the **Father of All**: the god who asked for [[Satalyn]] and was granted it by [[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator. He is one of the twelve deities of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]].

## Domain and role

Lebarka asked the Supreme Creator for a world, and was granted an empty region of space. He calls himself the Father of All. The Supreme Creator also gave him a partner, Modingua.

Lebarka does not build. He passes requests up the chain to the Supreme Creator, on behalf of himself and the other gods. Together with Modingua he lifted [[The Nine]] from the lowest caste.

## In the seven phases

- **Phases 1 to 4:** his only input is where things are placed.
- **Phase 5:** for the first time the Supreme Creator asks him what changes he wants, and they fine-tune the land together.
- **Phase 6:** he gathers the Nine's requests, adds a few of his own, and brings them to the Supreme Creator.

See [[Seven Phases of Creation]].

## Worship

Honoured everywhere, in a formal, distant way, with dances shared by the whole world. At the [[Creation Festival]] the worship leader sings of him passing each request to the Supreme Creator.
