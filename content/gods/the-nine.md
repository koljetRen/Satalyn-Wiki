**The Nine** are the nine deities that [[Lebarka]], the Father of All, and [[Modingua]], the Mother of All, lifted from the lowest caste of [[The Multiverse|the multiverse]] to help shape [[Satalyn]], giving them a chance they would never otherwise have had.

## The choice of domains

Each of the Nine could pick **one domain**: the single thing they believed would give them the most power. Some chose badly. Their flawed choices can explain flaws in the world and rivalry among the gods. Names may change, and each god is known by different names in different regions.

| # | God | Domain | Gift to mortals | Flaw |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[Dauviti]] | The dead | Death | None in truth; mortals see death as his flaw |
| 2 | [[Stomell]] | The skies | Predictable weather; his daughters became [[The Three Moons]] | People trust the patterns too much |
| 3 | [[Ozefio]] | The seas | Shipbuilding and fishing | Forgot Stomell's storms rule the sea |
| 4 | [[Felderg]] | The earth (perhaps the harvest) | Plants and farming; coloured the plant life | Poisonous plants |
| 5 | [[Gehflaun]] | Intelligence | Reading, writing and study | Knowledge can divide people |
| 6 | [[Dreplagen]] | Strength | Strength, speed and mobility | War; bodies wear down |
| 7 | [[Krarkur]] | Deceit | Persuasion, negotiation and cunning | Makes lies possible |
| 8 | [[Klist]] | Music | Sound, music and languages | Words can wound and spread lies |
| 9 | [[Magurkai]] | Magic | Magic, through a bloodline | Overcasting; magic-born vs everyone else |

Love belongs to [[Modingua]], the Mother of All, not one of the Nine.

## Phase six: the requests

In phase six of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], the Father of All asked the Nine for their requests and brought them to [[Euiklatz]], the Supreme Creator, who made them real.

Each gift pushes mortals far ahead, so they skip the hunter-gatherer stage and start farming and building cities right away. Each gift also has a hidden flaw its god didn't foresee. The Nine didn't coordinate, so some gifts clash, like Ozefio's ships and Stomell's storms.

## Worship

The Nine are worshipped by region, as in the Greek pantheon. Each region has its own dances for its favourite god (see [[Worship Through Dance and Song]]). Each god keeps a small amount of power, and only inside their own domain: the god of love can't end a drought.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> The Nine came from the lowest caste and didn't know that every world's seventh phase begins with a great freeze, so they planned their gifts for a mild world that was about to freeze. This is where many of their mistakes come from. The Father of All didn't warn them, partly assuming they knew and partly as a quiet test of whether they could rise to a higher caste. [[Dauviti]] and [[Krarkur]] came from the top of the lowest caste and knew exactly what they were doing.
