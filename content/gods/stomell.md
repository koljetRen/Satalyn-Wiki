**Stomell**, God of the Skies, is one of [[The Nine]]: the god of the weather, whose three daughters became the world's three moons.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | The skies and weather |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Predictable weather patterns |
| Flaw | People trust the patterns so completely that they are caught out when the weather breaks |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Stomell controls the weather but kindly keeps it to predictable patterns, usually. In phase six of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], his three daughters were placed into the bare moons and became [[The Three Moons]].

## The flaw

People trust his patterns so completely that they are caught out when the weather breaks. His weather also rules the sea, which [[Ozefio]] did not realise when she gave mortals ships, so early sailors were lost to storms.

## Ideas and open threads

- Idea: he gave mortals knowledge of the seasons and stars for planting and navigation.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) describes him as one who "controls the weather in the sky".
