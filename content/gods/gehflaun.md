**Gehflaun**, Goddess of Intelligence, is one of [[The Nine]]: the goddess who gave mortals reading, writing and study.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Intelligence |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Reading, writing and study |
| Flaw | Believed knowledge would simply make everyone happy |
| Worshipped most by | The [[Dyken]] people |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Gehflaun gave mortals reading, writing and study.

## The flaw

She innocently believed knowledge would simply make everyone happy, and never imagined it could divide people. That left the opening for [[Krarkur]], who twisted intelligence into castes and discrimination. This is felt across the world, but most strongly among the Dyken, whose society relies on intelligence.

## Worship

Gehflaun is central to worship among the Dyken. Dyken temples are also schools, and their priests are teachers and philosophers (see [[Dyken Temples]]).

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) gives her "power to all learning".
