**Ozefio**, Goddess of the Seas, is one of [[The Nine]]: the goddess who gave mortals shipbuilding and fishing.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | The seas |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Shipbuilding and fishing |
| Flaw | Didn't realise the sea is ruled by Stomell's weather |
| Worshipped most by | Sailors |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

Ozefio gave mortals shipbuilding and fishing. Her pets live in the seas and inspire coastal tales of [[Sea Monsters|sea monsters]].

## The flaw

She didn't realise the sea is ruled by [[Stomell]]'s weather, so early sailors were lost to storms. The Nine didn't coordinate their gifts, and hers and Stomell's clash.

## Worship

Sailors pray to her for safe passage. Lost ships are said to have lost her favour. Dances for the water god use flowing, wave-like movements (see [[Worship Through Dance and Song]]).

## Ideas and open threads

- A water-dwelling people, such as possible [[Merfolk|merfolk]], would favour the water god, who may have chosen where the first merfolk lived.

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways god list (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) calls Ozefio "God of the Seas", male, who "controls all that happens in and on the sea". The 2026 notes make Ozefio a goddess.
