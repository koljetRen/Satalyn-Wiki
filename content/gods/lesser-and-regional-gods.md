**Lesser and regional gods** are the deities and spirits of [[Satalyn]] outside the twelve of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]], worshipped by small cults, indigenous peoples or single cultures.

## Overview

Only the twelve gods of the pantheon are known worldwide. Other lesser deities exist too, worshipped by small cults or indigenous peoples. Beside the worldwide pantheon, the regional faiths in the notes are:

| Faith | People | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two brother gods given the desert as a punishment | [[Aegryamis]] | [[Brother Gods of Aegryamis]] |
| An ocean goddess, represented by the queen shaman | [[Osisu]] | [[Ocean Goddess of Osisu]] |
| Elemental spirits led by the Skyfather | [[Aramore]] and [[Skarg]] | [[Skyfather and the Elemental Spirits]] |
| Ancestors who watch over the people | [[Xi]] | [[Xi Ancestor Worship]] |

> [!info] Canon note
> The older Runaways notes (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) say that besides the worldwide pantheon "there are 2 other major religions. One found in Aegyramis and the other scattered around the world, but mainly in the mountains and areas separated from the main population." The first matches the [[Brother Gods of Aegryamis]]. The notes don't say which faith the second, mountain religion is.

## Open questions

- Which faith is the scattered, mostly mountain religion from the older notes?
- Are the Osisu ocean goddess and the Aegryamis brother gods lesser deities, or regional names for gods of the pantheon?
