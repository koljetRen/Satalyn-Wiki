**Felderg**, God of the Earth, is one of [[The Nine]]: an artist who coloured the world's plant life and gave mortals the knowledge of plants and farming.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | The earth (the author is leaning towards the harvest) |
| Rank | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| Gift to mortals | Knowledge of plants and farming |
| Flaw | Didn't think about poisonous plants |
| Sacred creature | One, rumoured (see [[Sacred Creatures]]) |

## Domain and gift

In phase six of the [[Seven Phases of Creation]], Felderg coloured and shaped the simple plant life into forests, deserts, tundra and snowy peaks. He gave mortals the knowledge of plants and farming, and worked with [[Dreplagen]] on food and nourishment.

## The flaw

He didn't think about poisonous plants, so people sometimes harmed themselves by accident.

## Ideas and open threads

- The author is now leaning towards making him a God of the Harvest.
