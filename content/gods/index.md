The twelve deities known worldwide — [[Euiklatz]] the Supreme Creator, [[Lebarka]] the Father of All, [[Modingua]] the Mother of All, and [[The Nine]] — plus the lesser gods of small cults and regions.

## The pantheon

The twelve deities of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]] at a glance. See each god's page for detail.

| God | Title and domain | Gift to mortals | Flaw | Rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Euiklatz]] | Supreme Creator; God of the Eternities | Builds every world, including Satalyn | — | Supreme Creator, above every caste |
| [[Lebarka]] | Father of All; God of Life | Asked for the world; brings the gods' requests to the Supreme Creator | — | A ruler of worlds |
| [[Modingua]] | Mother of All; Goddess of Motherhood, love, fertility and reproduction | Chose the five humanoid races | — | Partner of the Father of All |
| [[Dauviti]] | God of the Dead | Death | None in truth; mortals see death itself as his flaw | One of the Nine, lifted from the lowest caste |
| [[Stomell]] | God of the Skies (skies and weather) | Predictable weather; his daughters became [[The Three Moons]] | People trust the patterns too much and are caught out when the weather breaks | One of the Nine |
| [[Ozefio]] | Goddess of the Seas | Shipbuilding and fishing | Forgot that Stomell's weather rules the sea | One of the Nine |
| [[Felderg]] | God of the Earth (perhaps the harvest) | Knowledge of plants and farming; coloured the plant life | Didn't think about poisonous plants | One of the Nine |
| [[Gehflaun]] | Goddess of Intelligence | Reading, writing and study | Believed knowledge would simply make everyone happy; it can divide people | One of the Nine |
| [[Dreplagen]] | God of Strength | Strength, speed and mobility | Strength led to the first wars; bodies pushed hard wear down | One of the Nine |
| [[Krarkur]] | God of Deceit | Persuasion, negotiation and cunning | His gift makes lies possible | One of the Nine |
| [[Klist]] | Goddess of Music (music and sound) | Sound, music and languages | Words can wound and spread lies | One of the Nine |
| [[Magurkai]] | God of Magic | Magic, through a bloodline | Overcasting can kill; the bloodline splits the world | One of the Nine |

"—" means the notes give no flaw for that god. Older titles (Krarkur as God of War, Dauviti as God of the Underworld, Ozefio as a god) are recorded in canon notes on each god's page.
