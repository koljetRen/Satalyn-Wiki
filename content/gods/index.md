title: Gods

The twelve deities known worldwide — [[Euiklatz]] the Supreme Creator, [[Lebarka]] the Father of All, [[Modingua]] the Mother of All, and [[The Nine]] — plus the lesser gods of small cults and regions.

## Pages

**The pantheon**

- [[The Pantheon]] — the twelve deities known worldwide (still unnamed)
- [[Euiklatz]] — the Supreme Creator, God of the Eternities
- [[Lebarka]] — the Father of All, God of Life, who asked for Satalyn
- [[Modingua]] — the Mother of All, Goddess of Motherhood and love, who chose the races

**[[The Nine]]** — the nine gods lifted from the lowest caste to shape Satalyn

- [[Dauviti]] — God of the Dead, who gave every creature the ability to die
- [[Stomell]] — God of the Skies and the weather, father of the three moons
- [[Ozefio]] — Goddess of the Seas, giver of shipbuilding and fishing
- [[Felderg]] — God of the Earth, who coloured the plants and gave farming
- [[Gehflaun]] — Goddess of Intelligence, giver of reading, writing and study
- [[Dreplagen]] — God of Strength, giver of strength, speed and mobility
- [[Krarkur]] — God of Deceit, who corrupted the gifts of intelligence and strength
- [[Klist]] — Goddess of Music, who gave the world sound and languages
- [[Magurkai]] — God of Magic, from whom all magic comes

**Lesser and regional gods**

- [[Lesser and Regional Gods]] — deities and spirits outside the twelve
- [[Brother Gods of Aegryamis]] — two brothers given the desert of Aegryamis as a punishment
- [[Ocean Goddess of Osisu]] — worshipped by the Osisu, represented by the queen shaman

See also [[religion/index|Religion]] for how the gods are worshipped, and [[Sacred Creatures]] for the beasts said to belong to them.
