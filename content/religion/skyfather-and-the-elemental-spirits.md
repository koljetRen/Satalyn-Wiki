The **Skyfather and the elemental spirits** are the objects of an ancient religion of [[Aramore]] and [[Skarg]]: spirits found in the elements, led by the Skyfather, the spirit dominating the sky.

## Beliefs

Spirits are found in the elements, and each possesses different powers to control the world. Their leader is the Skyfather.

## Practice

People used to hold traditions and festivals when the auroras were seen, which they thought was the Skyfather blessing them through dance. The auroras haven't been seen since the [[Desolation of the Ground Water|desolation of the ground water]] by the evil wizard.

In Skarg, each town's centre holds a large totem pole depicting one of the elemental spirits as interpreted by humans.

## Who still believes

- **Aramore:** an ancient religion that no one seems to follow any more.
- **Skarg:** the same religion, but more people still believe it, especially up north in [[Thrymfell]] or [[Northwatch]].
