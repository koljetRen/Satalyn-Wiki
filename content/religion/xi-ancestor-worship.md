**Xi ancestor worship** is the religion of the [[Xi]] people, who believe their ancestors watch over them.

## Beliefs

Men gain honour for their families through acts of battle (duels, war, and so on). As men achieve honour and women practise proper prayer etiquette and worship, families may stand united to bless their posterity. The more honour the men gained and the more faith the women showed, the more it is believed to bless their descendants and place them where they are now.

## Practice

- Each Xi family home has a shrine for its ancestors.
- Women are the more religiously engaged. They are taught to be constantly praying for the gods to bless their husbands with strength while the men are away.

> [!info] Canon note
> The 2026 culture sheet (sources/drive/organized-runaways-notes.md) has the women praying "for the God's" (gods) to bless their husbands. The older Runaways notes (sources/drive/runaways/runaways-ideas.md) say they are "constantly worshiping their God" (singular) to bless the men with strength.
