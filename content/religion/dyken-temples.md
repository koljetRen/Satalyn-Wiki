**Dyken temples** are the temples found in every city of [[Dyken]], which serve both as places of worship and as schools for children and adults alike.

## Priests and acolytes

The priests and acolytes who look after the temples serve as worship leaders and teachers, and practise philosophy in their time. The Dyken believe the highest calling is that of a scholar, and being granted the role of priest at the temple is the highest fulfilment one can achieve. Priests wear full-covering white togas, white being the colour of ultimate knowledge.

## Buildings

Temples are the largest buildings in Dyken cities, topped with domes lined in gold. Large amphitheatres host meetings of the judges and lectures from the priests.

## The goddess of intelligence

[[Gehflaun]], Goddess of Intelligence, is central to worship among the Dyken. [[Krarkur]]'s twisting of intelligence into castes and discrimination is felt most strongly among them, since their society relies on intelligence.
