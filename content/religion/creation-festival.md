The **Creation Festival** is a worldwide festival that reenacts the creation story of [[Satalyn]]. ("Creation Festival" is a working name.)

## The reenactment

The worship leader sings of the Father of All ([[Lebarka]]) passing each request to the Supreme Creator ([[Euiklatz]]), and the dancers act out each gift as it arrives (see [[Seven Phases of Creation]] and [[Worship Through Dance and Song]]).

## Date

No one knows the world's true age or the real anniversary of creation, so the date varies by culture. Everywhere holds it within roughly the same month.

## Regional versions

The festival is shared, but each region tells the story a little differently, putting its own favourite god at the centre.
