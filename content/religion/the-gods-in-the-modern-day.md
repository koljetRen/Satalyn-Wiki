**The gods in the modern day** (around year 1067) are more distant than ever: worship of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]] has faded, and answers to prayer are rare and small. How each god feels about fading worship depends on the god.

## Worship today

Worship has drifted far from its ancient form. Dance and song are rare, which makes the gods feel even more distant. The gods answer only now and then, and even an answered dance brings a small effect. See [[Why Worship Faded]].

## The gods

| God | How they are faring |
| --- | --- |
| [[Modingua]] | Wants to be more involved. The world is still medieval and most people marry and start families young, but a few races may be marrying later or having fewer children, which saddens her. Still open. |
| [[Magurkai]] | May have the most influence today, because his bloodline has spread so widely. He went from touching one tiny cult to being the most present god. |
