**Why worship faded** is the story of how the ancient [[Worship Through Dance and Song|dance-and-song worship]] of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]] declined until, by the modern day, it is rare.

## Causes

1. **Cities:** as people gathered into bigger cities, local traditions were slowly lost.
2. **An age of questioning:** a kind of revolutionary age came, when people began asking why they worshipped.
3. **Small results:** because the gods are distant and their power is limited, a dance might bring only a small rain, not enough for a season. People saw little result and stopped taking part.

## The cycle

Weak answers led to doubt, and doubt led the gods to withdraw further. In ancient times the gods answered far more often; in the modern day responses are rare and smaller. See [[The Gods in the Modern Day]].

## Regional faiths

Regional faiths have faded too. The [[Skyfather and the Elemental Spirits|Skyfather religion]] of [[Aramore]] is an ancient religion that no one seems to follow any more, though more people in [[Skarg]] still believe it.

## Open questions

- When was the age of questioning? The notes don't date it.

> [!warning]- Secrets (author only)
> As belief faded, the gods came to see themselves as less important and largely went back to their old lives in the wider cosmos. They are only called back now and then, which is why responses are rare and smaller.
