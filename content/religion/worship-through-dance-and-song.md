**Worship through dance and song** is the everyday form of worship of [[The Pantheon|the pantheon]] on [[Satalyn]]: in ancient times people honoured the gods with dance and song rather than asking them for favours.

## How it works

- A **worship leader** sets the rhythm and leads the singing.
- A small group of ordinary, untrained people dance, perhaps because the dance runs in their family or they feel called.
- Everyone else watches, claps or joins the song.
- Bards could be seen as holy figures.

## Regional dances

Each region has its own dances for its favourite god, such as flowing, wave-like movements for the water god ([[Ozefio]]). The whole world shares dances for the Supreme Creator ([[Euiklatz]]), the Father ([[Lebarka]]) and the Mother ([[Modingua]]). [[Klist]], Goddess of Music, is a natural fit for this kind of worship.

## When people dance

| Occasion | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Set occasions | Seasonal dances, such as a dance after planting to hope for rain |
| Celebrations | Dances to celebrate life |
| In times of need | A family in dire need may dance to ask for help |
| The [[Creation Festival]] | Dancers act out each gift of creation |

## Do the dances work?

Only sometimes. Even an answered dance brings a small effect, such as a light rain. In ancient times the gods answered far more often. In the modern day responses are rare and smaller. See [[Why Worship Faded]].

## In the modern day

Worship has drifted far from its ancient form. Dance and song are rare, which makes the gods feel even more distant.
